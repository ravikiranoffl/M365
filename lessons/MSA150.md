# Day 150 - Mid-course proficiency evaluation

## Lesson

"Mid-course proficiency evaluation" means we are taking a moment to pause, reflect, and most importantly, **you** get to show **me** everything you've mastered so far. This isn't about getting every single word perfect, but rather about demonstrating your overall command of advanced Arabic grammar, your extensive vocabulary across various domains, and your ability to construct complex, coherent, and persuasive arguments.

Just like a first-grader gets to show their teacher how well they can read by reading a story, you're going to show me how well you can "read" (and write!) in advanced Arabic by tackling some challenging tasks.

This evaluation will challenge you across several key areas:

1.  **Integrated Vocabulary:** How well you can recall and correctly use specialized vocabulary from all the fields we've covered: government, legal, economic, diplomacy, conflict, data description, interview/job language, and the recent intensive reporting domains of culture, science, and environment.
2.  **Advanced Grammatical Structures:** Your accuracy in applying complex grammar such as `أَنَّ` and `أَنْ` clauses, subjunctive verbs, light verb constructions, verb + preposition collocations, intricate Idafa constructions, and the correct handling of various plural forms (human/non-human, sound/broken, diptotes).
3.  **Connectors and Argumentation Structures:** Your skill in using the full range of advanced connectors and argumentation phrases to logically link ideas, present nuanced viewpoints, and build compelling arguments.
4.  **Morphological Understanding:** Your ability to use the correct forms of verbs, Masdars, active, and passive participles, reflecting a precise understanding of their meanings.
5.  **Overall Fluency and Cohesion:** Your capacity to produce fluid, accurate, and comprehensive Arabic texts that effectively communicate complex information.

For this evaluation, I will provide you with a series of tasks, and your goal is to provide your best Arabic response. **I will not be providing model answers for these tasks today.** This is your opportunity to work independently and showcase your current proficiency. When you are finished, you can submit your answers, and we will review them in detail.

Take your time, think through each task carefully, and remember all the tools you've gathered on our journey. This is your chance to truly shine!

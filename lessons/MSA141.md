# Day 141 - Fluency: Translation exercises

## Lesson

"Fluency: Translation exercises" is a continuous and crucial practice for consolidating your advanced Arabic skills. You've become proficient in constructing complex sentences, expressing nuanced meanings, and reporting on diverse topics. Now, the challenge is to seamlessly and precisely translate from English to Arabic, ensuring every grammatical rule, every case ending, and every word choice is meticulously correct and contextually appropriate.

Think of yourself as a highly skilled artisan, now tasked with crafting exquisite pieces by carefully selecting and combining all the specialized materials and techniques you've acquired. This exercise will rigorously test your ability to:

1.  **Recall and apply diverse vocabulary:** Select the most appropriate words from all the specialized categories you've studied, including government, economy, environment, culture, science, legal, interview language, and the newer reporting fields.
2.  **Master grammatical structures:** Accurately implement `أَنَّ` and `أَنْ` clauses, subjunctive verbs, light verb constructions, verb + preposition collocations, Idafa constructions, and correctly handle plural forms (human/non-human, sound/broken, diptote).
3.  **Utilize connectors and stance markers:** Strategically place advanced connectors (`عَلَاوَةً عَلَى ذَلِكَ`, `بِالرَّغْمِ مِنْ أَنَّ`, `فِي ضَوْءِ ذَلِكَ`, `لِذَلِكَ`, etc.) and stance markers (`مِنَ الْمُؤَكَّدِ أَنَّ`, `لَا شَكَّ أَنَّ`, `لَا يُنْكَرُ أَنَّ`, `لِسُوءِ الْحَظِّ`, `عَلَى سَبِيلِ الْمِثَالِ`, `وَبِالْعَكْسِ`, etc.) to ensure the translated text flows logically, maintains coherence, and conveys the intended tone and emphasis.
4.  **Demonstrate morphological understanding:** Use the correct forms of verbs, Masdars, active, and passive participles derived from roots.
5.  **Apply editing skills:** Pay meticulous attention to details like noun case endings (Nominative, Accusative, Genitive), adjective agreement (especially with non-human plurals and diptotes), and verb-subject agreement.

Each sentence is a comprehensive puzzle, requiring you to carefully consider how to best express the English meaning using your entire advanced Arabic toolkit. Remember to take your time, think through the grammar, and write your translations with full Harakat.

## Examples

Let's work through a few examples together. Read the English sentence, try to translate it in your mind (or aloud!), and then compare your version with my Arabic translation and the breakdown.

1.  **English Sentence:** "It cannot be denied that the scientific research on climate change is very important. And the evidence for that is that it affects human health directly."
    *   **Arabic Translation:** `لَا يُنْكَرُ أَنَّ الْأَبْحَاثَ الْعِلْمِيَّةَ بِشَأْنِ تَغَيُّرِ الْمُنَاخِ مُهِمَّةٌ جِدًّا. وَالدَّلِيلُ عَلَى ذَلِكَ أَنَّهَا تُؤَثِّرُ فِي صِحَّةِ الْإِنْسَانِ بِشَكْلٍ مُبَاشِرٍ.`
    *   **Breakdown:**
        *   `لَا يُنْكَرُ أَنَّ` (It cannot be denied that - Argumentation phrase, Day 132).
        *   `الْأَبْحَاثَ الْعِلْمِيَّةَ بِشَأْنِ تَغَيُّرِ الْمُنَاخِ` (the scientific research on climate change - `الْأَبْحَاثَ الْعِلْمِيَّةَ` (scientific research, Day 123) Accusative subject of `أَنَّ`; `بِشَأْنِ` (regarding, Day 129); `تَغَيُّرِ الْمُنَاخِ` (climate change, Day 124) Idafa).
        *   `مُهِمَّةٌ جِدًّا` (very important - `مُهِمَّةٌ` (important, Day 54) Nominative predicate for non-human plural `الْأَبْحَاثَ`; `جِدًّا` (very, Day 130)).
        *   `وَالدَّلِيلُ عَلَى ذَلِكَ أَنَّ` (And the evidence for that is that - Argumentation phrase, Day 132).
        *   `تُؤَثِّرُ فِي صِحَّةِ الْإِنْسَانِ بِشَكْلٍ مُبَاشِرٍ` (it affects human health directly - `تُؤَثِّرُ فِي` (she affects in, Day 122) feminine verb for `الْأَبْحَاثَ`; `صِحَّةِ الْإِنْسَانِ` (human health, Day 126/106) Idafa; `بِشَكْلٍ مُبَاشِرٍ` (in a direct way, new for this context) adverbial phrase).

2.  **English Sentence:** "Despite the fact that the government supports traditional arts, some believe, on the contrary, that modern technologies have a negative influence on them (f.)."
    *   **Arabic Translation:** `بِالرَّغْمِ مِنْ أَنَّ الْحُكُومَةَ تَدْعَمُ الْفُنُونَ التَّقْلِيدِيَّةَ، يَعْتَقِدُ الْبَعْضُ، وَبِالْعَكْسِ، أَنَّ التِّقْنِيَّاتِ الْحَدِيثَةَ لَهَا تَأْثِيرٌ سَلْبِيٌّ عَلَيْهَا.`
    *   **Breakdown:**
        *   `بِالرَّغْمِ مِنْ أَنَّ` (Despite the fact that - Advanced connector, Day 107).
        *   `الْحُكُومَةَ تَدْعَمُ الْفُنُونَ التَّقْلِيدِيَّةَ` (the government supports traditional arts - `الْحُكُومَةَ` (Day 101) Accusative subject of `أَنَّ`; `تَدْعَمُ` (supports, Day 132); `الْفُنُونَ التَّقْلِيدِيَّةَ` (traditional arts - `الْفُنُونَ` (Day 122), `التَّقْلِيدِيَّةَ` (traditional, Day 136) Accusative object for non-human plural)).
        *   `يَعْتَقِدُ الْبَعْضُ، وَبِالْعَكْسِ، أَنَّ` (some believe, on the contrary, that - `يَعْتَقِدُ` (he believes, Day 79); `الْبَعْضُ` (some, Day 130) Nominative subject; `وَبِالْعَكْسِ` (on the contrary, Day 139) argumentation phrase).
        *   `التِّقْنِيَّاتِ الْحَدِيثَةَ لَهَا تَأْثِيرٌ سَلْبِيٌّ عَلَيْهَا` (modern technologies have a negative influence on them - `التِّقْنِيَّاتِ الْحَدِيثَةَ` (modern technologies, Day 130) Accusative subject of `أَنَّ`; `لَهَا` (they have, Day 116); `تَأْثِيرٌ سَلْبِيٌّ` (negative influence - `تَأْثِيرٌ` (Day 122), `سَلْبِيٌّ` (negative, Day 131)) Nominative subject of `لَهَا`; `عَلَيْهَا` (on them, Day 24 for non-human plural).

3.  **English Sentence:** "The economic report indicates a big decrease in investments. And the evidence for that is that many companies, for example foreign ones, are not expanding. Therefore, this affects job opportunities."
    *   **Arabic Translation:** `يُشِيرُ التَّقْرِيرُ الِاقْتِصَادِيُّ إِلَى نَقْصٍ كَبِيرٍ فِي الِاسْتِثْمَارَاتِ. وَالدَّلِيلُ عَلَى ذَلِكَ أَنَّ شَرِكَاتٍ كَثِيرَةً، عَلَى سَبِيلِ الْمِثَالِ الْأَجْنَبِيَّةَ، لَا تَتَوَسَّعُ. لِذَلِكَ، يُؤَثِّرُ هَذَا فِي فُرَصِ الْعَمَلِ.`
    *   **Breakdown:**
        *   `يُشِيرُ التَّقْرِيرُ الِاقْتِصَادِيُّ إِلَى` (the economic report indicates - `يُشِيرُ إِلَى` (Day 123); `التَّقْرِيرُ الِاقْتِصَادِيُّ` (economic report, Day 115/103)).
        *   `نَقْصٍ كَبِيرٍ فِي الِاسْتِثْمَارَاتِ` (a big decrease in investments - `نَقْصٍ كَبِيرٍ` (big decrease, Day 115/54) Genitive after `إِلَى`; `الِاسْتِثْمَارَاتِ` (investments, Day 120) Genitive after `فِي`).
        *   `وَالدَّلِيلُ عَلَى ذَلِكَ أَنَّ` (And the evidence for that is that - Argumentation phrase, Day 132).
        *   `شَرِكَاتٍ كَثِيرَةً، عَلَى سَبِيلِ الْمِثَالِ الْأَجْنَبِيَّةَ، لَا تَتَوَسَّعُ` (many companies, for example foreign ones, are not expanding - `شَرِكَاتٍ كَثِيرَةً` (many companies, Day 110) Accusative subject of `أَنَّ`; `عَلَى سَبِيلِ الْمِثَالِ` (for example, Day 139) argumentation phrase; `الْأَجْنَبِيَّةَ` (foreign, Day 113) Accusative adjective for `شَرِكَاتٍ`; `لَا تَتَوَسَّعُ` (she does not expand, new for this context) negative present tense verb, feminine for non-human plural).
        *   `لِذَلِكَ، يُؤَثِّرُ هَذَا فِي فُرَصِ الْعَمَلِ` (Therefore, this affects job opportunities - `لِذَلِكَ` (Day 132) argumentation word; `يُؤَثِّرُ` (he affects, Day 122); `هَذَا` (this, Day 19) Nominative subject; `فِي فُرَصِ الْعَمَلِ` (in job opportunities - `فُرَصِ الْعَمَلِ` (job opportunities, Day 140) Idafa, Genitive after `فِي`).

## 📚 Core Vocabulary

*   حَالِيٌّ (ḥāliyyun) - current (masculine adjective)

# Day 159 - Advanced Journalism: Polling language

## Lesson

"Advanced Polling language" is about enhancing your ability to communicate public opinion data with greater sophistication. Beyond simply stating what a poll found, you'll learn how to discuss the *quality* of the poll, analyze opinions across *different groups*, and articulate how these findings *reflect* broader societal trends or *impact* decision-makers. This topic naturally builds on both "Polling language" (Day 152) and "Advanced Statistics language" (Day 158).

Think of it as adding specialized magnifying glasses to your advanced Arabic toolkit, allowing you to examine and report on public sentiment with critical depth and precision.

Here are the key vocabulary and phrases you'll learn today:

### 1. Discussing Poll Quality:

*   `هَامِشُ الْخَطَأِ` (hāmiš_u al-khaṭaʾi) - 'haa-mi-shu al-kha-ṭa-'i' (masculine noun phrase, Idafa)
    *   *Meaning:* Margin of error.
    *   *Explanation:* This essential term in polling refers to the range within which the true value of the population is likely to fall. `هَامِشٌ` means 'margin' and `خَطَأٌ` means 'error'.
*   `مِصْدَاقِيَّةٌ` (miṣdāqiyyatun) - 'miṣ-dā-qiy-ya-tun' (feminine noun)
    *   *Meaning:* Credibility, reliability.
    *   *Explanation:* You encountered this term on Day 156. Here, we're reinforcing its specific application to the trustworthiness of polls and surveys. We often say `مِصْدَاقِيَّةُ الِاسْتِطْلَاعَاتِ` (the credibility of polls).

### 2. Analyzing Society Segments:

*   `شَرَائِحُ الْمُجْتَمَعِ` (sharā'iḥu al-mujtama'i) - 'sha-raa-'i-ḥu al-muj-ta-ma-'i' (feminine plural noun phrase, Idafa)
    *   *Meaning:* Segments of society / Social strata.
    *   *Explanation:* `شَرِيحَةٌ` (sharīḥatun) means 'slice' or 'segment'. Its broken plural `شَرَائِحُ` (diptote) is used to refer to different groups or demographics within the population, often examined in polls.

### 3. Verbs for Reflecting and Influencing:

*   `يَعْكِسُ` (yaʿkisu) - 'ya`-ki-su' (present tense verb, Form I)
    *   *Meaning:* He reflects / mirrors.
    *   *Explanation:* This verb is excellent for describing what a poll's results reveal or indicate about public sentiment or broader trends. Its feminine form is `تَعْكِسُ` (taʿkisu).
*   `صُنْعُ الْقَرَارِ` (ṣunʿu al-qarār) - 'ṣun-ʿu al-qa-raar' (masculine noun phrase, Idafa)
    *   *Meaning:* Decision-making.
    *   *Explanation:* `صُنْعٌ` means 'making' or 'manufacturing' (Masdar of `صَنَعَ`), and `قَرَارٌ` (Day 101) means 'decision'. Together, this is the formal term for the process of making decisions, often influenced by public opinion.

### 4. Describing Trends in Opinion:

*   `مُتَذَبْذِبٌ` (mutadhaddhibun) - 'mu-ta-dhadh-dhi-bun' (active participle, masculine singular)
    *   *Meaning:* Fluctuating, volatile, wavering.
    *   *Explanation:* This adjective, derived from the same root as `تَقَلُّبَاتٌ` (fluctuations, Day 158), is used to describe public opinion or support that is unstable or changing frequently. Remember to make it feminine (`مُتَذَبْذِبَةٌ`) or plural if needed.

### 5. Reinforcing Support/Opposition Ratios:

*   `نِسْبَةُ التَّأْيِيدِ` (nisbat_u at-taʾyīd) - 'nis-ba-tu at-ta'-yeed' (feminine noun phrase, Idafa)
    *   *Meaning:* Approval rating / Percentage of support.
    *   *Explanation:* Combines `نِسْبَةٌ` (percentage, Day 151) with `تَأْيِيدٌ` (support, Day 152) to specifically refer to the proportion of people who support something.
*   `نِسْبَةُ الْمُعَارَضَةِ` (nisbat_u al-muʿāraḍah) - 'nis-ba-tu al-mu-'aa-ra-ḍah' (feminine noun phrase, Idafa)
    *   *Meaning:* Disapproval rating / Percentage of opposition.
    *   *Explanation:* The counterpart to `نِسْبَةُ التَّأْيِيدِ`, using `مُعَارَضَةٌ` (opposition, Day 152).

Remember to seamlessly integrate these new terms with your existing advanced grammar, statistics vocabulary (Day 151/158), core polling terms (Day 152), and especially with your comprehensive argumentation structures. This will enable you to report on public opinion with exceptional depth and analytical rigor.

## Examples

Let's look at some examples that seamlessly integrate this new "advanced polling language" vocabulary with other learned concepts. Carefully read each English sentence, then the Arabic translation, and pay close attention to the breakdown of how different elements are combined.

1.  **"It is important to point out that recent public opinion polls reflect fluctuating support for the new government, with a margin of error of +/-3%."**
    *   `مِنَ الْمُهِمِّ الْإِشَارَةُ إِلَى أَنَّ اسْتِطْلَاعَاتِ الرَّأْيِ الْأَخِيرَةَ تَعْكِسُ تَأْيِيدًا مُتَذَبْذِبًا لِلْحُكُومَةِ الْجَدِيدَةِ، بِمَعْدَلِ خَطَأٍ يَبْلُغُ حَوَالَيْ 3% +/-.`
    *   **Breakdown:**
        *   `مِنَ الْمُهِمِّ الْإِشَارَةُ إِلَى أَنَّ` (It is important to point out that - Day 156).
        *   `اسْتِطْلَاعَاتِ الرَّأْيِ الْأَخِيرَةَ` (recent public opinion polls - Day 152/152/152) Accusative subject of `أَنَّ`.
        *   `تَعْكِسُ تَأْيِيدًا مُتَذَبْذِبًا لِلْحُكُومَةِ الْجَدِيدَةِ` (reflect fluctuating support for the new government - `تَعْكِسُ` (**new for today**); `تَأْيِيدًا` (support, Day 152) Accusative object; `مُتَذَبْذِبًا` (**new for today**) Accusative adjective; `لِلْحُكُومَةِ الْجَدِيدَةِ` (for the new government, Day 101/54) Genitive).
        *   `بِمَعْدَلِ خَطَأٍ يَبْلُغُ حَوَالَيْ 3% +/-` (with a margin of error of approximately +/-3% - `بِـ` (with); `هَامِشِ الْخَطَأِ` (**new for today**) Genitive Idafa; `يَبْلُغُ` (reaches, Day 151); `حَوَالَيْ 3%` (approximately 3%, Day 151)). *Self-correction: Used `بِمَعْدَلِ خَطَأٍ` instead of `بِهَامِشِ الْخَطَأِ` for variety and common usage.*

2.  **"According to experts, the credibility of the survey is high, which confirms that the approval rating for economic reforms reached more than 60% among most segments of society."**
    *   `وَفْقًا لِلْخُبَرَاءِ، مِصْدَاقِيَّةُ الِاسْتِطْلَاعِ عَالِيَةٌ، مِمَّا يُؤَكِّدُ أَنَّ نِسْبَةَ التَّأْيِيدِ لِلْإِصْلَاحَاتِ الِاقْتِصَادِيَّةِ بَلَغَتْ أَكْثَرَ مِنْ 60% بَيْنَ أَغْلَبِ شَرَائِحِ الْمُجْتَمَعِ.`
    *   **Breakdown:**
        *   `وَفْقًا لِلْخُبَرَاءِ` (According to experts - Day 158/123).
        *   `مِصْدَاقِيَّةُ الِاسْتِطْلَاعِ عَالِيَةٌ` (the credibility of the survey is high - `مِصْدَاقِيَّةُ` (**new for today**); `الِاسْتِطْلَاعِ` (the survey, Day 152) Nominative subject Idafa; `عَالِيَةٌ` (high, feminine adjective, Day 151 context `رَقْمٌ عَالٍ`)).
        *   `مِمَّا يُؤَكِّدُ أَنَّ` (which confirms that - `مِمَّا` Day 155; `يُؤَكِّدُ` Day 156).
        *   `نِسْبَةَ التَّأْيِيدِ لِلْإِصْلَاحَاتِ الِاقْتِصَادِيَّةِ بَلَغَتْ أَكْثَرَ مِنْ 60%` (the approval rating for economic reforms reached more than 60% - `نِسْبَةَ التَّأْيِيدِ` (**new for today**) Accusative subject of `أَنَّ`; `لِلْإِصْلَاحَاتِ الِاقْتِصَادِيَّةِ` (for economic reforms, Day 148); `بَلَغَتْ` (she reached, Day 151); `أَكْثَرَ مِنْ 60%` (more than 60%, Day 110/151)).
        *   `بَيْنَ أَغْلَبِ شَرَائِحِ الْمُجْتَمَعِ` (among most segments of society - `بَيْنَ` (between/among); `أَغْلَبِ` (most of, from `أَغْلَبِيَّةٌ` Day 152); `شَرَائِحِ الْمُجْتَمَعِ` (**new for today**) Genitive Idafa).

3.  **"While some believe that public opinion has a big influence on decision-making, others point out that the opposition rating remains stable among young people, compared to other segments of society."**
    *   `بَيْنَمَا يَرَى الْبَعْضُ أَنَّ الرَّأْيَ الْعَامَّ لَهُ تَأْثِيرٌ كَبِيرٌ فِي صُنْعِ الْقَرَارِ، يُشِيرُ آخَرُونَ إِلَى أَنَّ نِسْبَةَ الْمُعَارَضَةِ تَبْقَى مُسْتَقِرَّةً بَيْنَ الشَّبَابِ، مُقَارَنَةً بِشَرَائِحَ أُخْرَى مِنَ الْمُجْتَمَعِ.`
    *   **Breakdown:**
        *   `بَيْنَمَا يَرَى الْبَعْضُ أَنَّ` (While some believe that - Day 146).
        *   `الرَّأْيَ الْعَامَّ لَهُ تَأْثِيرٌ كَبِيرٌ فِي صُنْعِ الْقَرَارِ` (public opinion has a big influence on decision-making - `الرَّأْيَ الْعَامَّ` (public opinion, Day 152) Accusative subject of `أَنَّ`; `لَهُ` (it has); `تَأْثِيرٌ كَبِيرٌ` (big influence, Day 122/54); `فِي صُنْعِ الْقَرَارِ` (**new for today**) Genitive).
        *   `يُشِيرُ آخَرُونَ إِلَى أَنَّ` (others point out that - Day 146).
        *   `نِسْبَةَ الْمُعَارَضَةِ تَبْقَى مُسْتَقِرَّةً بَيْنَ الشَّبَابِ` (the opposition rating remains stable among young people - `نِسْبَةَ الْمُعَارَضَةِ` (**new for today**) Accusative subject of `أَنَّ`; `تَبْقَى` (she remains, Day 158 context); `مُسْتَقِرَّةً` (stable, feminine, Day 158) Accusative predicate; `بَيْنَ الشَّبَابِ` (among youth, Day 134) Genitive).
        *   `مُقَارَنَةً بِشَرَائِحَ أُخْرَى مِنَ الْمُجْتَمَعِ` (compared to other segments of society - `مُقَارَنَةً بِـ` (compared to, Day 158); `شَرَائِحَ أُخْرَى` (other segments - `شَرَائِحُ` **new for today**, `أُخْرَى` Day 110 feminine plural, diptote Accusative with Fatha because of `بِـ` and being non-human plural adjective for diptote noun); `مِنَ الْمُجْتَمَعِ` (of society, Day 106) Genitive).

# Day 261 - Irregular verb mastery

## Lesson

"Irregular verb mastery" at this truly advanced stage means applying your comprehensive understanding of weak letters (حُرُوفُ الْعِلَّةِ) and irregular verb categories to even more intricate verb forms. We'll now examine how Form VI (تَفَاعَلَ) and Form VIII (اِفْتَعَلَ) verbs behave when their roots contain weak letters, with detailed conjugation examples for `هُوَ` (he), `هِيَ` (she/it), and `أَنَا` (I).

Recall the **weak letters** (حُرُوفُ الْعِلَّةِ - 'ḥurūfu al-ʿillah'): `أَلِفٌ` (`ا`/`ى`), `وَاوٌ` (`و`), and `يَاءٌ` (`ي`). A **sound verb** (`فِعْلٌ صَحِيحٌ`) has no weak letters, while an **irregular verb** (`فِعْلٌ مُعْتَلٌّ`) has one or more.

### 1. Form VI Irregular Verbs (الفِعْلُ الْمُعْتَلُّ فِي الْبِنَاءِ السَّادِسِ)
Form VI (`تَفَاعَلَ` - tafaʿala) often indicates reciprocity (doing something to/with each other) or pretense. It is distinguished by the initial `تَـ` and a long `ا` after the first root letter.

*   **a. Assimilated Form VI (مِثَالٌ فِي الْبِنَاءِ السَّادِسِ)**
    *   **Definition:** First root letter is weak (`و` or `ي`). The initial `و` usually **remains stable** throughout conjugations.
    *   **Example Verb:** `تَوَاعَدَ` (tawāʿada) - 'ta-wā-ʿa-da' (He/they promised each other).
        *   **Root:** و ع د (w-ʿ-d) (Form I: `وَعَدَ` - he promised)
        *   **Past Tense (الْمَاضِي):**
            *   `هُوَ تَوَاعَدَ` (huwa tawāʿada) - He/they promised each other. (Weak letter `و` is present).
            *   `هِيَ تَوَاعَدَتْ` (hiya tawāʿadat) - She/they promised each other. (Weak letter `و` is present).
            *   `أَنَا تَوَاعَدْتُ` (anā tawāʿadtu) - I promised each other. (Weak letter `و` is present).
        *   **Present Tense (الْمُضَارِعُ):**
            *   `هُوَ يَتَوَاعَدُ` (huwa yatawāʿadu) - He/they promise each other. (The `و` remains).
            *   `هِيَ تَتَوَاعَدُ` (hiya tatawāʿadu) - She/they promise each other. (The `و` remains).
            *   `أَنَا أَتَوَاعَدُ` (anā atawāʿadu) - I promise each other. (The `و` remains).
    *   **Key Behavior:** The initial `و` remains stable throughout conjugations in Form VI.

*   **b. Hollow Form VI (أَجْوَفُ فِي الْبِنَاءِ السَّادِسِ)**
    *   **Definition:** Middle root letter is weak (`و` or `ي`). The weak letter typically remains stable in both past and present tense due to the long `ا` of Form VI.
    *   **Example Verb:** `تَقَاوَلَ` (taqāwala) - 'ta-qā-wa-la' (He/they negotiated/discussed mutually).
        *   **Original Root:** ق و ل (q-w-l) (Form I: `قَالَ` - he said; Form III: `قَاوَلَ` - he negotiated)
        *   **Past Tense (الْمَاضِي):**
            *   `هُوَ تَقَاوَلَ` (huwa taqāwala) - He negotiated mutually. (The `و` is present after the `ا`).
            *   `هِيَ تَقَاوَلَتْ` (hiya taqāwalat) - She negotiated mutually. (The `و` is present).
            *   `أَنَا تَقَاوَلْتُ` (anā taqāwaltu) - I negotiated mutually. (The `و` is present).
        *   **Present Tense (الْمُضَارِعُ):**
            *   `هُوَ يَتَقَاوَلُ` (huwa yataqāwalu) - He negotiates mutually. (The `و` remains).
            *   `هِيَ تَتَقَاوَلُ` (hiya tataqāwalu) - She negotiates mutually. (The `و` remains).
            *   `أَنَا أَتَقَاوَلُ` (anā ataqāwalu) - I negotiate mutually. (The `و` remains).
    *   **Key Behavior:** The middle weak letter (`و` or `ي`) typically remains stable and appears as a consonant in Form VI after the `ا`.

*   **c. Deficient Form VI (نَاقِصٌ فِي الْبِنَاءِ السَّادِسِ)**
    *   **Definition:** Third root letter is weak (`و` or `ي`). The weak letter converts to `ى` (alif maqṣūrah) in past and present singular.
    *   **Example Verb:** `تَدَاعَى` (tadāʿā) - 'ta-dā-ʿā' (He/they called each other/collapsed).
        *   **Original Root:** د ع و (d-ʿ-w) (Form I: `دَعَا` - he called)
        *   **Past Tense (الْمَاضِي):**
            *   `هُوَ تَدَاعَى` (huwa tadāʿā) - He collapsed. (The `و` becomes `ى`).
            *   `هِيَ تَدَاعَتْ` (hiya tadāʿat) - She collapsed. (The `ى` (from `و`) drops for feminine suffix).
            *   `أَنَا تَدَاعَيْتُ` (anā tadāʿaytu) - I collapsed. (The `و` reappears as `ي`).
        *   **Present Tense (الْمُضَارِعُ):**
            *   `هُوَ يَتَدَاعَى` (huwa yatadāʿā) - He collapses. (The `و` becomes `ى`).
            *   `هِيَ تَتَدَاعَى` (hiya tatadāʿā) - She collapses. (The `و` becomes `ى`).
            *   `أَنَا أَتَدَاعَى` (anā atadāʿā) - I collapse. (The `و` becomes `ى`).
    *   **Key Behavior:** The final weak letter (`و` or `ي`) typically converts to `ى` (alif maqṣūrah) in both `هُوَ`/`هِيَ` past and present singular. It drops for `هِيَ` past with `تْ` suffix, and reappears as `ي` for `أَنَا` past.

*   **d. Doubly Weak Form VI (لَفِيفٌ فِي الْبِنَاءِ السَّادِسِ)**
    *   **Separated (لَفِيفٌ مَفْرُوقٌ):** First and third root letters are weak.
        *   **Example Verb:** `تَوَاقَى` (tawāqā) - 'ta-wā-qā' (He/they protected each other).
        *   **Original Root:** و ق ي (w-q-y) (Form I: `وَقَى` - he protected)
        *   **Past Tense (الْمَاضِي):**
            *   `هُوَ تَوَاقَى` (huwa tawāqā) - He protected each other. (`و` is present, `ي` becomes `ى`).
            *   `هِيَ تَوَاقَتْ` (hiya tawāqat) - She protected each other. (`و` is present, `ى` (from `ي`) drops).
            *   `أَنَا تَوَاقَيْتُ` (anā tawāqaytu) - I protected each other. (`و` is present, `ي` reappears as `ي`).
        *   **Present Tense (الْمُضَارِعُ):**
            *   `هُوَ يَتَوَاقَى` (huwa yatawāqā) - He protects each other. (`و` is present, `ي` becomes `ى`).
            *   `هِيَ تَتَوَاقَى` (hiya tatawāqā) - She protects each other. (`و` is present, `ي` becomes `ى`).
            *   `أَنَا أَتَوَاقَى` (anā atawāqā) - I protect each other. (`و` is present, `ي` becomes `ى`).
        *   **Key Behavior:** The initial `و` remains stable, and the final `ي` behaves like a Form VI Nāqiṣ.

    *   **Conjoined (لَفِيفٌ مَقْرُونٌ):** Second and third root letters are weak.
        *   **Example Verb:** `تَحَاوَى` (taḥāwā) - 'ta-ḥā-wā' (They gathered around).
        *   **Original Root:** ح و ي (ḥ-w-y) (Form I: `حَوَى` - he contained)
        *   **Past Tense (الْمَاضِي):**
            *   `هُوَ تَحَاوَى` (huwa taḥāwā) - He gathered around. (`و` is present, `ي` becomes `ى`).
            *   `هِيَ تَحَاوَتْ` (hiya taḥāwat) - She gathered around. (`و` is present, `ى` (from `ي`) drops).
            *   `أَنَا تَحَاوَيْتُ` (anā taḥāwaytu) - I gathered around. (`و` is present, `ي` reappears as `ي`).
        *   **Present Tense (الْمُضَارِعُ):**
            *   `هُوَ يَتَحَاوَى` (huwa yataḥāwā) - He gathers around. (`و` is present, `ي` becomes `ى`).
            *   `هِيَ تَتَحَاوَى` (hiya tataḥāwā) - She gathers around. (`و` is present, `ي` becomes `ى`).
            *   `أَنَا أَتَحَاوَى` (anā ataḥāwā) - I gather around. (`و` is present, `ي` becomes `ى`).
        *   **Key Behavior:** The second weak letter (`و`) remains stable, and the final `ي` behaves like a Form VI Nāqiṣ.

### 2. Form VIII Irregular Verbs (الفِعْلُ الْمُعْتَلُّ فِي الْبِنَاءِ الثَّامِنِ)
Form VIII (`اِفْتَعَلَ` - iftaʿala) often expresses a reflexive action or refers to the accomplishment of an action. It is characterized by the `ت` inserted after the first root letter. Special rules apply when the first root letter is weak, or a sibilant/dental.

*   **a. Assimilated Form VIII (مِثَالٌ فِي الْبِنَاءِ الثَّامِنِ)**
    *   **Definition:** First root letter is weak (`و` or `ي`). If the first root letter is `و`, it assimilates with the `ت` of Form VIII to become a doubled `تّ`.
    *   **Example Verb:** `اِتَّصَلَ` (ittaṣala) - 'it-ta-ṣa-la' (He connected/contacted).
        *   **Original Root:** و ص ل (w-ṣ-l) (Form I: `وَصَلَ` - he arrived)
        *   **Past Tense (الْمَاضِي):**
            *   `هُوَ اِتَّصَلَ` (huwa ittaṣala) - He connected. (The `و` becomes `تّ`).
            *   `هِيَ اِتَّصَلَتْ` (hiya ittaṣalat) - She connected. (The `و` becomes `تّ`).
            *   `أَنَا اِتَّصَلْتُ` (anā ittaṣaltu) - I connected. (The `و` becomes `تّ`).
        *   **Present Tense (الْمُضَارِعُ):**
            *   `هُوَ يَتَّصِلُ` (huwa yattaṣilu) - He connects. (The `و` becomes `تّ`).
            *   `هِيَ تَتَّصِلُ` (hiya tattaṣilu) - She connects. (The `و` becomes `تّ`).
            *   `أَنَا أَتَّصِلُ` (anā attaṣilu) - I connect. (The `و` becomes `تّ`).
    *   **Key Behavior:** The initial `و` of the root merges and geminates with the `ت` of the Form VIII pattern, resulting in `تَّ`.

*   **b. Hollow Form VIII (أَجْوَفُ فِي الْبِنَاءِ الثَّامِنِ)**
    *   **Definition:** Middle root letter is weak (`و` or `ي`). The weak letter converts to `ا` in the past tense, and often `ي` or `و` in the present tense, interacting with the `ت` of Form VIII.
    *   **Example Verb:** `اِعْتَادَ` (iʿtāda) - 'iʿ-tā-da' (He got used to/accustomed).
        *   **Original Root:** ع و د (ʿ-w-d) (Form I: `عَادَ` - he returned/used to)
        *   **Past Tense (الْمَاضِي):**
            *   `هُوَ اِعْتَادَ` (huwa iʿtāda) - He got used to. (The `و` becomes `ا`).
            *   `هِيَ اِعْتَادَتْ` (hiya iʿtādat) - She got used to. (The `و` becomes `ا`).
            *   `أَنَا اِعْتَدْتُ` (anā iʿtattu) - I got used to. (The `ا` (from `و`) drops, and the preceding vowel adjusts).
        *   **Present Tense (الْمُضَارِعُ):**
            *   `هُوَ يَعْتَادُ` (huwa yaʿtādu) - He gets used to. (The `و` becomes `ا`).
            *   `هِيَ تَعْتَادُ` (hiya taʿtādu) - She gets used to. (The `و` becomes `ا`).
            *   `أَنَا أَعْتَادُ` (anā aʿtādu) - I get used to. (The `و` becomes `ا`).
    *   **Key Behavior:** The middle weak letter (`و` or `ي`) typically converts to `ا` in the past and present for `هُوَ`/`هِيَ`. When suffixes are added (like for `أَنَا` past), this `ا` often drops, and the short vowel before it takes its place.

*   **c. Deficient Form VIII (نَاقِصٌ فِي الْبِنَاءِ الثَّامِنِ)**
    *   **Definition:** Third root letter is weak (`و` or `ي`). Behaves similarly to other Nāqiṣ verbs across forms.
    *   **Example Verb:** `اِرْتَمَى` (irtamā) - 'ir-ta-mā' (He threw himself/fell).
        *   **Original Root:** ر م ي (r-m-y) (Form I: `رَمَى` - he threw)
        *   **Past Tense (الْمَاضِي):**
            *   `هُوَ اِرْتَمَى` (huwa irtamā) - He threw himself. (The `ي` becomes `ى` - alif maqṣūrah).
            *   `هِيَ اِرْتَمَتْ` (hiya irtamat) - She threw herself. (The `ى` (from `ي`) drops entirely for feminine suffix).
            *   `أَنَا اِرْتَمَيْتُ` (anā irtamaytu) - I threw myself. (The `ي` reappears as `ي`).
        *   **Present Tense (الْمُضَارِعُ):**
            *   `هُوَ يَرْتَمِي` (huwa yartamī) - He throws himself. (The `ي` appears as `ي`).
            *   `هِيَ تَرْتَمِي` (hiya tartamī) - She throws herself. (The `ي` appears as `ي`).
            *   `أَنَا أَرْتَمِي` (anā artamī) - I throw myself. (The `ي` appears as `ي`).
    *   **Key Behavior:** The final weak letter undergoes similar transformations to Form I, II, IV, and V Nāqiṣ verbs (converting to `ى` in the past `هُوَ`, dropping for `هِيَ`, and typically becoming `ي` in the present tense).

*   **d. Doubly Weak Form VIII (لَفِيفٌ فِي الْبِنَاءِ الثَّامِنِ)**
    *   **Separated (لَفِيفٌ مَفْرُوقٌ):** First and third root letters are weak. The initial `و` assimilates with the `ت` of Form VIII, and the final weak letter behaves like a Nāqiṣ.
        *   **Example Verb:** `اِتَّقَى` (ittaqā) - 'it-ta-qā' (He feared God/protected himself).
        *   **Original Root:** و ق ي (w-q-y) (Form I: `وَقَى` - he protected)
        *   **Past Tense (الْمَاضِي):**
            *   `هُوَ اِتَّقَى` (huwa ittaqā) - He protected himself. (`و` becomes `تّ`, `ي` becomes `ى`).
            *   `هِيَ اِتَّقَتْ` (hiya ittaqat) - She protected herself. (`و` becomes `تّ`, `ى` (from `ي`) drops).
            *   `أَنَا اِتَّقَيْتُ` (anā ittaqaytu) - I protected myself. (`و` becomes `تّ`, `ي` reappears as `ي`).
        *   **Present Tense (الْمُضَارِعُ):**
            *   `هُوَ يَتَّقِي` (huwa yattaqī) - He protects himself. (`و` becomes `تّ`, `ي` appears as `ي`).
            *   `هِيَ تَتَّقِي` (hiya tattaqī) - She protects herself. (`و` becomes `تّ`, `ي` appears as `ي`).
            *   `أَنَا أَتَّقِي` (anā attaqī) - I protect myself. (`و` becomes `تّ`, `ي` appears as `ي`).
        *   **Key Behavior:** This combines the assimilation of the initial `و` with `ت` (resulting in `تَّ`) and the typical Nāqiṣ behavior of the final weak letter.

Mastering these specific patterns for Form VI and Form VIII verbs will further significantly expand your active vocabulary and comprehension of complex Arabic texts. Remember, the general rules for weak letters often carry across forms, but each form has its own specific pattern.

## Examples

    *   **Irregular Verb Focus:**
        *   `اِتَّصَلَ` (ittaṣala - he connected, past tense Form VIII): From `اِتَّصَلَ` (Assimilated, و ص ل). The `و` becomes `تّ`.

    *   **Cohesion Breakdown:** This sentence builds on policy argumentation and irregular verbs. `مِمَّا لَا شَكَّ فِيهِ، أَنَّ` (Day 196) sets a strong opening.

# Day 251 - Irregular verb mastery

## Lesson

"Irregular verb mastery" at this advanced stage means applying your understanding of weak letters (حُرُوفُ الْعِلَّةِ) and irregular verb categories to more complex verb forms. We'll now examine how Form II (فَعَّلَ) and Form III (فَاعَلَ) verbs behave when their roots contain weak letters, with detailed conjugation examples for `هُوَ` (he), `هِيَ` (she/it), and `أَنَا` (I).

Recall the **weak letters** (حُرُوفُ الْعِلَّةِ - 'ḥurūfu al-ʿillah'): `أَلِفٌ` (`ا`/`ى`), `وَاوٌ` (`و`), and `يَاءٌ` (`ي`). A **sound verb** (`فِعْلٌ صَحِيحٌ`) has no weak letters, while an **irregular verb** (`فِعْلٌ مُعْتَلٌّ`) has one or more.

### 1. Form II Irregular Verbs (الفِعْلُ الْمُعْتَلُّ فِي الْبِنَاءِ الثَّانِي)
Form II (`فَعَّلَ` - faʿʿala) intensifies or causes the action of Form I. The key characteristic of Form II is the gemination (doubling) of the middle root letter.

*   **a. Assimilated Form II (مِثَالٌ فِي الْبِنَاءِ الثَّانِي)**
    *   **Definition:** First root letter is weak (`و` or `ي`). Unlike Form I, the initial `و` **does not drop** in the present tense of Form II.
    *   **Example Verb:** `وَصَّلَ` (waṣṣala) - 'waṣ-ṣa-la' (He delivered/connected).
        *   **Root:** و ص ل (w-ṣ-l)
        *   **Past Tense (الْمَاضِي):**
            *   `هُوَ وَصَّلَ` (huwa waṣṣala) - He delivered.
            *   `هِيَ وَصَّلَتْ` (hiya waṣṣalat) - She delivered.
            *   `أَنَا وَصَّلْتُ` (anā waṣṣaltu) - I delivered.
        *   **Present Tense (الْمُضَارِعُ):**
            *   `هُوَ يُوَصِّلُ` (huwa yuwaṣṣilu) - He delivers. (The `و` remains).
            *   `هِيَ تُوَصِّلُ` (hiya tuwaṣṣilu) - She delivers. (The `و` remains).
            *   `أَنَا أُوَصِّلُ` (anā uwaṣṣilu) - I deliver. (The `و` remains).
    *   **Key Behavior:** The initial `و` remains stable throughout conjugations in Form II.

*   **b. Hollow Form II (أَجْوَفُ فِي الْبِنَاءِ الثَّانِي)**
    *   **Definition:** Middle root letter is weak (`و` or `ي`). In Form II, this weak letter is geminated.
    *   **Example Verb:** `قَوَّمَ` (qawwama) - 'qaw-wa-ma' (He evaluated/straightened).
        *   **Original Root:** ق و م (q-w-m) (Form I is `قَامَ` - he stood)
        *   **Past Tense (الْمَاضِي):**
            *   `هُوَ قَوَّمَ` (huwa qawwama) - He evaluated. (The `و` is geminated).
            *   `هِيَ قَوَّمَتْ` (hiya qawwamat) - She evaluated. (The `و` is geminated).
            *   `أَنَا قَوَّمْتُ` (anā qawwamtu) - I evaluated. (The `و` is geminated).
        *   **Present Tense (الْمُضَارِعُ):**
            *   `هُوَ يُقَوِّمُ` (huwa yuqawwimu) - He evaluates. (The `و` is geminated).
            *   `هِيَ تُقَوِّمُ` (hiya tuqawwimu) - She evaluates. (The `و` is geminated).
            *   `أَنَا أُقَوِّمُ` (anā uqawwimu) - I evaluate. (The `و` is geminated).
    *   **Key Behavior:** The original weak letter `و` or `ي` typically becomes stable and geminated in Form II.

*   **c. Deficient Form II (نَاقِصٌ فِي الْبِنَاءِ الثَّانِي)**
    *   **Definition:** Third root letter is weak (`و` or `ي`).
    *   **Example Verb:** `رَبَّى` (rabbā) - 'rab-bā' (He raised/educated).
        *   **Original Root:** ر ب و (r-b-w) (similar to `سَمَا` Form I from Day 246)
        *   **Past Tense (الْمَاضِي):**
            *   `هُوَ رَبَّى` (huwa rabbā) - He raised. (The `و` becomes `ى` - alif maqṣūrah).
            *   `هِيَ رَبَّتْ` (hiya rabbat) - She raised. (The `ى` (from `و`) drops for feminine suffix).
            *   `أَنَا رَبَّيْتُ` (anā rabbaytu) - I raised. (The `و` reappears as `ي`).
        *   **Present Tense (الْمُضَارِعُ):**
            *   `هُوَ يُرَبِّي` (huwa yurabbī) - He raises. (The `و` becomes `ي`).
            *   `هِيَ تُرَبِّي` (hiya turabbī) - She raises. (The `و` becomes `ي`).
            *   `أَنَا أُرَبِّي` (anā urabbī) - I raise. (The `و` becomes `ي`).
    *   **Key Behavior:** The final weak letter undergoes similar transformations to Form I Nāqiṣ verbs (converting to `ى` in the past `هُوَ`, dropping for `هِيَ`, and often becoming `ي` in other conjugations).

*   **d. Doubly Weak Form II (لَفِيفٌ فِي الْبِنَاءِ الثَّانِي)**
    *   **Separated (لَفِيفٌ مَفْرُوقٌ):** First and third root letters are weak.
        *   **Example Verb:** `وَفَّى` (waffā) - 'waf-fā' (He fulfilled/paid in full).
        *   **Original Root:** و ف ي (w-f-y) (Form I is `وَفَى` from Day 246)
        *   **Past Tense (الْمَاضِي):**
            *   `هُوَ وَفَّى` (huwa waffā) - He fulfilled.
            *   `هِيَ وَفَّتْ` (hiya waffat) - She fulfilled.
            *   `أَنَا وَفَّيْتُ` (anā waffaytu) - I fulfilled.
        *   **Present Tense (الْمُضَارِعُ):**
            *   `هُوَ يُوَفِّي` (huwa yuwaffī) - He fulfills.
            *   `هِيَ تُوَفِّي` (hiya tuwaffī) - She fulfills.
            *   `أَنَا أُوَفِّي` (anā uwaffī) - I fulfill.
        *   **Key Behavior:** The initial `و` remains stable (like Form II Mithāl), and the final `ي` behaves like a Form II Nāqiṣ.

### 2. Form III Irregular Verbs (الفِعْلُ الْمُعْتَلُّ فِي الْبِنَاءِ الثَّالِثِ)
Form III (`فَاعَلَ` - fāʿala) implies a mutual or attempted action. The key characteristic is the long `ا` after the first root letter.

*   **a. Assimilated Form III (مِثَالٌ فِي الْبِنَاءِ الثَّالِثِ)**
    *   **Definition:** First root letter is weak (`و` or `ي`). The initial weak letter `و` usually **remains**.
    *   **Example Verb:** `وَاصَلَ` (wāṣala) - 'wā-ṣa-la' (He continued).
        *   **Root:** و ص ل (w-ṣ-l)
        *   **Past Tense (الْمَاضِي):**
            *   `هُوَ وَاصَلَ` (huwa wāṣala) - He continued. (The `و` is present).
            *   `هِيَ وَاصَلَتْ` (hiya wāṣalat) - She continued. (The `و` is present).
            *   `أَنَا وَاصَلْتُ` (anā wāṣaltu) - I continued. (The `و` is present).
        *   **Present Tense (الْمُضَارِعُ):**
            *   `هُوَ يُوَاصِلُ` (huwa yuwāṣilu) - He continues. (The `و` remains).
            *   `هِيَ تُوَاصِلُ` (hiya tuwāṣilu) - She continues. (The `و` remains).
            *   `أَنَا أُوَاصِلُ` (anā uwāṣilu) - I continue. (The `و` remains).
    *   **Key Behavior:** The initial `و` remains stable throughout conjugations in Form III.

*   **b. Hollow Form III (أَجْوَفُ فِي الْبِنَاءِ الثَّالِثِ)**
    *   **Definition:** Middle root letter is weak (`و` or `ي`). In Form III, the `ا` is characteristic of the form itself, and the original weak letter usually becomes stable in the present.
    *   **Example Verb:** `قَاوَمَ` (qāwama) - 'qā-wa-ma' (He resisted).
        *   **Original Root:** ق و م (q-w-m) (Form I is `قَامَ` - he stood)
        *   **Past Tense (الْمَاضِي):**
            *   `هُوَ قَاوَمَ` (huwa qāwama) - He resisted. (The `و` is present after the `ا` of Form III).
            *   `هِيَ قَاوَمَتْ` (hiya qāwamat) - She resisted. (The `و` is present).
            *   `أَنَا قَاوَمْتُ` (anā qāwamtu) - I resisted. (The `و` is present).
        *   **Present Tense (الْمُضَارِعُ):**
            *   `هُوَ يُقَاوِمُ` (huwa yuqāwimu) - He resists. (The `و` remains).
            *   `هِيَ تُقَاوِمُ` (hiya tuqāwimu) - She resists. (The `و` remains).
            *   `أَنَا أُقَاوِمُ` (anā uqāwimu) - I resist. (The `و` remains).
    *   **Key Behavior:** The weak letter from the root (و/ي) generally remains stable in Form III after the `ا`.

*   **c. Deficient Form III (نَاقِصٌ فِي الْبِنَاءِ الثَّالِثِ)**
    *   **Definition:** Third root letter is weak (`و` or `ي`).
    *   **Example Verb:** `رَاعَى` (rāʿā) - 'rā-ʿā' (He observed/respected).
        *   **Original Root:** ر ع ي (r-ʿ-y) (similar to `جَرَى` Form I from Day 246)
        *   **Past Tense (الْمَاضِي):**
            *   `هُوَ رَاعَى` (huwa rāʿā) - He observed. (The `ي` becomes `ى` - alif maqṣūrah).
            *   `هِيَ رَاعَتْ` (hiya rāʿat) - She observed. (The `ى` (from `ي`) drops for feminine suffix).
            *   `أَنَا رَاعَيْتُ` (anā rāʿaytu) - I observed. (The `ي` reappears as `ي`).
        *   **Present Tense (الْمُضَارِعُ):**
            *   `هُوَ يُرَاعِي` (huwa yurāʿī) - He observes. (The `ي` appears as `ي`).
            *   `هِيَ تُرَاعِي` (hiya turāʿī) - She observes. (The `ي` appears as `ي`).
            *   `أَنَا أُرَاعِي` (anā urāʿī) - I observe. (The `ي` appears as `ي`).
    *   **Key Behavior:** The final weak letter behaves similarly to Form I Nāqiṣ verbs, converting to `ى` in the past `هُوَ`, dropping for `هِيَ`, and typically remaining `ي` in the present tense.

Mastering these specific patterns for Form II and Form III verbs will significantly expand your active vocabulary and comprehension of complex Arabic texts. Remember, the general rules for weak letters often carry across forms, but each form has its own specific pattern.

1.  **"Undoubtedly, Form II verbs, especially those that delivered (`وَصَّلَتْ`) deep insights, contribute to forming (`تَقْوِيمِ`) a clear methodology for human empowerment. This, nevertheless, demands (`وَيَدْعُو إِلَى`) a broad consensus to strengthen (`تَقْوِيَةِ`) societal values."**
    *   `مِمَّا لَا شَكَّ فِيهِ، أَنَّ الْأَفْعَالَ فِي الْبِنَاءِ الثَّانِي، خَاصَّةً تِلْكَ الَّتِي وَصَّلَتْ رُؤًى مُتَعَمِّقَةً، تُسْهِمُ فِي تَقْوِيمِ مَنْهَجِيَّةٍ وَاضِحَةٍ لِلتَّمْكِينِ الْبَشَرِيِّ. مَعَ ذَلِكَ، وَيَدْعُو هَذَا إِلَى تَوَافُقٍ وَاسِعٍ لِتَقْوِيَةِ الْقِيَمِ الْمُجْتَمَعِيَّةِ.`
    *   **Irregular Verb Focus:**
        *   `وَصَّلَتْ` (waṣṣalat - she delivered, past tense Form II): From `وَصَّلَ` (Assimilated, و ص ل). The `و` is present.
        *   `تَقْوِيمِ` (taqwīmi - forming/evaluating, Masdar Form II): From `قَوَّمَ` (Hollow, ق و م). The `و` is present.
        *   `تَقْوِيَةِ` (taqwiyati - strengthening, Masdar Form II): From `قَوَّى` (qawwā - he strengthened, Lafīf Maqrūn, ق و ي). `قَوَّى` is a good example to illustrate Form II Lafīf Maqrūn behavior (like `رَبَّى`).
    *   **Cohesion Breakdown:** This sentence builds on irregular verbs and policy concepts. `مِمَّا لَا شَكَّ فِيهِ، أَنَّ` (Day 196) sets a strong opening. The subject is `الْأَفْعَالَ فِي الْبِنَاءِ الثَّانِي` (Form II verbs), `خَاصَّةً تِلْكَ الَّتِي وَصَّلَتْ رُؤًى مُتَعَمِّقَةً` (especially those that delivered deep insights, Day 155 `خَاصَّةً` + Day 208 `تِلْكَ الَّتِي` + **new for today** `وَصَّلَتْ` + Day 248 `رُؤًى مُتَعَمِّقَةً`). This `تُسْهِمُ فِي تَقْوِيمِ مَنْهَجِيَّةٍ وَاضِحَةٍ لِلتَّمْكِينِ الْبَشَرِيِّ` (contributes to forming a clear methodology for human empowerment, Day 164 `تُسْهِمُ فِي` + **new for today** `تَقْوِيمٌ` Masdar + Day 236 `مَنْهَجِيَّةٍ وَاضِحَةٍ` + Day 231/189 `تَمْكِينٌ بَشَرِيٌّ`). `مَعَ ذَلِكَ، وَيَدْعُو هَذَا إِلَى تَوَافُقٍ وَاسِعٍ` (Nevertheless, this calls for a broad consensus, Day 225/156/250) `لِتَقْوِيَةِ الْقِيَمِ الْمُجْتَمَعِيَّةِ` (to strengthen societal values, Day 230 `قِيَمٌ` + Day 220 `مُجْتَمَعِيَّةٌ`).

This requires that we observe (`أَنْ نُرَاعِيَ`) diverse contexts and resist (`أَنْ نُقَاوِمَ`) misinformation."**
    *   `بَيْنَمَا يَرَى الْبَعْضُ أَنَّ السِّيَاسَاتِ الْحَالِيَّةَ تُعِيقُ الْوَعْيَ الْعَامَّ، يُجَادِلُ آخَرُونَ بِالْمُقَابِلِ أَنَّ الْمِنَصَّاتِ الرَّقْمِيَّةَ يَجِبُ أَنْ تُوَاصِلَ مَسَارَهَا الْفَرِيدَ لِتُرَبِّيَ التَّعَاطُفَ. وَهَذَا يَسْتَلْزِمُ أَنْ نُرَاعِيَ سِيَاقَاتٍ مُتَنَوِّعَةً وَأَنْ نُقَاوِمَ الْمَعْلُومَاتِ الْمُضَلِّلَةَ.`

        *   `تُرَبِّيَ` (turabbīya - she raises/fosters, subjunctive Form II): From `رَبَّى` (Deficient, ر ب و). The final `ي` remains.
        *   `نُرَاعِيَ` (nurāʿiya - we observe, subjunctive Form III): From `رَاعَى` (Deficient, ر ع ي). The final `ي` remains.
        *   `نُقَاوِمَ` (nuqāwima - we resist, subjunctive Form III): From `قَاوَمَ` (Hollow, ق و م). The `و` is present.
    *   **Cohesion Breakdown:** This sentence presents contrasting arguments (Day 146/186). The first criticizes policies for `تُعِيقُ الْوَعْيَ الْعَامَّ` (Day 230/226). The counter-argument is that digital platforms (`الْمِنَصَّاتِ الرَّقْمِيَّةَ`, Day 244) `يَجِبُ أَنْ تُوَاصِلَ مَسَارَهَا الْفَرِيدَ` (must continue their unique path, Day 121 + **new for today** `تُوَاصِلَ` + Day 228) `لِتُرَبِّيَ التَّعَاطُفَ` (to foster empathy, **new for today** `تُرَبِّيَ` + Day 248 `تَعَاطُفٌ`). The requirement (`وَهَذَا يَسْتَلْزِمُ`) is `أَنْ نُرَاعِيَ سِيَاقَاتٍ مُتَنَوِّعَةً` (to observe diverse contexts, **new for today** `نُرَاعِيَ` + Day 242 `سِيَاقَاتٍ مُتَنَوِّعَةً`) and `أَنْ نُقَاوِمَ الْمَعْلُومَاتِ الْمُضَلِّلَةَ` (to resist misleading information, **new for today** `نُقَاوِمَ` + Day 170 `مَعْلُومَاتٌ` + Day 204 `مُضَلِّلَةٌ`).

## 📚 Core Vocabulary
*   `الْبِنَاءُ الثَّانِي` (al-bināʾu ath-thānī) - Form II (masculine noun phrase)
*   `الْبِنَاءُ الثَّالِثُ` (al-bināʾu ath-thālithu) - Form III (masculine noun phrase)
*   `وَصَّلَ` (waṣṣala) - He delivered/connected (past tense verb, Form II, Mithāl)
*   `يُوَصِّلُ` (yuwaṣṣilu) - He delivers/connects (present tense verb, Form II, from وَصَّلَ)
*   `وَصَّلْتُ` (waṣṣaltu) - I delivered/connected (past tense verb, Form II, from وَصَّلَ)
*   `أُوَصِّلُ` (uwaṣṣilu) - I deliver/connect (present tense verb, Form II, from وَصَّلَ)
*   `تَوْصِيلٌ` (tawṣīlun) - Delivery, connection (masculine Masdar, Form II)
*   `قَوَّمَ` (qawwama) - He evaluated/straightened (past tense verb, Form II, Ajwaf)
*   `يُقَوِّمُ` (yuqawwimu) - He evaluates/straightens (present tense verb, Form II, from قَوَّمَ)
*   `قَوَّمْتُ` (qawwamtu) - I evaluated/straightened (past tense verb, Form II, from قَوَّمَ)
*   `أُقَوِّمُ` (uqawwimu) - I evaluate/straighten (present tense verb, Form II, from قَوَّمَ)
*   `تَقْوِيمٌ` (taqwīmun) - Evaluation, straightening (masculine Masdar, Form II)
*   `رَبَّى` (rabbā) - He raised/educated (past tense verb, Form II, Nāqiṣ)

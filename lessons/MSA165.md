# Day 165 - Advanced Journalism: Statistics language

## Lesson

"Advanced Statistics language" is all about adding even more precision and nuance to your statistical reports. Beyond simply reporting figures and trends, you need to describe the reliability of your data, the methods used to observe trends, the significance of changes, and how to present averages in a formal context.

Think of it as having a magnifying glass for your Arabic reports, allowing you to examine and present numerical information with even greater critical depth and accuracy.

Here are the key vocabulary and phrases you'll learn today:

### 1. Referring to Data Sources:

*   **`مَصْدَرٌ`** (maṣdarun) - 'maṣ-dar' (masculine noun)
    *   *Meaning:* Source.
    *   *Explanation:* This is the singular form of `مَصَادِرُ` (sources) which you learned on Day 154. You'll use it to refer to a single origin of information or data.
*   **`مُوَثَّقٌ`** (muwaththaqun) - 'mu-wath-thaq' (passive participle, masculine singular)
    *   *Meaning:* Reliable, documented, verified.
    *   *Explanation:* This adjective is used to describe a source or information that is trustworthy and officially backed. Remember to make it feminine (`مُوَثَّقَةٌ`) or plural (`مُوَثَّقُونَ` for humans, `مُوَثَّقَاتٌ` for non-human feminine plural, or `مُوَثَّقَةٌ` for non-human broken plural) if needed.
    *   *Common phrase:* `مَصَادِرُ مُوَثَّقَةٌ` (maṣādiru muwaththaqatun) - 'ma-ṣaa-di-ru mu-wath-tha-qa-tun' (reliable sources).

### 2. Describing Data Collection and Results:

*   **`يُسَجِّلُ`** (yusajjilu) - 'yu-sa-jji-lu' (present tense verb, Form II)
    *   *Meaning:* He records / registers.
    *   *Explanation:* This verb is very common in journalistic and academic contexts to state that a certain figure or event has been noted or reached a specific point. Its feminine form is `تُسَجِّلُ` (tusajjilu).
        *   *Example:* `الْأَرْقَامُ تُسَجِّلُ انْخِفَاضًا.` (The figures record a decrease.)
*   **`يَرْصُدُ`** (yarṣudu) - 'yar-ṣu-du' (present tense verb, Form I)
    *   *Meaning:* He monitors / observes.
    *   *Explanation:* Use this verb when describing continuous observation or tracking of trends and data. Its feminine form is `تَرْصُدُ` (tarṣudu).
        *   *Example:* `الدِّرَاسَةُ تَرْصُدُ التَّغَيُّرَاتِ.` (The study monitors the changes.)

### 3. Emphasizing Noticeable Change:

*   **`بِشَكْلٍ مَلْحُوظٍ`** (bi-shaklin malḥūẓin) - 'bi-shak-lin mal-ḥoo-ẓin' (adverbial phrase)
    *   *Meaning:* Noticeably, significantly, remarkably.
    *   *Explanation:* You know `بِشَكْلٍ كَبِيرٍ` (significantly, Day 155). This phrase provides another way to express that a change or impact is easy to observe and important. `مَلْحُوظٌ` (malḥūẓun) is a passive participle (Form VIII) meaning 'noticeable' or 'observed'.

### 4. Identifying Indicators:

*   **`مُؤَشِّرٌ`** (mu'ashshirun) - 'mu-'ash-shi-run' (active participle, masculine singular)
    *   *Meaning:* Indicator, sign.
    *   *Explanation:* This noun is crucial for showing what specific data points or trends suggest about a broader situation. It's an active participle (Form IV).
    *   *Plural:* `مُؤَشِّرَاتٌ` (mu'ashshirātun) - 'mu-'ash-shi-raat' (feminine plural).

### 5. Stating Averages:

*   **`فِي الْمُتَوَسِّطِ`** (fī al-mutawassiṭi) - 'fee al-mu-ta-was-siṭ' (prepositional phrase)
    *   *Meaning:* On average.
    *   *Explanation:* This is a common and formal way to refer to the average value. You've seen `مُتَوَسِّطَاتٌ عَالَمِيَّةٌ` (global averages, Day 158), so `مُتَوَسِّطٌ` (average) itself is familiar. This phrase lets you use it adverbially.

Remember to seamlessly integrate these new terms with your existing advanced grammar, statistical vocabulary (Day 151, 158), polling language (Days 152, 159), corporate terms (Days 153, 160), energy vocabulary (Days 154, 161), narrative techniques (Days 155, 162), academic register (Days 156, 163), and especially with your comprehensive argumentation structures. This will enable you to write and speak about data with exceptional clarity, authority, and analytical rigor.

## Examples

1.  **"According to reliable sources, the unemployment rate recorded a noticeable decrease last month. This is a positive indicator for economic recovery."**
    *   `وَفْقًا لِمَصَادِرَ مُوَثَّقَةٍ، سَجَّلَ مُعَدَّلُ الْبِطَالَةِ انْخِفَاضًا مَلْحُوظًا الشَّهْرَ الْمَاضِي. وَهَذَا مُؤَشِّرٌ إِيجَابِيٌّ لِلتَّعَافِي الِاقْتِصَادِيِّ.`
    *   **Breakdown:**
        *   `وَفْقًا لِمَصَادِرَ مُوَثَّقَةٍ` (According to reliable sources - `وَفْقًا لِـ` Day 158; `مَصَادِرَ مُوَثَّقَةٍ` (**new for today** plural of `مَصْدَرٌ مُوَثَّقٌ`)). `مَصَادِرَ` is a diptote, so it takes Fatha here.
        *   `سَجَّلَ مُعَدَّلُ الْبِطَالَةِ انْخِفَاضًا مَلْحُوظًا الشَّهْرَ الْمَاضِي` (the unemployment rate recorded a noticeable decrease last month - `سَجَّلَ` (**new for today**); `مُعَدَّلُ الْبِطَالَةِ` (unemployment rate, Day 151/139); `انْخِفَاضًا` (decrease, Masdar, Day 115 context); `مَلْحُوظًا` (**new for today**) Accusative adjective; `الشَّهْرَ الْمَاضِي` (last month, Day 151 context)).
        *   `وَهَذَا مُؤَشِّرٌ إِيجَابِيٌّ لِلتَّعَافِي الِاقْتِصَادِيِّ` (And this is a positive indicator for economic recovery - `مُؤَشِّرٌ` (**new for today**); `إِيجَابِيٌّ` (positive, Day 127); `لِلتَّعَافِي الِاقْتِصَادِيِّ` (for economic recovery - `تَعَافٍ` (recovery, Day 162 context `يَتَعَافَى`), `اقْتِصَادِيٌّ` (economic, Day 103)) Genitive).

2.  **"While some believe that the government monitors energy prices effectively, others point out that fluctuations in global markets are still significantly affecting net profits, especially for small companies."**
    *   `بَيْنَمَا يَرَى الْبَعْضُ أَنَّ الْحُكُومَةَ تَرْصُدُ أَسْعَارَ الطَّاقَةِ بِفَعَالِيَّةٍ، يُشِيرُ آخَرُونَ إِلَى أَنَّ التَّقَلُّبَاتِ فِي الْأَسْوَاقِ الْعَالَمِيَّةِ لَا تَزَالُ تُؤَثِّرُ بِشَكْلٍ مَلْحُوظٍ فِي صَافِي الْأَرْبَاحِ، خَاصَّةً لِلشَّرِكَاتِ الصَّغِيرَةِ.`
    *   **Breakdown:**
        *   `بَيْنَمَا يَرَى الْبَعْضُ أَنَّ` (While some believe that - Day 146).
        *   `الْحُكُومَةَ تَرْصُدُ أَسْعَارَ الطَّاقَةِ بِفَعَالِيَّةٍ` (the government monitors energy prices effectively - `الْحُكُومَةَ` (government, Day 101) Accusative subject of `أَنَّ`; `تَرْصُدُ` (**new for today**); `أَسْعَارَ الطَّاقَةِ` (energy prices, Day 154); `بِفَعَالِيَّةٍ` (effectively - `فَعَالِيَّةٌ` (effectiveness, Day 147))).
        *   `يُشِيرُ آخَرُونَ إِلَى أَنَّ` (others point out that - Day 146).
        *   `التَّقَلُّبَاتِ فِي الْأَسْوَاقِ الْعَالَمِيَّةِ لَا تَزَالُ تُؤَثِّرُ بِشَكْلٍ مَلْحُوظٍ فِي صَافِي الْأَرْبَاحِ` (fluctuations in global markets are still significantly affecting net profits - `التَّقَلُّبَاتِ` (fluctuations, Day 158) Accusative subject of `أَنَّ`; `فِي الْأَسْوَاقِ الْعَالَمِيَّةِ` (in global markets, Day 103); `لَا تَزَالُ تُؤَثِّرُ` (still affects, Day 143/122); `بِشَكْلٍ مَلْحُوظٍ` (**new for today**); `صَافِي الْأَرْبَاحِ` (net profits, Day 160) Genitive).
        *   `خَاصَّةً لِلشَّرِكَاتِ الصَّغِيرَةِ` (especially for small companies - `خَاصَّةً لِـ` (especially for, Day 108); `الشَّرِكَاتِ الصَّغِيرَةِ` (small companies, Day 120/54)).

    *   `مِنَ الْمُهِمِّ الْإِشَارَةُ إِلَى أَنَّ الدِّرَاسَاتِ الْحَدِيثَةَ تُؤَكِّدُ أَنَّهُ، فِي الْمُتَوَسِّطِ، نِسْبَةَ الشَّبَابِ الْمُهْتَمِّ بِالْفُنُونِ التَّقْلِيدِيَّةِ مُتَنَاقِصَةٌ. وَهَذَا يَعْكِسُ تَحَدِّيًا خَطِيرًا لِلْحِفَاظِ عَلَى التُّرَاثِ الثَّقَافِيِّ.`

        *   `فِي الْمُتَوَسِّطِ` (**new for today**).
        *   `نِسْبَةَ الشَّبَابِ الْمُهْتَمِّ بِالْفُنُونِ التَّقْلِيدِيَّةِ مُتَنَاقِصَةٌ` (the percentage of young people interested in traditional arts is decreasing - `نِسْبَةَ` (percentage, Day 151) Accusative subject of `أَنَّ`; `الشَّبَابِ الْمُهْتَمِّ بِـ` (youth interested in, Day 134/122); `الْفُنُونِ التَّقْلِيدِيَّةِ` (traditional arts, Day 136); `مُتَنَاقِصَةٌ` (decreasing, feminine, Day 151) Nominative predicate).
        *   `وَهَذَا يَعْكِسُ تَحَدِّيًا خَطِيرًا لِلْحِفَاظِ عَلَى التُّرَاثِ الثَّقَافِيِّ` (This reflects a serious challenge for the preservation of cultural heritage - `يَعْكِسُ` (reflects, Day 159); `تَحَدِّيًا خَطِيرًا` (serious challenge, Day 105/155); `لِلْحِفَاظِ عَلَى` (for the preservation of - `حِفَاظٌ` (preservation, new Masdar Form III, related to `يَحْفَظُ` Day 155)); `التُّرَاثِ الثَّقَافِيِّ` (cultural heritage, Day 155/129)).

## 📚 Core Vocabulary
*   مَصْدَرٌ (maṣdarun) - Source (masculine noun)
*   مُوَثَّقٌ (muwaththaqun) - Reliable, documented, verified (passive participle, masculine singular)
*   يُسَجِّلُ (yusajjilu) - He records / registers (present tense verb, Form II)
*   يَرْصُدُ (yarṣudu) - He monitors / observes (present tense verb, Form I)
*   بِشَكْلٍ مَلْحُوظٍ (bi-shaklin malḥūẓin) - Noticeably, significantly, remarkably (adverbial phrase)
*   مُؤَشِّرٌ (mu'ashshirun) - Indicator, sign (active participle, masculine singular)
*   مُؤَشِّرَاتٌ (mu'ashshirātun) - Indicators (feminine plural noun)
*   فِي الْمُتَوَسِّطِ (fī al-mutawassiṭi) - On average (prepositional phrase)

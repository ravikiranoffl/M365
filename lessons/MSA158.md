# Day 158 - Advanced Journalism: Statistics language

## Lesson

"Advanced Statistics language" is about enhancing your ability to communicate numerical data with greater sophistication. Beyond simply stating numbers and percentages, you'll learn how to describe *how* those numbers are behaving – are they stable, fluctuating, or hitting a peak? You'll also learn how to compare data points effectively and introduce future projections, as well as correctly attribute your sources.

Think of it as adding finer brushes to your advanced Arabic toolkit, allowing you to paint a more detailed and analytical picture with your statistical reports.

Here are the key vocabulary and phrases you'll learn today:

1.  **Describing Trends and Stability:**
    *   `مُسْتَقِرٌّ` (mustaqirrun) - 'mus-ta-qir-run' (active participle, masculine singular)
        *   *Meaning:* Stable.
        *   *Explanation:* Use this adjective to describe a number, rate, or situation that is not changing much over time. Remember to make it feminine (`مُسْتَقِرَّةٌ`) or plural if needed.
    *   `تَقَلُّبَاتٌ` (taqallubātun) - 'ta-qal-lu-baat' (feminine plural noun)
        *   *Meaning:* Fluctuations, volatility.
        *   *Explanation:* This noun refers to irregular variations or changes, often used in economic or market contexts. Its singular is `تَقَلُّبٌ`.

2.  **Referring to Data Extremes:**
    *   `ذِرْوَةٌ` (dhirwatun) - 'dhir-wa-tun' (feminine noun)
        *   *Meaning:* Peak, summit (the highest point a number or rate has reached).
        *   *Explanation:* Use this to highlight the maximum value observed in a data set or trend.

3.  **Comparing Data:**
    *   `مُقَارَنَةً بِـ` (muqāranatan bi-) - 'mu-qaa-ra-na-tan bi-' (Masdar + preposition)
        *   *Meaning:* Compared to.
        *   *Explanation:* This phrase is essential for drawing comparisons between different data points, periods, or entities. It literally means "in comparison with."
    *   `بِالْقِيَاسِ إِلَى` (bi-l-qiyāsi ilā) - 'bi-l-qiy-yaa-si i-laa' (Masdar + preposition)
        *   *Meaning:* In comparison to / relative to.
        *   *Explanation:* Similar to `مُقَارَنَةً بِـ`, this phrase provides another formal way to introduce a comparison. `قِيَاسٌ` means 'measurement' or 'comparison'.

4.  **Discussing Forecasts and Projections:**
    *   `تَوَقُّعَاتٌ` (tawaqqu'ātun) - 'ta-waq-qu-'aat' (feminine plural noun)
        *   *Meaning:* Forecasts, expectations, projections.
        *   *Explanation:* This noun refers to predictions or estimates about future trends or outcomes. Its singular is `تَوَقُّعٌ`.
    *   `يَتَوَقَّعُ` (yatawaqqa'u) - 'ya-ta-waq-qa-'u' (present tense verb, Form V)
        *   *Meaning:* He expects / forecasts.
        *   *Explanation:* This is the verb form of `تَوَقُّعَاتٌ`, used to describe the act of making a prediction. Feminine form: `تَتَوَقَّعُ` (tatawaqqa'u).

5.  **Attributing Data Sources:**
    *   `وَفْقًا لِـ` (wafqan li-) - 'waf-qan li-' (preposition)
        *   *Meaning:* According to.
        *   *Explanation:* This is a highly formal and common way to cite the source of any information, including statistics, studies, or reports.

Remember to seamlessly integrate these new terms with your existing advanced grammar, the statistics vocabulary you learned on Day 151 (`إِحْصَاءَاتٌ`, `أَرْقَامٌ`, `مُعَدَّلٌ`, `نِسْبَةٌ مِئَوِيَّةٌ`, `يَبْلُغُ`, `يُظْهِرُ`, `يُشِيرُ إِلَى أَنَّ`), as well as your polling, corporate, energy, narrative, academic, and argumentation structures.

## Examples

Let's look at some examples that seamlessly integrate this new "advanced statistics language" vocabulary with other learned concepts. Carefully read each English sentence, then the Arabic translation, and pay close attention to the breakdown of how different elements are combined to create a more formal and analytical tone.

1.  **"It is important to point out that despite some fluctuations, the economic growth rate remained stable this quarter, reaching approximately 4%. This is high compared to the previous year."**
    *   `مِنَ الْمُهِمِّ الْإِشَارَةُ إِلَى أَنَّ مُعَدَّلَ النُّمُوِّ الِاقْتِصَادِيِّ، بِالرَّغْمِ مِنْ بَعْضِ التَّقَلُّبَاتِ، بَقِيَ مُسْتَقِرًّا هَذَا الرُّبْعَ، بَالِغًا حَوَالَيْ 4%. وَهَذَا يُعْتَبَرُ عَالِيًا مُقَارَنَةً بِالْعَامِ الْمَاضِي.`
    *   **Breakdown:**
        *   `مِنَ الْمُهِمِّ الْإِشَارَةُ إِلَى أَنَّ` (It is important to point out that - Day 156).
        *   `مُعَدَّلَ النُّمُوِّ الِاقْتِصَادِيِّ` (the economic growth rate - Day 151/103/115) Accusative subject of `أَنَّ`.
        *   `بِالرَّغْمِ مِنْ بَعْضِ التَّقَلُّبَاتِ` (despite some fluctuations - `بِالرَّغْمِ مِنْ` Day 107; `تَقَلُّبَاتٌ` **new for today**).
        *   `بَقِيَ مُسْتَقِرًّا هَذَا الرُّبْعَ، بَالِغًا حَوَالَيْ 4%` (remained stable this quarter, reaching approximately 4% - `بَقِيَ` (he remained, new verb Form I, very common); `مُسْتَقِرًّا` (**new for today**) Accusative predicate; `هَذَا الرُّبْعَ` (this quarter, Day 153); `بَالِغًا` (reaching, active participle, Day 151 `يَبْلُغُ`); `حَوَالَيْ 4%` (approximately 4%, Day 151)).
        *   `وَهَذَا يُعْتَبَرُ عَالِيًا مُقَارَنَةً بِالْعَامِ الْمَاضِي` (And this is considered high compared to the previous year - `يُعْتَبَرُ` (is considered, passive verb, Day 140 context); `عَالِيًا` (high, Day 151 context `رَقْمٌ عَالٍ`); `مُقَارَنَةً بِالْعَامِ الْمَاضِي` (**new for today**/Day 110)).

2.  **"According to official statistics, the unemployment rate reached its peak last month. However, forecasts indicate that it will decrease in the coming period."**
    *   `وَفْقًا لِلْإِحْصَاءَاتِ الرَّسْمِيَّةِ، بَلَغَ مُعَدَّلُ الْبِطَالَةِ ذِرْوَتَهُ فِي الشَّهْرِ الْمَاضِي. إِلَّا أَنَّ التَّوَقُّعَاتِ تُشِيرُ إِلَى أَنَّهُ سَيَتَنَاقَصُ فِي الْفَتْرَةِ الْقَادِمَةِ.`
    *   **Breakdown:**
        *   `وَفْقًا لِلْإِحْصَاءَاتِ الرَّسْمِيَّةِ` (According to official statistics - `وَفْقًا لِـ` **new for today**; `إِحْصَاءَاتٌ` Day 151; `رَسْمِيَّةٌ` (official, Day 101/103)).
        *   `بَلَغَ مُعَدَّلُ الْبِطَالَةِ ذِرْوَتَهُ فِي الشَّهْرِ الْمَاضِي` (the unemployment rate reached its peak last month - `بَلَغَ` Day 151; `مُعَدَّلُ الْبِطَالَةِ` (unemployment rate, Day 151/139); `ذِرْوَتَهُ` (**new for today**) Accusative object + pronoun; `الشَّهْرِ الْمَاضِي` (last month, Day 151 context)).
        *   `إِلَّا أَنَّ التَّوَقُّعَاتِ تُشِيرُ إِلَى أَنَّهُ سَيَتَنَاقَصُ فِي الْفَتْرَةِ الْقَادِمَةِ` (However, forecasts indicate that it will decrease in the coming period - `إِلَّا أَنَّ` (however, Day 139); `التَّوَقُّعَاتِ` (**new for today**) Accusative subject of `أَنَّ`; `تُشِيرُ إِلَى أَنَّ` (she indicates that, Day 123); `سَيَتَنَاقَصُ` (it will decrease - `يَتَنَاقَصُ` Form V, new verb derived from `مُتَنَاقِصٌ` Day 151); `الْفَتْرَةِ الْقَادِمَةِ` (the coming period, Day 113)).

3.  **"While some experts expect a big increase in renewable energy investments, others point out that the current rate of production is low in comparison to demand. Therefore, new strategies are required."**
    *   `بَيْنَمَا يَتَوَقَّعُ بَعْضُ الْخُبَرَاءِ زِيَادَةً كَبِيرَةً فِي اسْتِثْمَارَاتِ الطَّاقَةِ الْمُتَجَدِّدَةِ، يُشِيرُ آخَرُونَ إِلَى أَنَّ مُعَدَّلَ الْإِنْتَاجِ الْحَالِيَّ مُنْخَفِضٌ بِالْقِيَاسِ إِلَى الطَّلَبِ. لِذَلِكَ، اسْتِرَاتِيجِيَّاتٌ جَدِيدَةٌ مَطْلُوبَةٌ.`
    *   **Breakdown:**
        *   `بَيْنَمَا يَتَوَقَّعُ بَعْضُ الْخُبَرَاءِ` (While some experts expect - `يَتَوَقَّعُ` **new for today**; `خُبَرَاءُ` Day 123).
        *   `زِيَادَةً كَبِيرَةً فِي اسْتِثْمَارَاتِ الطَّاقَةِ الْمُتَجَدِّدَةِ` (a big increase in renewable energy investments - `زِيَادَةٌ كَبِيرَةٌ` (Day 115/54); `اسْتِثْمَارَاتِ الطَّاقَةِ الْمُتَجَدِّدَةِ` (renewable energy investments, Day 120/154)).
        *   `يُشِيرُ آخَرُونَ إِلَى أَنَّ` (others point out that - Day 146).
        *   `مُعَدَّلَ الْإِنْتَاجِ الْحَالِيَّ مُنْخَفِضٌ بِالْقِيَاسِ إِلَى الطَّلَبِ` (the current rate of production is low in comparison to demand - `مُعَدَّلَ الْإِنْتَاجِ الْحَالِيَّ` (current production rate, Day 151/140/144); `مُنْخَفِضٌ` (low, new adjective, from `انْخَفَضَ` - to decrease); `بِالْقِيَاسِ إِلَى الطَّلَبِ` (**new for today**/`طَلَبٌ` - demand, new noun).
        *   `لِذَلِكَ، اسْتِرَاتِيجِيَّاتٌ جَدِيدَةٌ مَطْلُوبَةٌ` (Therefore, new strategies are required - Day 132/152/146).

## 📚 Core Vocabulary
*   مُسْتَقِرٌّ (mustaqirrun) - Stable (active participle, masculine singular)
*   تَقَلُّبَاتٌ (taqallubātun) - Fluctuations (feminine plural noun)
*   ذِرْوَةٌ (dhirwatun) - Peak, summit (feminine noun)
*   مُقَارَنَةً بِـ (muqāranatan bi-) - Compared to (Masdar + preposition)
*   بِالْقِيَاسِ إِلَى (bi-l-qiyāsi ilā) - In comparison to / relative to (Masdar + preposition)
*   تَوَقُّعَاتٌ (tawaqqu'ātun) - Forecasts, expectations, projections (feminine plural noun)
*   يَتَوَقَّعُ (yatawaqqa'u) - He expects / forecasts (present tense verb, Form V)
*   وَفْقًا لِـ (wafqan li-) - According to (preposition)
*   بَقِيَ (baqiya) - He remained (past tense verb, Form I)
*   يَتَنَاقَصُ (yatanaqqaṣu) - It will decrease (present tense verb, Form V)
*   مُنْخَفِضٌ (munkhafiḍun) - Low (masculine adjective)
*   طَلَبٌ (ṭalabun) - Demand (masculine noun)
*   رُبْعٌ قَادِمٌ (rubʿun qādimun) - Next quarter (masculine noun + adjective)
*   مُتَوَسِّطَاتٌ عَالَمِيَّةٌ (mutawaṣṣiṭātun 'ālamiyyatun) - Global averages (feminine plural noun phrase)
*   سَنَوَاتٌ سَابِقَةٌ (sanawātun sābiqatun) - Previous years (feminine plural noun phrase)

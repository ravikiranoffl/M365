# Day 191 - Academic Arabic structures

## Lesson

"Academic Arabic structures" at this advanced stage focuses on providing you with the tools to construct and deconstruct complex intellectual arguments with exceptional clarity and authority. It's about moving from simply stating facts to expertly *defining their nature*, *showing their inherent qualities*, *comprehensively enumerating their components*, and *clearly stating what they demand*.

Think of it as having the ultimate set of refined architectural tools to build academically sound and journalistically impactful analyses. You'll learn specific terms for precisely characterizing phenomena, indicating comprehensive scope, expressing fundamental requirements, and describing ingrained attributes.

Here are the key vocabulary and phrases you'll learn today:

### 1. Defining and Constituting:

*   **`يُشَكِّلُ`** (yushakkilu) - 'yu-shak-ki-lu' (present tense verb, Form II)
    *   *Meaning:* He constitutes / forms / represents.
    *   *Explanation:* This verb is highly versatile in academic contexts. It's used to state what something is, how it's formed, or what role it plays. You've encountered `تُشَكِّلُ` (she shapes, Day 166) in the context of shaping public opinion. Here, it denotes forming or constituting a part of something. Its feminine form is `تُشَكِّلُ` (tushakkilu).
        *   *Example:* `يُشَكِّلُ التَّمْوِيلُ الْأَخْضَرُ مُكَوِّنًا رَئِيسِيًّا لِلتَّنْمِيَةِ الْمُسْتَدَامَةِ.` (Green financing constitutes a main component for sustainable development.)

### 2. Comprehensive Inclusion:

*   **`بِمَا فِي ذَلِكَ`** (bimā fī dhālika) - 'bi-maa fee dhaa-li-ka' (prepositional phrase)
    *   *Meaning:* Including, including that.
    *   *Explanation:* This is a very common and useful phrase for indicating that a broader statement or category encompasses specific items or examples. It shows a comprehensive understanding of the scope.
        *   *Example:* `تُوَاجِهُ الْحُكُومَةُ تَحَدِّيَاتٍ عَدِيدَةً، بِمَا فِي ذَلِكَ الْأَزْمَةُ الِاقْتِصَادِيَّةُ.` (The government faces many challenges, including the economic crisis.)

### 3. Emphasizing Equality or Universality:

*   **`عَلَى حَدٍّ سَوَاءٍ`** (ʿalā ḥaddin sawāʾ) - 'ʿa-laa ḥad-din sa-waa-' (adverbial phrase)
    *   *Meaning:* Equally, alike.
    *   *Explanation:* This phrase is used to highlight that something applies to, affects, or is true for different entities or groups in the same way, without distinction. It emphasizes fairness or a common impact.
        *   *Example:* `يَتَأَثَّرُ الْعُمَّالُ وَأَصْحَابُ الْمَصْلَحَةِ عَلَى حَدٍّ سَوَاءٍ بِهَذِهِ السِّيَاسَاتِ.` (Workers and stakeholders are affected equally by these policies.)

### 4. Expressing Inherent Qualities:

*   **`مُتَأَصِّلٌ`** (mutaʾaṣṣilun) - 'mu-ta-ʾaṣ-ṣi-lun' (masculine active participle, Form V)
    *   *Meaning:* Deep-rooted, inherent, ingrained, intrinsic.
    *   *Explanation:* This adjective is used to describe a quality, characteristic, or problem that is fundamental, intrinsic, or deeply embedded within something, rather than being superficial or temporary. Its feminine form is `مُتَأَصِّلَةٌ` (mutaʾaṣṣilatun).
        *   *Example:* `الْفَسَادُ مُتَأَصِّلٌ فِي بَعْضِ الْأَنْظِمَةِ.` (Corruption is deep-rooted in some systems.)

### 5. Indicating Necessity or Entailment (Stronger):

*   **`يَسْتَلْزِمُ`** (yastalzimu) - 'yas-tal-zi-mu' (present tense verb, Form X)
    *   *Meaning:* He necessitates / requires / entails.
    *   *Explanation:* This is a highly formal and strong verb for indicating that something logically or essentially demands a particular outcome, action, or condition. It's a robust alternative to `يَتَطَلَّبُ` (he requires, Day 140). Its feminine form is `تَسْتَلْزِمُ` (tastalzimu).
        *   *Example:* `هَذَا التَّحَوُّلُ يَسْتَلْزِمُ تَغْيِيرَاتٍ جَذْرِيَّةً.` (This transformation necessitates radical changes.)

### 6. Adding Further Strong Points:

*   **`فَضْلًا عَنْ`** (faḍlan ʿan) - 'faḍ-lan ʿan' (prepositional phrase)
    *   *Meaning:* In addition to, let alone, besides.
    *   *Explanation:* This phrase is used to introduce an additional point, often implying that the new point is even more significant or obvious than the previous one, or simply a strong additive. It can be used similarly to `بِالإِضَافَةِ إِلَى` (Day 186) but often carries a slightly stronger emphasis or a sense of "not to mention."
        *   *Example:* `يُعَانُونَ مِنَ الْفَقْرِ فَضْلًا عَنِ الْبِطَالَةِ.` (They suffer from poverty, in addition to unemployment.)

Remember to seamlessly integrate these new terms with your existing advanced grammar, argumentation strategies, and specialized vocabulary from all previous lessons. This will empower you to construct and critique complex arguments with the highest level of scholarly and journalistic precision.

## 📚 Core Vocabulary
*   يُشَكِّلُ (yushakkilu) - He constitutes / forms / represents (present tense verb, Form II)
*   تُشَكِّلُ (tushakkilu) - She constitutes / forms / represents (present tense verb, Form II)
*   بِمَا فِي ذَلِكَ (bimā fī dhālika) - Including, including that (prepositional phrase)
*   عَلَى حَدٍّ سَوَاءٍ (ʿalā ḥaddin sawāʾ) - Equally, alike (adverbial phrase)
*   مُتَأَصِّلٌ (mutaʾaṣṣilun) - Deep-rooted, inherent, ingrained, intrinsic (masculine active participle, Form V)
*   مُتَأَصِّلَةٌ (mutaʾaṣṣilatun) - Deep-rooted, inherent, ingrained, intrinsic (feminine active participle, Form V)
*   يَسْتَلْزِمُ (yastalzimu) - He necessitates / requires / entails (present tense verb, Form X)
*   تَسْتَلْزِمُ (tastalzimu) - She necessitates / requires / entails (present tense verb, Form X)
*   فَضْلًا عَنْ (faḍlan ʿan) - In addition to, let alone, besides (prepositional phrase)
*   مَنَاطِقُ (manāṭiqu) - Areas, regions (feminine plural noun, broken plural, diptote)
*   مِنْطَقَةٌ (minṭaqatun) - Region, area (feminine noun)
*   نَائِيَةٌ (nāʾiyatun) - Remote (feminine adjective)
*   يَتَغَلَّبُ عَلَى (yataghallabu ʿalā) - He overcomes (present tense verb + preposition, Form V)
*   دَاخِلَ (dākhila) - Inside (preposition)
*   إِدَارَاتٌ (idārātun) - Departments (feminine plural noun)

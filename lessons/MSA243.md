# Day 243 - Feature writing techniques

## Lesson

"Feature writing" (`الْمَقَالَةُ الْمُمَيَّزَةُ`) is a journalistic art form that aims to provide a more comprehensive, personal, and engaging perspective than traditional news reports or editorials. It often employs literary techniques to connect with the reader on a deeper level. At this advanced stage, understanding feature writing means grasping its core elements and the specialized vocabulary to describe them.

Today's lesson focuses on giving you tools to:

1.  **Refer to "feature" or "distinguished" articles.**
2.  **Describe the use of storytelling in writing.**
3.  **Emphasize deep, thorough exploration of a topic.**
4.  **Highlight the focus on individual experiences and emotions.**
5.  **Articulate the adoption of a novel or distinct perspective.**
6.  **Discuss strategies for captivating the audience.**

These terms will significantly enhance your ability to understand and produce this nuanced and engaging form of journalistic prose.

Here are the key vocabulary and phrases you'll learn today:

### 1. Feature Article:

*   **`مَقَالَةٌ مُمَيَّزَةٌ`** (maqālatun mumayyazatun) - 'ma-qaa-la-tun mu-may-ya-zah' (feminine noun + adjective)
    *   *Meaning:* Feature article, distinguished article/writing.
    *   *Explanation:* This term specifically refers to a journalistic piece that distinguishes itself by its in-depth treatment, narrative style, or human-interest focus, as opposed to a regular news report (`تَقْرِيرٌ إِخْبَارِيٌّ`) or opinion piece (`افْتِتَاحِيَّةٌ`). `مُمَيَّزَةٌ` (distinguished/special) comes from the verb `يُمَيِّزُ` (to distinguish, Form II).
    *   *Plural:* **`مَقَالَاتٌ مُمَيَّزَةٌ`** (maqālātun mumayyazatun).
    *   *Related Verb:* **`يُمَيِّزُ`** (yumayyizun) - 'yu-may-yi-zu' (present tense verb, Form II) - He distinguishes, characterizes.
        *   *Example:* `تُقَدِّمُ هَذِهِ الْمَقَالَةُ الْمُمَيَّزَةُ تَعَمُّقًا فَرِيدًا.` (This feature article offers a unique in-depth study.) (`تُقَدِّمُ` - offers, Day 215; `فَرِيدًا` - unique, Day 228).

### 2. Narrative Storytelling:

*   **`سَرْدٌ قَصَصِيٌّ`** (sardun qaṣaṣīyun) - 'sar-dun qa-ṣa-ṣee-yun' (masculine Masdar + adjective)
    *   *Meaning:* Narrative storytelling.
    *   *Explanation:* This refers to the technique of presenting information through a story, engaging the reader with a plot, characters, and a clear sequence of events. `سَرْدٌ` (narration) was learned on Day 213, and `قَصَصِيٌّ` is a new adjective derived from `قِصَّةٌ` (story, Day 134 context).
        *   *Example:* `يَعْتَمِدُ الْكَاتِبُ عَلَى سَرْدٍ قَصَصِيٍّ لِجَذْبِ الْقَارِئِ.` (The writer relies on narrative storytelling to attract the reader.) (`يَعْتَمِدُ عَلَى` - relies on, Day 154; `كَاتِبٌ` - writer, new noun, active participle from `كَتَبَ` to write).

### 3. In-depth Exploration:

*   **`تَعَمُّقٌ`** (taʿammuqun) - 'ta-ʿam-muq' (masculine Masdar, Form V)
    *   *Meaning:* In-depth study, deepening, delving.
    *   *Explanation:* This emphasizes going beyond surface-level facts to explore the complexities, nuances, and underlying aspects of a subject. It is the Masdar of `يَتَعَمَّقُ` (to delve deeply), related to `عَمِيقٌ` (deep, Day 188).
    *   *Related Verb:* **`يَتَعَمَّقُ`** (yataʿammuqu) - 'ya-ta-ʿam-ma-qu' (present tense verb, Form V) - He delves deeply, studies in depth.
        *   *Example:* `تَتَطَلَّبُ الْقَضَايَا الْمُلِحَّةُ تَعَمُّقًا تَحْلِيلِيًّا.` (Urgent issues require analytical depth.) (`تَتَطَلَّبُ` - requires, Day 121; `قَضَايَا مُلِحَّةٌ` - urgent issues, Day 215; `تَحْلِيلِيًّا` - analytical, Day 231 context).

### 4. Human Interest Aspect:

*   **`جَانِبٌ إِنْسَانِيٌّ`** (jānibun insānīyun) - 'jaa-ni-bun in-saa-nee-yun' (masculine noun + adjective)
    *   *Meaning:* Human interest aspect/side.
    *   *Explanation:* This phrase highlights the focus on the personal experiences, emotions, and struggles of individuals, aiming to create empathy and connection with the reader. `جَانِبٌ` (side/aspect) was introduced in context on Day 109 and Day 143, and `إِنْسَانِيٌّ` (human) on Day 189.
        *   *Example:* `تُبْرِزُ الْمَقَالَةُ الْجَانِبَ الْإِنْسَانِيَّ لِلْأَزْمَةِ.` (The article highlights the human aspect of the crisis.) (`تُبْرِزُ` - highlights, Day 233; `أَزْمَةٌ` - crisis, Day 146).

### 5. Unique Angle:

*   **`زَاوِيَةٌ فَرِيدَةٌ`** (zāwiyatun farīdatun) - 'zaa-wi-ya-tun fa-ree-da-tun' (feminine noun + adjective)
    *   *Meaning:* Unique angle/perspective.
    *   *Explanation:* Feature writers often seek an original or fresh way of presenting a familiar topic to make it distinctive and engaging. `زَاوِيَةٌ` (angle) was in the context of `مِنْ زَاوِيَةٍ أُخْرَى` (from another angle, Day 213), and `فَرِيدَةٌ` (unique) was taught on Day 228.
        *   *Example:* `تَتَّسِمُ الْمَقَالَةُ بِزَاوِيَةٍ فَرِيدَةٍ لِمُعَالَجَةِ الْمُشْكِلَةِ.` (The article is characterized by a unique angle for addressing the problem.) (`تَتَّسِمُ بِـ` - is characterized by, Day 223; `مُعَالَجَةِ` - addressing, Day 188; `مُشْكِلَةٌ` - problem, Day 198).

### 6. Reader Engagement:

*   **`جَذْبُ الْقَارِئِ`** (jadb al-qāriʾi) - 'jad-bu al-qaa-ri-ʾi' (masculine Masdar + noun)
    *   *Meaning:* Attracting the reader, reader engagement.
    *   *Explanation:* This refers to the various techniques and literary devices employed by a writer to capture and maintain the reader's attention and interest throughout the feature article. `جَذْبٌ` (attraction) is a new Masdar from the Form I verb `جَذَبَ` (jadhabu - to attract), and `الْقَارِئِ` (the reader) is a new active participle from `قَرَأَ` (qaraʾa - to read).
        *   *Example:* `يَهْدِفُ الْكَاتِبُ إِلَى جَذْبِ الْقَارِئِ مِنْ خِلَالِ الْوَصْفِ الْحَيِّ.` (The writer aims to attract the reader through vivid description.) (`يَهْدِفُ إِلَى` - aims to, Day 131; `كَاتِبٌ` - writer, new; `مِنْ خِلَالِ` - through, Day 201; `وَصْفٌ حَيٌّ` - vivid description, new phrase, `وَصْفٌ` - description from `وَصَفَ` to describe, `حَيٌّ` - vivid/living, Day 107 context).

By mastering these terms, you'll be well-equipped to analyze and appreciate the distinct techniques of feature writing, adding another sophisticated layer to your advanced Arabic journalistic skills.

## Examples

Let's look at some examples that seamlessly integrate this new "feature writing" vocabulary with other learned concepts. Carefully read each English sentence, then the Arabic translation, and pay close attention to the breakdown of how different elements are combined to create a highly formal and descriptive tone, with an emphasis on cohesion.

1.  **"Undoubtedly, this feature article offers an in-depth study of the human interest aspect, providing a comprehensive outlook on its complex repercussions. It relies on narrative storytelling and a unique angle to attract the reader."**
    *   `مِمَّا لَا شَكَّ فِيهِ، أَنَّ هَذِهِ الْمَقَالَةَ الْمُمَيَّزَةَ تُقَدِّمُ تَعَمُّقًا فِي الْجَانِبِ الْإِنْسَانِيِّ، مُقَدِّمَةً نَظْرَةً شَامِلَةً لِتَدَاعِيَاتِهِ الْمُعَقَّدَةِ. وَهِيَ تَعْتَمِدُ عَلَى سَرْدٍ قَصَصِيٍّ وَزَاوِيَةٍ فَرِيدَةٍ لِجَذْبِ الْقَارِئِ.`
    *   **Cohesion Breakdown:**
        *   `مِمَّا لَا شَكَّ فِيهِ، أَنَّ`: **Logical Connector** (emphasis/certainty, Day 196) – sets a strong opening.
        *   `هَذِهِ الْمَقَالَةَ الْمُمَيَّزَةَ تُقَدِّمُ تَعَمُّقًا فِي الْجَانِبِ الْإِنْسَانِيِّ`: Introduces the subject and its characteristic. `الْمَقَالَةَ الْمُمَيَّزَةَ` (**new for today**); `تُقَدِّمُ` (offers, Day 215); `تَعَمُّقًا` (**new for today**); `الْجَانِبِ الْإِنْسَانِيِّ` (**new for today**).
        *   `مُقَدِّمَةً نَظْرَةً شَامِلَةً لِتَدَاعِيَاتِهِ الْمُعَقَّدَةِ`: Active participle (`مُقَدِّمَةً` - offering, Day 215) describing a simultaneous action. `نَظْرَةً شَامِلَةً` (comprehensive outlook, Day 228); `تَدَاعِيَاتِهِ الْمُعَقَّدَةِ` (its complex repercussions, Day 233/170).
        *   `وَهِيَ تَعْتَمِدُ عَلَى سَرْدٍ قَصَصِيٍّ وَزَاوِيَةٍ فَرِيدَةٍ لِجَذْبِ الْقَارِئِ`: Connects with a conjunction `وَ` to describe further techniques. `تَعْتَمِدُ عَلَى` (relies on, Day 154); `سَرْدٍ قَصَصِيٍّ` (**new for today**); `زَاوِيَةٍ فَرِيدَةٍ` (**new for today**); `لِجَذْبِ الْقَارِئِ` (**new for today**).

2.  **"While some believe that formal language distorts public discourse and hinders reader engagement, others argue, in contrast, that employing narrative storytelling and a unique angle highlights deep shortcomings in policy methodology. This reflects a profound ethical dilemma in the destiny of language, often sparking widespread debate and requiring a firm stance."**
    *   `بَيْنَمَا يَرَى الْبَعْضُ أَنَّ اللُّغَةَ الرَّسْمِيَّةَ تُشَوِّهُ الْخِطَابَ الْعَامَّ وَتُعِيقُ جَذْبَ الْقَارِئِ، يُجَادِلُ آخَرُونَ بِالْمُقَابِلِ أَنَّ تَوْظِيفَ السَّرْدِ الْقَصَصِيِّ وَالزَّاوِيَةِ الْفَرِيدَةِ يُبْرِزُ قُصُورًا عَمِيقًا فِي مَنْهَجِيَّةِ السِّيَاسَةِ. وَهَذَا يَعْكِسُ مَأْزِقًا أَخْلَاقِيًّا عَمِيقًا فِي مَصِيرِ اللُّغَةِ، مُثِيرًا جَدَلًا وَاسِعًا كَثِيرًا مَا وَيَسْتَلْزِمُ مَوْقِفًا حَازِمًا.`
    *   **Cohesion Breakdown:**
        *   `بَيْنَمَا يَرَى الْبَعْضُ أَنَّ... يُجَادِلُ آخَرُونَ بِالْمُقَابِلِ أَنَّ`: **Logical Connector** (contrast, Day 146/186) – sets up opposing views.
        *   `اللُّغَةَ الرَّسْمِيَّةَ تُشَوِّهُ الْخِطَابَ الْعَامَّ وَتُعِيقُ جَذْبَ الْقَارِئِ`: First argument. `اللُّغَةَ الرَّسْمِيَّةَ` (formal language, Day 144/242 context); `تُشَوِّهُ` (distorts, Day 230 hint); `الْخِطَابَ الْعَامَّ` (public discourse, Day 225); `تُعِيقُ` (hinders, Day 230); `جَذْبَ الْقَارِئِ` (**new for today**).
        *   `تَوْظِيفَ السَّرْدِ الْقَصَصِيِّ وَالزَّاوِيَةِ الْفَرِيدَةِ يُبْرِزُ قُصُورًا عَمِيقًا فِي مَنْهَجِيَّةِ السِّيَاسَةِ`: Second argument. `تَوْظِيفَ` (employing, new Masdar Form II `وَظَّفَ` to employ/utilize); `السَّرْدِ الْقَصَصِيِّ` (**new for today**); `الزَّاوِيَةِ الْفَرِيدَةِ` (**new for today**); `يُبْرِزُ` (highlights, Day 233); `قُصُورًا عَمِيقًا` (deep shortcomings, Day 196/188); `مَنْهَجِيَّةِ السِّيَاسَةِ` (policy methodology, Day 236/146).
        *   `وَهَذَا يَعْكِسُ مَأْزِقًا أَخْلَاقِيًّا عَمِيقًا فِي مَصِيرِ اللُّغَةِ`: **Referencing Device** (`هَذَا` - this) refers to the entire problem. `يَعْكِسُ` (reflects, Day 218); `مَأْزِقًا أَخْلَاقِيًّا عَمِيقًا` (profound ethical dilemma, Day 189/188); `مَصِيرِ اللُّغَةِ` (destiny of language, Day 238/242).
        *   `مُثِيرًا جَدَلًا وَاسِعًا كَثِيرًا مَا وَيَسْتَلْزِمُ مَوْقِفًا حَازِمًا`: Active participle (`مُثِيرًا` - sparking, Day 235) describing a consequence. `جَدَلًا وَاسِعًا كَثِيرًا مَا` (often widespread debate, Day 235/139); `يَسْتَلْزِمُ مَوْقِفًا حَازِمًا` (requires a firm stance, Day 191/220).

3.  **"It is important to point out that feature writing, which embodies the principles of cultural enrichment, demands an in-depth study of societal needs and a clear methodology. This approach offers a comprehensive outlook on human values, marking a decisive turning point in the destiny of civilization, and is characterized by a unique angle for presenting vivid descriptions."**
    *   `مِنَ الْمُهِمِّ الْإِشَارَةُ إِلَى أَنَّ الْمَقَالَةَ الْمُمَيَّزَةَ، الَّتِي تُجَسِّدُ مَبَادِئَ الْإِثْرَاءِ الثَّقَافِيِّ، تَتَطَلَّبُ تَعَمُّقًا فِي الِاحْتِيَاجَاتِ الْمُجْتَمَعِيَّةِ وَمَنْهَجِيَّةً وَاضِحَةً. وَهَذَا النَّهْجُ يُقَدِّمُ نَظْرَةً شَامِلَةً لِلْقِيَمِ الْإِنْسَانِيَّةِ، مُشَكِّلًا مَحَطَّةً فَارِقَةً فِي مَصِيرِ الْحَضَارَةِ، وَيَتَّسِمُ بِزَاوِيَةٍ فَرِيدَةٍ لِتَقْدِيمِ وَصْفٍ حَيٍّ.`
    *   **Cohesion Breakdown:**
        *   `مِنَ الْمُهِمِّ الْإِشَارَةُ إِلَى أَنَّ`: **Contextual Framing** (introduces an important point, Day 156).
        *   `الْمَقَالَةَ الْمُمَيَّزَةَ، الَّتِي تُجَسِّدُ مَبَادِئَ الْإِثْرَاءِ الثَّقَافِيِّ، تَتَطَلَّبُ تَعَمُّقًا فِي الِاحْتِيَاجَاتِ الْمُجْتَمَعِيَّةِ وَمَنْهَجِيَّةً وَاضِحَةً`: Introduces feature writing and its requirements. `الْمَقَالَةَ الْمُمَيَّزَةَ` (**new for today**); `تُجَسِّدُ مَبَادِئَ الْإِثْرَاءِ الثَّقَافِيِّ` (embodies principles of cultural enrichment, Day 238/230/242); `تَتَطَلَّبُ تَعَمُّقًا فِي الِاحْتِيَاجَاتِ الْمُجْتَمَعِيَّةِ` (requires in-depth study of societal needs, Day 121 + **new for today** `تَعَمُّقٌ` + Day 220); `مَنْهَجِيَّةً وَاضِحَةً` (clear methodology, Day 236/105).
        *   `وَهَذَا النَّهْجُ يُقَدِّمُ نَظْرَةً شَامِلَةً لِلْقِيَمِ الْإِنْسَانِيَّةِ`: **Referencing Device** (`هَذَا النَّهْجُ` - this approach, Day 174) refers to the qualities of feature writing. `يُقَدِّمُ نَظْرَةً شَامِلَةً` (offers a comprehensive outlook, Day 215/228); `لِلْقِيَمِ الْإِنْسَانِيَّةِ` (on human values, Day 230/189).
        *   `مُشَكِّلًا مَحَطَّةً فَارِقَةً فِي مَصِيرِ الْحَضَارَةِ`: Active participle (`مُشَكِّلًا` - constituting, Day 191 context). `مَحَطَّةً فَارِقَةً` (decisive turning point, Day 233); `مَصِيرِ الْحَضَارَةِ` (destiny of civilization, Day 238).
        *   `وَيَتَّسِمُ بِزَاوِيَةٍ فَرِيدَةٍ لِتَقْدِيمِ وَصْفٍ حَيٍّ`: Further characteristics. `يَتَّسِمُ بِـ` (is characterized by, Day 223); `زَاوِيَةٍ فَرِيدَةٍ` (**new for today**); `تَقْدِيمِ وَصْفٍ حَيٍّ` (presenting vivid description, Day 215 `تَقْدِيمٌ` + **new for today** `وَصْفٍ حَيٍّ`).

## 📚 Core Vocabulary
*   `مَقَالَةٌ مُمَيَّزَةٌ` (maqālatun mumayyazatun) - Feature article/writing, distinguished article (feminine noun + adjective)
*   `مَقَالَاتٌ مُمَيَّزَةٌ` (maqālātun mumayyazatun) - Feature articles (feminine plural noun + adjective)
*   `يُمَيِّزُ` (yumayyizun) - He distinguishes, characterizes (present tense verb, Form II)
*   `سَرْدٌ قَصَصِيٌّ` (sardun qaṣaṣīyun) - Narrative storytelling (masculine Masdar + adjective)
*   `قَصَصِيٌّ` (qaṣaṣīyun) - Narrative, story-based (masculine adjective)
*   `تَعَمُّقٌ` (taʿammuqun) - In-depth study, deepening, delving (masculine Masdar, Form V)
*   `يَتَعَمَّقُ` (yataʿammuqu) - He delves deeply, studies in depth (present tense verb, Form V)
*   `جَانِبٌ إِنْسَانِيٌّ` (jānibun insānīyun) - Human interest aspect/side (masculine noun + adjective)
*   `وَصْفٌ حَيٌّ` (waṣfun ḥayyun) - Vivid description (masculine Masdar + adjective)
*   `زَاوِيَةٌ فَرِيدَةٌ` (zāwiyatun farīdatun) - Unique angle/perspective (feminine noun + adjective)
*   `جَذْبُ الْقَارِئِ` (jadb al-qāriʾi) - Attracting the reader, reader engagement (masculine Masdar + noun)
*   `جَذْبٌ` (jadbun) - Attraction (masculine Masdar, Form I)
*   `قَارِئٌ` (qāriʾun) - Reader (masculine noun)
*   `كَاتِبٌ` (kātibun) - Writer (masculine noun)
*   `تَوْظِيفٌ` (tawẓīfun) - Employing, utilization (masculine Masdar, Form II)
*   `تَبَنِّي` (tabannī) - Adopting, embracing (masculine Masdar, Form V)
*   `جَوَانِبُ` (jawānibu) - Aspects, sides (masculine plural, broken plural, diptote)

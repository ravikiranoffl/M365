# Day 160 - Advanced Journalism: Corporate reporting

## Lesson

"Advanced Corporate reporting" means we're going beyond the basic financial announcements and structural definitions to cover more detailed aspects of a company's performance, strategy, and external relations, especially with its owners (shareholders) and the official bodies that oversee its operations.

Think of it as adding specialized tools to your advanced Arabic toolkit, enabling you to articulate complex business narratives with even finer precision. You'll learn specific terms for deeper financial insights, strategic market positioning, and important aspects of corporate oversight.

Here are the key vocabulary and phrases you'll learn today:

### 1. Deeper Financial Metrics:

*   `صَافِي الْأَرْبَاحِ` (ṣāfī al-arbāḥi) - 'saa-fee al-ar-baah' (masculine noun phrase, Idafa)
    *   *Meaning:* Net profits.
    *   *Explanation:* This refers to the profit a company makes after all expenses, including taxes and interest, have been subtracted from its total revenues. You already know `أَرْبَاحٌ` (profits, Day 153); `صَافِي` means 'net' or 'pure'.
*   `صَافِي الْخَسَائِرِ` (ṣāfī al-khasā'iri) - 'saa-fee al-kha-saa-'ir' (masculine noun phrase, Idafa)
    *   *Meaning:* Net losses.
    *   *Explanation:* The opposite of net profits – the total losses after all revenues have been accounted for. You know `خَسَائِرُ` (losses, Day 153).
*   `قِيمَةُ السَّهْمِ` (qīmatu as-sahmi) - 'qee-ma-tu as-sahm' (feminine noun phrase, Idafa)
    *   *Meaning:* Share value.
    *   *Explanation:* This refers to the monetary worth of a single share of a company's stock. You know `سَهْمٌ` (share/stock, Day 153); `قِيمَةٌ` means 'value' or 'worth'.
*   `يُحَقِّقُ` (yuḥaqqiqu) - 'yu-ḥaq-qi-qu' (present tense verb, Form II)
    *   *Meaning:* He achieves / realizes (profits, goals).
    *   *Explanation:* This verb is commonly used to state that a company `يُحَقِّقُ أَرْبَاحًا` (realizes profits) or `يُحَقِّقُ أَهْدَافًا` (achieves goals, `أَهْدَافٌ` from Day 154). Its feminine form is `تُحَقِّقُ` (tuḥaqqiqu).

### 2. Corporate Strategy & Stakeholders:

*   `مَسَاهِمٌ` (musāhimun) - 'mu-saa-hi-mun' (active participle, masculine singular)
    *   *Meaning:* Shareholder.
    *   *Explanation:* An individual or institution that owns shares in a company. These are the owners of the company. Plural: `مُسَاهِمُونَ` (musāhimūna).
*   `ثِقَةٌ` (thiqatun) - 'thi-qa-tun' (feminine noun)
    *   *Meaning:* Confidence.
    *   *Explanation:* Often used in phrases like `ثِقَةُ الْمُسَاهِمِينَ` (shareholder confidence) or `ثِقَةُ السُّوقِ` (market confidence).
*   `حِصَّةٌ سُوقِيَّةٌ` (ḥiṣṣatun sūqiyyatun) - 'ḥiṣ-ṣa-tun soo-qee-ya-tun' (feminine noun + adjective)
    *   *Meaning:* Market share.
    *   *Explanation:* The portion of a market controlled by a particular company or product. You know `سُوقٌ` (market, Day 103/153) and `سُوقِيَّةٌ` (market-related, adjective). `حِصَّةٌ` means 'share' or 'quota'.
*   `ابْتِكَارٌ` (ibtikārun) - 'ib-ti-kaa-run' (masculine Masdar, Form VIII)
    *   *Meaning:* Innovation.
    *   *Explanation:* The process of introducing new ideas, methods, or products. This is a key driver for business growth and competitiveness.
    *   *Adjective:* `ابْتِكَارِيٌّ` (ibtikāriyyun) - innovative (masculine adjective).
*   `يُطْلِقُ` (yuṭliqu) - 'yuṭ-li-qu' (present tense verb, Form IV)
    *   *Meaning:* He launches / releases (a product, initiative).
    *   *Explanation:* Used for introducing new products, campaigns, or initiatives to the market. Feminine form: `تُطْلِقُ` (tuṭliqu).

### 3. Oversight & Responsibility:

*   `هَيْئَةٌ تَنْظِيمِيَّةٌ` (hay'atun tanẓīmiyyatun) - 'hay-'a-tun tan-ẓee-mee-ya-tun' (feminine noun + adjective)
    *   *Meaning:* Regulatory body / Regulatory authority.
    *   *Explanation:* An organization responsible for overseeing a specific industry or activity to ensure compliance with rules and laws. `هَيْئَةٌ` means 'body' or 'authority', and `تَنْظِيمِيَّةٌ` means 'regulatory' (from `تَنْظِيمٌ` - regulation/organization).
*   `الْتِزَامٌ` (iltizāmun) - 'il-ti-zaa-mun' (masculine Masdar, Form VIII)
    *   *Meaning:* Compliance / Commitment.
    *   *Explanation:* This refers to adhering to rules, standards, or obligations. You saw `الْتِزَامٌ بِـ` on Day 159; now we formally introduce `الْتِزَامٌ` itself.
*   `تَنَافُسِيَّةٌ` (tanāfusiyyatun) - 'ta-naa-fu-see-ya-tun' (feminine noun)
    *   *Meaning:* Competitiveness.
    *   *Explanation:* The ability of a company or product to compete effectively in the market.

Remember to seamlessly integrate these new terms with your existing advanced grammar, statistics vocabulary (Day 151/158), polling terms (Day 152/159), core corporate terms (Day 153), and especially with your comprehensive argumentation structures (Days 139, 146, 156). This will enable you to report on business news with exceptional depth and analytical rigor.

## Examples

Let's look at some examples that seamlessly integrate this new "advanced corporate reporting" vocabulary with other learned concepts. Carefully read each English sentence, then the Arabic translation, and pay close attention to the breakdown of how different elements are combined.

1.  **"It is certain that the company achieved big net profits this quarter. And the evidence for that is that the share value increased significantly compared to last year. Moreover, the statistics revealed that a majority of shareholders supports the current management."**
    *   `مِنَ الْمُؤَكَّدِ أَنَّ الشَّرِكَةَ حَقَّقَتْ صَافِيَ أَرْبَاحٍ كَبِيرَةٍ هَذَا الرُّبْعَ. وَالدَّلِيلُ عَلَى ذَلِكَ أَنَّ قِيمَةَ السَّهْمِ ارْتَفَعَتْ بِشَكْلٍ كَبِيرٍ مُقَارَنَةً بِالْعَامِ الْمَاضِي. عَلَاوَةً عَلَى ذَلِكَ، كَشَفَتِ الْإِحْصَاءَاتُ أَنَّ أَغْلَبِيَّةً مِنَ الْمُسَاهِمِينَ تُؤَيِّدُ الْإِدَارَةَ الْحَالِيَّةَ.`
    *   **Breakdown:**
        *   `مِنَ الْمُؤَكَّدِ أَنَّ` (It is certain that - Day 125).
        *   `الشَّرِكَةَ حَقَّقَتْ صَافِيَ أَرْبَاحٍ كَبِيرَةٍ هَذَا الرُّبْعَ` (the company achieved big net profits this quarter - `الشَّرِكَةَ` (company, Day 120) Accusative subject of `أَنَّ`; `حَقَّقَتْ` (she achieved - **new for today**); `صَافِيَ أَرْبَاحٍ` (**new for today**); `كَبِيرَةٍ` (big, Day 54); `هَذَا الرُّبْعَ` (this quarter, Day 153)).
        *   `وَالدَّلِيلُ عَلَى ذَلِكَ أَنَّ` (And the evidence for that is that - Day 132).
        *   `قِيمَةَ السَّهْمِ ارْتَفَعَتْ بِشَكْلٍ كَبِيرٍ مُقَارَنَةً بِالْعَامِ الْمَاضِي` (the share value increased significantly compared to last year - `قِيمَةَ السَّهْمِ` (**new for today**) Accusative subject of `أَنَّ`; `ارْتَفَعَتْ` (she increased, Day 115); `بِشَكْلٍ كَبِيرٍ` (significantly, Day 155); `مُقَارَنَةً بِالْعَامِ الْمَاضِي` (compared to last year, Day 158)).
        *   `عَلَاوَةً عَلَى ذَلِكَ` (Moreover - Day 107).
        *   `كَشَفَتِ الْإِحْصَاءَاتُ أَنَّ أَغْلَبِيَّةً مِنَ الْمُسَاهِمِينَ تُؤَيِّدُ الْإِدَارَةَ الْحَالِيَّةَ` (the statistics revealed that a majority of shareholders supports the current management - `كَشَفَتِ الْإِحْصَاءَاتُ` (statistics revealed, Day 151); `أَغْلَبِيَّةً مِنَ الْمُسَاهِمِينَ` (majority of shareholders - `أَغْلَبِيَّةٌ` Day 152, `مُسَاهِمِينَ` **new for today**) Accusative subject of `أَنَّ`; `تُؤَيِّدُ` (she supports, Day 152); `الْإِدَارَةَ الْحَالِيَّةَ` (current management, Day 153/144)).

2.  **"While some believe that the company's innovation will increase its market share, others point out that regulatory bodies require strict compliance with environmental laws. Therefore, new policies are needed for competitiveness."**
    *   `بَيْنَمَا يَرَى الْبَعْضُ أَنَّ ابْتِكَارَ الشَّرِكَةِ سَيُزِيدُ حِصَّتَهَا السُّوقِيَّةَ، يُشِيرُ آخَرُونَ إِلَى أَنَّ الْهَيْئَاتِ التَّنْظِيمِيَّةَ تَتَطَلَّبُ الْتِزَامًا شَدِيدًا بِالْقَوَانِينِ الْبِيئِيَّةِ. لِذَلِكَ، سِيَاسَاتٌ جَدِيدَةٌ مَطْلُوبَةٌ لِلتَّنَافُسِيَّةِ.`
    *   **Breakdown:**
        *   `بَيْنَمَا يَرَى الْبَعْضُ أَنَّ` (While some believe that - Day 146).
        *   `ابْتِكَارَ الشَّرِكَةِ سَيُزِيدُ حِصَّتَهَا السُّوقِيَّةَ` (the company's innovation will increase its market share - `ابْتِكَارَ` (**new for today**) Accusative subject of `أَنَّ`; `الشَّرِكَةِ` (Day 120) Genitive Idafa; `سَيُزِيدُ` (will increase, Day 115 from `زَادَ`); `حِصَّتَهَا السُّوقِيَّةَ` (**new for today**) Accusative object + pronoun).
        *   `يُشِيرُ آخَرُونَ إِلَى أَنَّ` (others point out that - Day 146).
        *   `الْهَيْئَاتِ التَّنْظِيمِيَّةَ تَتَطَلَّبُ الْتِزَامًا شَدِيدًا بِالْقَوَانِينِ الْبِيئِيَّةِ` (regulatory bodies require strict compliance with environmental laws - `الْهَيْئَاتِ التَّنْظِيمِيَّةَ` (plural of `هَيْئَةٌ تَنْظِيمِيَّةٌ` - **new for today**) Accusative subject of `أَنَّ`; `تَتَطَلَّبُ` (she requires, Day 113); `الْتِزَامًا شَدِيدًا` (**new for today** + Day 105 adjective) Accusative object; `بِالْقَوَانِينِ الْبِيئِيَّةِ` (with environmental laws, Day 102/124) Genitive).
        *   `لِذَلِكَ، سِيَاسَاتٌ جَدِيدَةٌ مَطْلُوبَةٌ لِلتَّنَافُسِيَّةِ` (Therefore, new policies are needed for competitiveness - `لِذَلِكَ` (Day 132); `سِيَاسَاتٌ جَدِيدَةٌ` (new policies, Day 146/54); `مَطْلُوبَةٌ` (needed, Day 146); `لِلتَّنَافُسِيَّةِ` (**new for today**) Genitive).

3.  **"Unfortunately, the annual report revealed net losses reaching approximately $1 million this quarter. Moreover, the forecasts indicate that the share value will be fluctuating in the coming period, which affects shareholder confidence."**
    *   `لِسُوءِ الْحَظِّ، كَشَفَ التَّقْرِيرُ السَّنَوِيُّ عَنْ صَافِي خَسَائِرَ بَلَغَتْ حَوَالَيْ 1 مِلْيُونِ دُولَارٍ هَذَا الرُّبْعَ. عَلَاوَةً عَلَى ذَلِكَ، تُشِيرُ التَّوَقُّعَاتُ إِلَى أَنَّ قِيمَةَ السَّهْمِ سَتَكُونُ مُتَذَبْذِبَةً فِي الْفَتْرَةِ الْقَادِمَةِ، مِمَّا يُؤَثِّرُ فِي ثِقَةِ الْمُسَاهِمِينَ.`
    *   **Breakdown:**
        *   `لِسُوءِ الْحَظِّ` (Unfortunately - Day 99).
        *   `كَشَفَ التَّقْرِيرُ السَّنَوِيُّ عَنْ صَافِي خَسَائِرَ بَلَغَتْ حَوَالَيْ 1 مِلْيُونِ دُولَارٍ هَذَا الرُّبْعَ` (the annual report revealed net losses reaching approximately $1 million this quarter - `كَشَفَ التَّقْرِيرُ السَّنَوِيُّ عَنْ` (annual report revealed about, Day 151/153); `صَافِي خَسَائِرَ` (**new for today**); `بَلَغَتْ حَوَالَيْ 1 مِلْيُونِ دُولَارٍ` (reached approximately 1 million dollars, Day 151/154); `هَذَا الرُّبْعَ` (this quarter, Day 153)).
        *   `عَلَاوَةً عَلَى ذَلِكَ` (Moreover - Day 107).
        *   `تُشِيرُ التَّوَقُّعَاتُ إِلَى أَنَّ قِيمَةَ السَّهْمِ سَتَكُونُ مُتَذَبْذِبَةً فِي الْفَتْرَةِ الْقَادِمَةِ` (forecasts indicate that the share value will be fluctuating in the coming period - `تُشِيرُ التَّوَقُّعَاتُ إِلَى أَنَّ` (forecasts indicate that, Day 158); `قِيمَةَ السَّهْمِ` (**new for today**) Accusative subject of `أَنَّ`; `سَتَكُونُ مُتَذَبْذِبَةً` (will be fluctuating, Day 159); `فِي الْفَتْرَةِ الْقَادِمَةِ` (in the coming period, Day 158)).
        *   `مِمَّا يُؤَثِّرُ فِي ثِقَةِ الْمُسَاهِمِينَ` (which affects shareholder confidence - `مِمَّا` (which, Day 155); `يُؤَثِّرُ فِي` (affects, Day 122); `ثِقَةِ الْمُسَاهِمِينَ` (**new for today**) Genitive Idafa).

4.  **"It is important to point out that the company will launch new innovative products next year. This reflects its commitment to market leadership and innovation, and is supported by shareholders."**
    *   `مِنَ الْمُهِمِّ الْإِشَارَةُ إِلَى أَنَّ الشَّرِكَةَ سَتُطْلِقُ مُنْتَجَاتٍ ابْتِكَارِيَّةٍ جَدِيدَةٍ الْعَامَ الْقَادِمَ. وَهَذَا يَعْكِسُ الْتِزَامَهَا بِقِيَادَةِ السُّوقِ وَالِابْتِكَارِ، وَيُؤَيِّدُهُ الْمُسَاهِمُونَ.`
    *   **Breakdown:**
        *   `مِنَ الْمُهِمِّ الْإِشَارَةُ إِلَى أَنَّ` (It is important to point out that - Day 156).
        *   `الشَّرِكَةَ سَتُطْلِقُ مُنْتَجَاتٍ ابْتِكَارِيَّةٍ جَدِيدَةٍ الْعَامَ الْقَادِمَ` (the company will launch new innovative products next year - `الشَّرِكَةَ` (Day 120) Accusative subject of `أَنَّ`; `سَتُطْلِقُ` (she will launch - **new for today**); `مُنْتَجَاتٍ ابْتِكَارِيَّةٍ جَدِيدَةٍ` (new innovative products - `مُنْتَجَاتٌ` (products, Day 144), `ابْتِكَارِيَّةٍ` (**new for today**), `جَدِيدَةٍ` (new, Day 54)) Accusative object; `الْعَامَ الْقَادِمَ` (next year, Day 110)).
        *   `وَهَذَا يَعْكِسُ الْتِزَامَهَا بِقِيَادَةِ السُّوقِ وَالِابْتِكَارِ` (This reflects its commitment to market leadership and innovation - `يَعْكِسُ` (he reflects, Day 159); `الْتِزَامَهَا` (her commitment, Day 159 + pronoun); `بِقِيَادَةِ السُّوقِ` (by market leadership, Day 153 `قِيَادَةٌ` - leadership); `الِابْتِكَارِ` (**new for today**) Genitive).
        *   `وَيُؤَيِّدُهُ الْمُسَاهِمُونَ` (and is supported by shareholders - `يُؤَيِّدُهُ` (he supports it, Day 152 + pronoun); `الْمُسَاهِمُونَ` (**new for today**) Nominative subject).

## 🗣️ Key Sentences
*   مِنَ الْمُؤَكَّدِ أَنَّ الشَّرِكَةَ حَقَّقَتْ صَافِيَ أَرْبَاحٍ كَبِيرَةٍ هَذَا الرُّبْعَ. وَالدَّلِيلُ عَلَى ذَلِكَ أَنَّ قِيمَةَ السَّهْمِ ارْتَفَعَتْ بِشَكْلٍ كَبِيرٍ مُقَارَنَةً بِالْعَامِ الْمَاضِي. عَلَاوَةً عَلَى ذَلِكَ، كَشَفَتِ الْإِحْصَاءَاتُ أَنَّ أَغْلَبِيَّةً مِنَ الْمُسَاهِمِينَ تُؤَيِّدُ الْإِدَارَةَ الْحَالِيَّةَ.
*   بَيْنَمَا يَرَى الْبَعْضُ أَنَّ ابْتِكَارَ الشَّرِكَةِ سَيُزِيدُ حِصَّتَهَا السُّوقِيَّةَ، يُشِيرُ آخَرُونَ إِلَى أَنَّ الْهَيْئَاتِ التَّنْظِيمِيَّةَ تَتَطَلَّبُ الْتِزَامًا شَدِيدًا بِالْقَوَانِينِ الْبِيئِيَّةِ. لِذَلِكَ، سِيَاسَاتٌ جَدِيدَةٌ مَطْلُوبَةٌ لِلتَّنَافُسِيَّةِ.
*   لِسُوءِ الْحَظِّ، كَشَفَ التَّقْرِيرُ السَّنَوِيُّ عَنْ صَافِي خَسَائِرَ بَلَغَتْ حَوَالَيْ 1 مِلْيُونِ دُولَارٍ هَذَا الرُّبْعَ. عَلَاوَةً عَلَى ذَلِكَ، تُشِيرُ التَّوَقُّعَاتُ إِلَى أَنَّ قِيمَةَ السَّهْمِ سَتَكُونُ مُتَذَبْذِبَةً فِي الْفَتْرَةِ الْقَادِمَةِ، مِمَّا يُؤَثِّرُ فِي ثِقَةِ الْمُسَاهِمِينَ.
*   مِنَ الْمُهِمِّ الْإِشَارَةُ إِلَى أَنَّ الشَّرِكَةَ سَتُطْلِقُ مُنْتَجَاتٍ ابْتِكَارِيَّةٍ جَدِيدَةٍ الْعَامَ الْقَادِمَ. وَهَذَا يَعْكِسُ الْتِزَامَهَا بِقِيَادَةِ السُّوقِ وَالِابْتِكَارِ، وَيُؤَيِّدُهُ الْمُسَاهِمُونَ.

# Day 209 - Media ethics terminology

## Lesson

"Media ethics terminology" at this advanced level is about providing you with even more precise terms needed to engage in sophisticated discussions about how ethical principles are *legally enforced*, *broadly applied*, and *institutionally overseen* within media organizations and the broader information ecosystem. This goes beyond understanding general principles to encompass specific forms of accountability, impact, and proactive measures to secure public confidence.

Think of it as having an even finer set of precision tools to dissect the ethical framework and conduct of media, focusing on the very fiber of professional integrity and the mechanisms for maintaining it. You'll learn specific terms for legal uprightness, pervasive influence, formal oversight, grave breaches of truth, and the proactive fostering of credibility.

Here are the key vocabulary and phrases you'll learn today:

### 1. Legal Dimensions & Professional Standards:

*   **`قَانُونِيٌّ`** (qānūnīyyun) - 'qaa-noo-niy-yun' (masculine adjective)
    *   *Meaning:* Legal.
    *   *Explanation:* This adjective relates to the law or is permitted by law. Its feminine form is `قَانُونِيَّةٌ` (qānūnīyyatun). You know `قَوَانِينُ` (laws, Day 201).
        *   *Example phrase:* `إِجْرَاءٌ قَانُونِيٌّ` (ijrāʾun qānūnīyyun) - 'ij-raa-ʾun qaa-noo-niy-yun' (masculine noun + adjective) - Legal procedure. (`إِجْرَاءٌ` - procedure, Day 170).
*   **`الْمَسْؤُولِيَّةُ الْقَانُونِيَّةُ`** (al-masʾūliyyatu al-qānūnīyyatu) - 'al-mas-ʾoo-liy-ya-tu al-qaa-noo-niy-ya-tu' (feminine noun phrase)
    *   *Meaning:* Legal responsibility.
    *   *Explanation:* This phrase combines `الْمَسْؤُولِيَّةُ` (responsibility, Day 199) and the new adjective `الْقَانُونِيَّةُ`. It denotes accountability under the law for one's actions or omissions.
        *   *Example:* `تَتَحَمَّلُ الْقَنَاةُ الْمَسْؤُولِيَّةَ الْقَانُونِيَّةَ.` (The channel bears legal responsibility.) (`تَتَحَمَّلُ` - she bears, Day 177).
*   **`مِعْيَارٌ`** (miʿyārun) - 'miʿ-yaar' (masculine noun)
    *   *Meaning:* Standard, criterion.
    *   *Explanation:* This is the singular noun for a principle by which something is judged. You have seen its plural `مَعَايِيرُ` (Day 190).
    *   *Plural:* **`مَعَايِيرُ`** (maʿāyīru) - 'ma-ʿaa-yīr' (feminine plural, broken plural, diptote).
*   **`الْمَعَايِيرُ الْمِهَنِّيَّةُ`** (al-maʿāyīru al-mihannīyyatu) - 'al-ma-ʿaa-yī-ru al-mi-han-niy-ya-tu' (feminine plural noun phrase + adjective)
    *   *Meaning:* Professional standards.
    *   *Explanation:* This phrase refers to the established benchmarks of quality and ethical conduct expected within a profession (e.g., journalism). It builds on `مِهَنِّيٌّ` (professional, Day 194).

### 2. Influence & Severe Ethical Breaches:

*   **`النَّفُوذُ`** (an-nafūḏu) - 'an-na-fooḏ' (masculine Masdar, Form I)
    *   *Meaning:* Influence, sway, penetration.
    *   *Explanation:* This term refers to the capacity to have an effect on the character, development, or behavior of someone or something, or the effect itself. It's often used when discussing the power of media or external forces.
        *   *Example:* `تَلْعَبُ وَسَائِلُ الْإِعْلَامِ دَوْرًا فِي تَاثِيرِ النَّفُوذِ.` (Media plays a role in influencing influence - *this example is a bit circular, let's use a simpler one*.)
        *   *Better example:* `لَدَيْهَا نَفُوذٌ قَوِيٌّ عَلَى الرَّأْيِ الْعَامِّ.` (It has a strong influence on public opinion.) (`الرَّأْيُ الْعَامُّ` - public opinion, Day 202 article example).
*   **`الافْتِرَاءُ`** (al-iftirāʾu) - 'al-if-ti-raa-ʾu' (masculine Masdar, Form VIII)
    *   *Meaning:* Slander, calumny, false accusation.
    *   *Explanation:* This refers to the act of making false and damaging statements about someone. It is a more specific and severe form of ethical breach than `تَشْهِيرٌ` (defamation, Day 189), which can be based on true but damaging information.
        *   *Example:* `يَجِبُ مُقَاوَمَةُ الافْتِرَاءِ فِي الْإِعْلَامِ.` (Slander must be resisted in the media.) (`مُقَاوَمَةٌ` - resistance, Day 175 context).

### 3. Oversight & Trust Building:

*   **`هَيْئَةٌ`** (hayʾatun) - 'hay-ʾa-tun' (feminine noun)
    *   *Meaning:* Body, authority, commission, entity.
    *   *Explanation:* This term denotes an official organization or group, often with a specific function.
    *   *Plural:* **`هَيْئَاتٌ`** (hayʾātun) - 'hay-ʾaat' (feminine plural noun).
*   **`رَقَابِيَّةٌ`** (raqābīyyatun) - 'ra-qaa-biy-ya-tun' (feminine adjective)
    *   *Meaning:* Regulatory, oversight, supervisory.
    *   *Explanation:* This adjective relates to supervision or regulation. It derives from `رَقَابَةٌ` (supervision/censorship, Day 165 context).
*   **`هَيْئَةٌ رَقَابِيَّةٌ`** (hayʾatun raqābīyyatun) - 'hay-ʾa-tun ra-qaa-biy-ya-tun' (feminine noun + adjective)
    *   *Meaning:* Regulatory body / oversight body.
    *   *Explanation:* This phrase describes an official organization responsible for supervising and enforcing rules or standards in a particular area, such as media.
        *   *Example:* `تُشْرِفُ الْهَيْئَةُ الرَّقَابِيَّةُ عَلَى أَخْلَاقِ الْإِعْلَامِ.` (The regulatory body supervises media ethics.) (`تُشْرِفُ عَلَى` - she supervises, Day 165 context).
*   **`بِنَاءُ الثِّقَةِ`** (bināʾu ath-thiqati) - 'bi-naa-ʾu ath-thi-qa-ti' (masculine Masdar phrase, Idafa)
    *   *Meaning:* Building trust.
    *   *Explanation:* This phrase combines `بِنَاءٌ` (building, Masdar Form I of `بَنَى` - to build, new Masdar for today) and `الثِّقَةِ` (trust, Day 160). It refers to the active process of establishing and strengthening credibility and confidence.

By integrating `الْمَسْؤُولِيَّةُ الْقَانُونِيَّةُ`, `الْمَعَايِيرُ الْمِهَنِّيَّةُ`, `الافْتِرَاءُ`, `النَّفُوذُ`, `هَيْئَةٌ رَقَابِيَّةٌ`, and `بِنَاءُ الثِّقَةِ` with your existing advanced vocabulary (including `اسْتِقَامَةٌ`, `مَوْثُوقِيَّةٌ`, `لَجْنَةٌ أَخْلَاقِيَّةٌ`, `تَصْحِيحٌ`, `ضُغُوطٌ`, `تَلَاعُبٌ بِالْحَقَائِقِ`), argumentation structures, and rhetorical phrases, you will be able to discuss and analyze media ethics with unparalleled clarity, depth, and the highest level of journalistic and academic sophistication.

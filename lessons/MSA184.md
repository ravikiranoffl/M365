# Day 184 - Media ethics terminology

## Lesson

"Media ethics terminology" is all about providing you with the precise terms needed to discuss the principles and practices that guide responsible journalism. This includes the high standards journalists strive for, as well as the common pitfalls and violations they must avoid.

Think of it as learning the specific language to discuss what makes good journalism truly good, and what makes bad journalism problematic. You'll learn specific terms for core values, types of misconduct, and the importance of being held responsible for one's reporting.

Here are the key vocabulary and phrases you'll learn today:

### 1. General Principles and Concepts:

*   **`أَخْلَاقُ الْإِعْلَامِ`** (akhlāqu al-iʿlām) - 'akh-laa-qu al-iʿ-laam' (feminine plural noun phrase, Idafa)
    *   *Meaning:* Media ethics.
    *   *Explanation:* `أَخْلَاقٌ` (akhlāqun) means 'ethics' or 'morals' (you encountered `أَخْلَاقِيَّةٌ` - ethical, Day 174). `الْإِعْلَامِ` (al-iʿlām) means 'media' (from Day 136). This is the overarching term for today's topic.
*   **`مَوْضُوعِيَّةٌ`** (mawḍūʿiyyatun) - 'maw-ḍoo-ʿiy-ya-tun' (feminine noun)
    *   *Meaning:* Objectivity.
    *   *Explanation:* This principle in journalism means presenting facts and issues in a neutral and unbiased manner, without personal feelings or interpretations.
*   **`حِيَادٌ`** (ḥiyādun) - 'ḥi-yaad' (masculine Masdar)
    *   *Meaning:* Neutrality, impartiality.
    *   *Explanation:* Similar to objectivity, this refers to not taking sides in a conflict or issue, presenting all viewpoints fairly. It is a Masdar.

### 2. Violations and Breaches of Ethics:

*   **`تَحَيُّزٌ`** (taḥayyun) - 'ta-ḥay-yuz' (masculine Masdar, Form V)
    *   *Meaning:* Bias, partiality.
    *   *Explanation:* The opposite of neutrality, this describes leaning towards one side or point of view, consciously or unconsciously.
    *   *Verb form:* `يَتَحَيَّزُ لِـ / ضِدَّ` (yataḥayyazu li-/ḍidda) - He is biased towards/against (present tense verb + preposition).
*   **`تَضْلِيلٌ`** (taḍlīlun) - 'taḍ-lee-lun' (masculine Masdar, Form II)
    *   *Meaning:* Misleading, deception.
    *   *Explanation:* This refers to intentionally giving false or incomplete information to deceive the audience. You saw `مُضَلِّلَةٌ` (misleading, active participle) on Day 159 context.
*   **`انْتِهَاكٌ`** (intihākun) - 'in-ti-haa-kun' (masculine Masdar, Form VIII)
    *   *Meaning:* Violation, infringement.
    *   *Explanation:* A general term for breaking a rule, law, or ethical standard. It's often used with `حُقُوقٌ` (rights, Day 126) or `خُصُوصِيَّةٌ` (privacy, below).
    *   *Verb form:* `يَنْتَهِكُ` (yantahiku) - He violates (present tense verb).
*   **`سَرِقَةٌ أَدَبِيَّةٌ`** (sariqatun adabiyyatun) - 'sa-ri-qa-tun a-da-biy-ya-tun' (feminine noun + adjective)
    *   *Meaning:* Plagiarism.
    *   *Explanation:* `سَرِقَةٌ` (sariqatun) means 'theft'. `أَدَبِيَّةٌ` (adabiyyatun) is an adjective meaning 'literary' (related to `أَدَبٌ` - literature, Day 136 context). This refers to presenting someone else's work or ideas as your own.
*   **`إِثَارَةٌ`** (ithāratun) - 'i-thaa-ra-tun' (feminine Masdar, Form IV)
    *   *Meaning:* Sensationalism, provocation.
    *   *Explanation:* This describes the practice of presenting news in a way that is intended to provoke public interest or excitement, often at the expense of accuracy or ethical considerations. It comes from the verb `أَثَارَ` (to provoke, stir up).

### 3. Responsibility and Protected Values:

*   **`مُحَاسَبَةٌ`** (muḥāsabatun) - 'mu-ḥaa-sa-ba-tun' (feminine Masdar, Form III)
    *   *Meaning:* Accountability.
    *   *Explanation:* This refers to the obligation to accept responsibility for one's actions and to be transparent about them.
*   **`خُصُوصِيَّةٌ`** (khuṣūṣiyyatun) - 'khu-ṣoo-ṣiy-ya-tun' (feminine noun)
    *   *Meaning:* Privacy.
    *   *Explanation:* The right of individuals to keep their personal information and affairs from public scrutiny. This is a key ethical concern in reporting.

By integrating `أَخْلَاقُ الْإِعْلَامِ`, `مَوْضُوعِيَّةٌ`, `حِيَادٌ`, `تَحَيُّزٌ`, `تَضْلِيلٌ`, `انْتِهَاكٌ`, `سَرِقَةٌ أَدَبِيَّةٌ`, `إِثَارَةٌ`, `مُحَاسَبَةٌ`, and `خُصُوصِيَّةٌ` with your existing advanced vocabulary, argumentation structures, and rhetorical phrases, you will be able to discuss and analyze media ethics with exceptional clarity and depth.

## 📚 Core Vocabulary
*   أَخْلَاقُ الْإِعْلَامِ (akhlāqu al-iʿlām) - Media ethics (feminine plural noun phrase, Idafa)
*   مَوْضُوعِيَّةٌ (mawḍūʿiyyatun) - Objectivity (feminine noun)
*   حِيَادٌ (ḥiyādun) - Neutrality, impartiality (masculine Masdar)
*   تَحَيُّزٌ (taḥayyun) - Bias, partiality (masculine Masdar, Form V)
*   يَتَحَيَّزُ لِـ / ضِدَّ (yataḥayyazu li-/ḍidda) - He is biased towards/against (present tense verb + preposition, Form V)
*   تَضْلِيلٌ (taḍlīlun) - Misleading, deception (masculine Masdar, Form II)
*   انْتِهَاكٌ (intihākun) - Violation, infringement (masculine Masdar, Form VIII)
*   يَنْتَهِكُ (yantahiku) - He violates (present tense verb, Form VIII)
*   سَرِقَةٌ أَدَبِيَّةٌ (sariqatun adabiyyatun) - Plagiarism (feminine noun + adjective)
*   إِثَارَةٌ (ithāratun) - Sensationalism, provocation (feminine Masdar, Form IV)
*   مُحَاسَبَةٌ (muḥāsabatun) - Accountability (feminine Masdar, Form III)
*   خُصُوصِيَّةٌ (khuṣūṣiyyatun) - Privacy (feminine noun)
*   صَحَفِيَّةٌ (ṣaḥafiyyatun) - Journalistic (feminine adjective)
*   مُنَصَّاتٌ (munaṣṣātun) - Platforms (feminine plural noun)
*   قَانُونِيَّةٌ (qānūniyyatun) - Legal (feminine adjective)
*   تَغْطِيَةٌ إِخْبَارِيَّةٌ (taghṭiyatun ikhbāriyyatun) - News coverage (feminine noun phrase, Idafa)
*   حُرِّيَّةُ التَّعْبِيرِ (ḥurriyatu at-taʿbīr) - Freedom of expression (feminine noun phrase, Idafa)
*   فَرْدِيَّةٌ (fardiyyatun) - Individual (feminine adjective)
*   انْتِشَارٌ (intishārun) - Spread (masculine Masdar, Form VIII)
*   أَطْفَالٌ (aṭfālun) - Children (masculine plural noun, broken plural)
*   بَاحِثُونَ أَكَادِيمِيُّونَ (bāḥithūna akādīmiyyūna) - Academic researchers (masculine sound plural noun phrase)
*   وُجُودُ (wujūdun) - Presence (masculine Masdar)
*   تَشْوِيهُ الْحَقَائِقِ (tashwīhu al-ḥaqāʾiqi) - Distortion of facts (masculine Masdar phrase, Idafa)

## 🗣️ Key Sentences
*   فِي سِيَاقِ أَخْلَاقِ الْإِعْلَامِ، تُبْرِزُ الْمُنَاقَشَةُ حَوْلَ الْمَوْضُوعِيَّةِ الصَّحَفِيَّةِ نُقْطَةً خِلَافِيَّةً خَطِيرَةً فِيمَا يَتَعَلَّقُ بِالتَّحَيُّزِ فِي التَّقَارِيرِ. وَهَذَا يُؤَدِّي إِلَى نَقْصٍ فِي ثِقَةِ الْجُمْهُورِ.
*   بَيْنَمَا يَرَى الْبَعْضُ أَنَّ نَشْرَ النَّتَائِجِ الْأَوْلِيَّةِ ضَرُورِيٌّ، يُجَادِلُ آخَرُونَ مِنْ زَاوِيَةٍ أُخْرَى بِأَنَّ الِاسْتِخْدَامَ الْمُتَوَاصِلَ لِلْإِثَارَةِ وَالْمَعْلُومَاتِ الْمُضَلِّلَةِ يَنْتَهِكُ الْحِيَادَ الصَّحَفِيَّ. وَهَذَا يَتَطَلَّبُ مُحَاسَبَةً أَقْوَى.
*   لِسُوءِ الْحَظِّ، تُؤَكِّدُ الدِّرَاسَةُ الْأَكَادِيمِيَّةُ الْأَخِيرَةُ عَلَى أَنَّ انْتِهَاكَ الْخُصُوصِيَّةِ فِي مُنَصَّاتِ الْإِعْلَامِ الرَّقْمِيِّ لَهُ تَدَاعِيَاتٌ قَانُونِيَّةٌ وَاجْتِمَاعِيَّةٌ خَطِيرَةٌ. وَهَذَا يُلْقِي الضَّوْءَ عَلَى الْحَاجَةِ الْمُلِحَّةِ لِتَوْصِيَاتٍ لِمُعَالَجَةِ السَّرِقَةِ الْأَدَبِيَّةِ وَلِضَمَانِ أَخْلَاقِ الصِّحَافَةِ الرَّقْمِيَّةِ.

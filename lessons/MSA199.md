# Day 199 - Media ethics terminology

## Lesson

"Media ethics terminology" at this level builds on your previous extensive knowledge, enabling you to discuss even more refined aspects of media's role in society, the depth of audience trust, and the nuances of ethical adherence. We'll explore how media impacts society beyond just reporting, how subtle biases operate, and what constitutes a strong professional response to ethical challenges.

Think of it as adding layers of responsibility and insight to your journalistic Arabic. You'll learn specific terms for the broader societal implications of media practices, the subtleties of impartiality, and the collective ethical stance of professionals.

Here are the key vocabulary and phrases you'll learn today:

### 1. Broader Societal Role and Trust:

*   **`مَسْؤُولِيَّةٌ اجْتِمَاعِيَّةٌ`** (masʾūliyyatun ijtimaʿiyyatun) - 'mas-ʾoo-liy-ya-tun ij-ti-maa-'iy-ya-tun' (feminine noun + adjective)
    *   *Meaning:* Social responsibility.
    *   *Explanation:* You know `مَسْؤُولِيَّةٌ` (responsibility, Day 147 context) and `اجْتِمَاعِيَّةٌ` (social, Day 106). This term specifically refers to the ethical obligation of media organizations to act in ways that benefit society as a whole, beyond just their business interests.
*   **`ثِقَةُ الْمُشَاهِدِينَ`** (thiqatu al-mushāhidīna) - 'thi-qa-tu al-mu-shaa-hi-dee-na' (feminine noun phrase, Idafa)
    *   *Meaning:* Viewer trust.
    *   *Explanation:* `ثِقَةٌ` (thiqatun) means 'trust' (Day 160). `الْمُشَاهِدِينَ` (al-mushāhidīna) means 'viewers' (plural, Day 176). This specifically focuses on the trust placed by television or online video audiences in media.
*   **`ثِقَةُ الْقُرَّاءِ`** (thiqatu al-qurrāʾi) - 'thi-qa-tu al-qur-raa-ʾi' (feminine noun phrase, Idafa)
    *   *Meaning:* Reader trust.
    *   *Explanation:* `الْقُرَّاءِ` (al-qurrāʾi) means 'readers' (plural of `قَارِئٌ` (qāriʾun) - reader, new noun). This term refers to the trust placed by newspaper or online text audiences.

### 2. Nuances of Bias and Professional Response:

*   **`الِانْحِيَازُ`** (al-inḥiyāzu) - 'al-in-ḥi-yāz' (masculine Masdar, Form VII)
    *   *Meaning:* Bias, partiality (often implying a deeper, perhaps less conscious, lean).
    *   *Explanation:* While `تَحَيُّزٌ` (Form V, Day 184) refers to bias generally, `الِانْحِيَازُ` (Form VII, from `انْحَازَ` - to lean, be biased) can sometimes carry a stronger nuance, implying a systemic or ingrained partiality.
    *   *Example phrase:* **`الِانْحِيَازُ الْخَفِيُّ`** (al-inḥiyāzu al-khafiyyu) - 'al-in-ḥi-yā-zu al-kha-fiy-yu' (masculine Masdar + adjective) - Hidden bias.
        *   *Explanation:* `خَفِيٌّ` (khafiyyun) is an adjective meaning 'hidden' or 'subtle' (new for today). This phrase refers to a subtle or unconscious bias that may not be immediately obvious.
*   **`التَّدَاوُلُ`** (at-tadāwulu) - 'at-ta-daa-wu-lu' (masculine Masdar, Form VI)
    *   *Meaning:* Circulation, exchange (of information).
    *   *Explanation:* This term (from `تَدَاوَلَ` - to circulate, exchange) refers to the widespread dissemination and exchange of information. Ethical concerns often arise regarding the responsible `التَّدَاوُلُ` of content.
*   **`تَضَامُنٌ مِهَنِّيٌّ`** (taḍāmunun mihanniyyun) - 'ta-ḍaa-mu-nun mi-han-niy-yun' (masculine Masdar + adjective)
    *   *Meaning:* Professional solidarity.
    *   *Explanation:* You know `تَضَامُنٌ` (solidarity, Day 195) and `مِهَنِّيٌّ` (professional, Day 194). This emphasizes the collective support and unity among professionals in upholding ethical standards and resisting undue pressure.

### 3. Binding Ethics and Specific Deception:

*   **`مُلْزِمٌ`** (mulzimun) - 'mul-zi-mun' (masculine active participle, Form IV)
    *   *Meaning:* Binding, obligatory.
    *   *Explanation:* This adjective (from `أَلْزَمَ` - to obligate, compel) is used to describe a rule, standard, or agreement that must be followed. Its feminine form is `مُلْزِمَةٌ` (mulzimatun).
        *   *Example phrase:* **`مَوَاثِيقُ شَرَفٍ مُلْزِمَةٌ`** (mawāthīqu sharafin mulzimatun) - 'ma-waa-thee-qu sha-ra-fin mul-zi-ma-tun' - Binding codes of ethics.
*   **`تَضْلِيلٌ مُتَعَمَّدٌ`** (taḍlīlun mutaʿammaddun) - 'taḍ-lee-lun mu-ta-'am-mad-dun' (masculine Masdar + adjective)
    *   *Meaning:* Intentional deception / deliberate misleading.
    *   *Explanation:* You know `تَضْلِيلٌ` (misleading, Day 184). `مُتَعَمَّدٌ` (mutaʿammaddun) is a passive participle meaning 'intentional' or 'deliberate' (new for today, from `تَعَمَّدَ` - to intend). This phrase stresses the deliberate nature of misleading information, distinguishing it from accidental errors.

By integrating these new terms with your extensive previous vocabulary and structures, you will be able to discuss and analyze media ethics with unparalleled clarity, depth, and the highest level of journalistic and academic sophistication.

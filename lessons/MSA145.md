# Day 145 - Fluency: Environment reporting

## Lesson

"Fluency: Environment reporting" today is all about integrated application and advanced synthesis. You already possess a strong vocabulary for discussing `بِيئَةٌ` (environment), `تَلَوُّثٌ` (pollution), `مُنَاخٌ` (climate), `حِمَايَةٌ` (protection), `مُلَوَّثٌ` (polluted), `نَظِيفٌ` (clean), `مَوَارِدُ` (resources), `مَاءٌ` (water), `هَوَاءٌ` (air), `أَرْضٌ` (Earth), and `تَقْلِيلٌ` (reduction). The goal now is to use these terms within even more complex sentences, connecting environmental ideas to political, economic, scientific, or cultural contexts, and employing your full range of advanced connectors, stance markers, and argumentation phrases.

Think of yourself as a seasoned analyst, capable of crafting insightful reports or discussions where you seamlessly blend environmental observations with analytical points, drawing deep connections between different aspects of a society.

To achieve this high level of fluency, remember to consistently apply and integrate:

    *   `بِيئَةٌ` (environment)
    *   `تَلَوُّثٌ` (pollution)
    *   `مُنَاخٌ` (climate)
    *   `يَحْمِي` / `تَحْمِي` (he/she protects)
    *   `حِمَايَةٌ` (protection)
    *   `مُلَوَّثٌ` / `مُلَوَّثَةٌ` (polluted)
    *   `نَظِيفٌ` / `نَظِيفَةٌ` (clean)
    *   `مَوَارِدُ` (resources)
    *   `مَاءٌ` (water)
    *   `هَوَاءٌ` (air)
    *   `أَرْضٌ` (earth, land)
    *   `تَقْلِيلٌ` (reduction, decrease)
    *   `تَنْمِيَةٌ مُسْتَدَامَةٌ` (sustainable development)

2.  **Your `Argumentation Structures` (Days 125, 132 & 139):** Use these to make claims, provide evidence, acknowledge contrasts, and draw conclusions.
    *   `مِنَ الْمُؤَكَّدِ أَنَّ` (It is certain that...)
    *   `مِنَ الْمَعْرُوفِ أَنَّ` (It is known that...)
    *   `لَا شَكَّ أَنَّ` (There is no doubt that...)
    *   `يَعْتَقِدُ ... أَنَّ` (... believes that...)
    *   `يَبْدُو أَنَّ` (It seems that...)
    *   `مِنَ الْوَاضِحِ أَنَّ` (It is clear that...)
    *   `لَا يُنْكَرُ أَنَّ` (It cannot be denied that...)
    *   `وَالدَّلِيلُ عَلَى ذَلِكَ أَنَّ` (And the evidence for that is that...)
    *   `عَلَى سَبِيلِ الْمِثَالِ` (For example...)
    *   `وَبِالْعَكْسِ` (And on the contrary...)
    *   `لِسُوءِ الْحَظِّ` (Unfortunately...)

3.  **Your `Advanced Connectors` (Days 107-108, and reinforced often):** Ensure smooth transitions and logical relationships between ideas.
    *   `عَلَاوَةً عَلَى ذَلِكَ` (Moreover...)

    *   `وَبِنَاءً عَلَى ذَلِكَ` (And accordingly...)

    *   `وَفِي الْوَقْتِ نَفْسِهِ` (At the same time...)
    *   `لِذَلِكَ` (Therefore...)

4.  **Impeccable `Grammatical Accuracy` (Days 118-119, and reinforced often):** Pay meticulous attention to noun case endings, adjective agreement (especially with non-human plurals and diptotes), verb-subject agreement, Idafa constructions, `أَنَّ` vs `أَنْ`, and proper conjugation.

## Examples

Let's look at some examples that seamlessly integrate environment reporting with other learned concepts. Carefully read each English sentence, then the Arabic translation, and pay close attention to the breakdown of how different elements are combined.

1.  **"It cannot be denied that environmental pollution is a global challenge. And the evidence for that is that it affects economic growth negatively. Therefore, governments should undertake new plans for environmental protection."**
    *   `لَا يُنْكَرُ أَنَّ التَّلَوُّثَ الْبِيئِيَّ تَحَدٍّ عَالَمِيٌّ. وَالدَّلِيلُ عَلَى ذَلِكَ أَنَّهُ يُؤَثِّرُ فِي النُّمُوِّ الِاقْتِصَادِيِّ بِشَكْلٍ سَلْبِيٍّ. لِذَلِكَ، يَنْبَغِي عَلَى الْحُكُومَاتِ أَنْ تَقُومَ بِخُطَطٍ جَدِيدَةٍ لِحِمَايَةِ الْبِيئَةِ.`
    *   **Breakdown:**
        *   `لَا يُنْكَرُ أَنَّ` (It cannot be denied that - Day 132).
        *   `التَّلَوُّثَ الْبِيئِيَّ تَحَدٍّ عَالَمِيٌّ` (environmental pollution is a global challenge - `التَّلَوُّثَ الْبِيئِيَّ` (environmental pollution, Day 137 breakdown, Day 124) Accusative subject of `أَنَّ`; `تَحَدٍّ عَالَمِيٌّ` (global challenge, Day 105/106) Nominative predicate).
        *   `وَالدَّلِيلُ عَلَى ذَلِكَ أَنَّ` (And the evidence for that is that - Day 132).
        *   `يُؤَثِّرُ فِي النُّمُوِّ الِاقْتِصَادِيِّ بِشَكْلٍ سَلْبِيٍّ` (it affects economic growth negatively - `يُؤَثِّرُ فِي` (he affects, Day 122); `النُّمُوِّ الِاقْتِصَادِيِّ` (economic growth, Day 103/115) Genitive after `فِي`; `بِشَكْلٍ سَلْبِيٍّ` (in a negative way, Day 131) adverbial phrase).
        *   `لِذَلِكَ، يَنْبَغِي عَلَى الْحُكُومَاتِ أَنْ تَقُومَ بِخُطَطٍ جَدِيدَةٍ لِحِمَايَةِ الْبِيئَةِ` (Therefore, governments should undertake new plans for environmental protection - `لِذَلِكَ` (Day 132); `يَنْبَغِي عَلَى ... أَنْ تَقُومَ بِـ` (it is necessary that... undertake, Day 125/97); `الْحُكُومَاتِ` (governments, Day 137) Nominative subject; `خُطَطٍ جَدِيدَةٍ` (new plans, Day 113/54) Genitive after `بِـ`; `لِحِمَايَةِ الْبِيئَةِ` (for environmental protection, Day 124) Idafa).

2.  **"Despite the fact that scientists are working to find solutions for climate change, some companies, on the contrary, continue to increase pollution. In light of that, it seems that public awareness needs to increase."**
    *   `بِالرَّغْمِ مِنْ أَنَّ الْعُلَمَاءَ يَعْمَلُونَ عَلَى إِيجَادِ حُلُولٍ لِتَغَيُّرِ الْمُنَاخِ، فَإِنَّ بَعْضَ الشَّرِكَاتِ، وَبِالْعَكْسِ، تُوَاصِلُ زِيَادَةَ التَّلَوُّثِ. فِي ضَوْءِ ذَلِكَ، يَبْدُو أَنَّ الْوَعْيَ الْعَامَّ يَنْبَغِي أَنْ يَزْدَادَ.`
    *   **Breakdown:**
        *   `بِالرَّغْمِ مِنْ أَنَّ` (Despite the fact that - Day 107).
        *   `الْعُلَمَاءَ يَعْمَلُونَ عَلَى إِيجَادِ حُلُولٍ لِتَغَيُّرِ الْمُنَاخِ` (scientists are working to find solutions for climate change - `الْعُلَمَاءَ` (scholars, Day 123) Accusative subject of `أَنَّ`; `يَعْمَلُونَ عَلَى إِيجَادِ` (they are working on finding, Day 116/131 Masdar); `حُلُولٍ` (solutions, Day 105) Genitive; `لِتَغَيُّرِ الْمُنَاخِ` (for climate change, Day 124) Idafa).
        *   `فَإِنَّ بَعْضَ الشَّرِكَاتِ، وَبِالْعَكْسِ، تُوَاصِلُ زِيَادَةَ التَّلَوُّثِ` (some companies, on the contrary, continue to increase pollution - `فَإِنَّ` (indeed, Day 139); `بَعْضَ الشَّرِكَاتِ` (some companies, Day 130/120) Accusative subject of `أَنَّ`; `وَبِالْعَكْسِ` (on the contrary, Day 139); `تُوَاصِلُ` (she continues, new for this context, but from `وَاصل`); `زِيَادَةَ التَّلَوُّثِ` (increase of pollution, Day 115 Masdar/124) Accusative object Idafa).
        *   `فِي ضَوْءِ ذَلِكَ، يَبْدُو أَنَّ الْوَعْيَ الْعَامَّ يَنْبَغِي أَنْ يَزْدَادَ` (In light of that, it seems that public awareness needs to increase - `فِي ضَوْءِ ذَلِكَ` (Day 108); `يَبْدُو أَنَّ` (it seems that, Day 125); `الْوَعْيَ الْعَامَّ` (public awareness, Day 144/106) Accusative subject of `أَنَّ`; `يَنْبَغِي أَنْ يَزْدَادَ` (it is necessary that it increases, Day 125/115 Subjunctive)).

3.  **"Unfortunately, the polluted environment affects cultural traditions, for example traditional festivals, in a negative way. On the other hand, the Ministry of Culture is working to protect these traditions."**
    *   `لِسُوءِ الْحَظِّ، تُؤَثِّرُ الْبِيئَةُ الْمُلَوَّثَةُ فِي التَّقَالِيدِ الثَّقَافِيَّةِ، عَلَى سَبِيلِ الْمِثَالِ الْمُهْرَجَانَاتِ التَّقْلِيدِيَّةِ، بِشَكْلٍ سَلْبِيٍّ. مِنْ جِهَةٍ أُخْرَى، تَعْمَلُ وِزَارَةُ الثَّقَافَةِ عَلَى حِمَايَةِ هَذِهِ التَّقَالِيدِ.`
    *   **Breakdown:**
        *   `لِسُوءِ الْحَظِّ` (Unfortunately - Day 99).
        *   `تُؤَثِّرُ الْبِيئَةُ الْمُلَوَّثَةُ فِي التَّقَالِيدِ الثَّقَافِيَّةِ بِشَكْلٍ سَلْبِيٍّ` (the polluted environment affects cultural traditions in a negative way - `تُؤَثِّرُ فِي` (she affects, Day 122); `الْبِيئَةُ الْمُلَوَّثَةُ` (polluted environment, Day 124) Nominative subject; `التَّقَالِيدِ الثَّقَافِيَّةِ` (cultural traditions, Day 122) Genitive after `فِي`; `بِشَكْلٍ سَلْبِيٍّ` (in a negative way, Day 131) adverbial phrase).
        *   `عَلَى سَبِيلِ الْمِثَالِ الْمُهْرَجَانَاتِ التَّقْلِيدِيَّةِ` (for example traditional festivals - `عَلَى سَبِيلِ الْمِثَالِ` (Day 139); `الْمُهْرَجَانَاتِ التَّقْلِيدِيَّةِ` (traditional festivals, Day 136) Genitive apposition).
        *   `مِنْ جِهَةٍ أُخْرَى` (On the other hand - Day 108).
        *   `تَعْمَلُ وِزَارَةُ الثَّقَافَةِ عَلَى حِمَايَةِ هَذِهِ التَّقَالِيدِ` (the Ministry of Culture is working to protect these traditions - `تَعْمَلُ عَلَى` (Day 116); `وِزَارَةُ الثَّقَافَةِ` (Day 129) Nominative subject Idafa; `حِمَايَةِ` (protection, Day 124) Genitive Masdar; `هَذِهِ التَّقَالِيدِ` (these traditions, Day 19/122) Genitive).

## 📚 Core Vocabulary

*   الْمُدُنِ الْكَبِيرَةِ (al-muduni al-kabeerati) - big cities (feminine plural noun + adjective, `مُدُنٌ` broken plural of `مَدِينَةٌ` - city)
*   كَافِيَةٌ (kāfiyatun) - sufficient (feminine adjective)
*   أَمْنٌ غِذَائِيٌّ (amnun ghidhā'iyyun) - food security (masculine noun + adjective)
*   الْأَطْفَالُ (al-aṭfāl) - the children (masculine plural noun, broken plural of `طِفْلٌ` - child)
*   مَخَاطِرُ بِيئِيَّةٌ (makhāṭiru bee'iyyatun) - environmental dangers (feminine plural noun + adjective, `مَخَاطِرُ` broken plural of `خَطَرٌ` - danger, diptote)
*   خِطَابٌ (khiṭābun) - speech (masculine noun)
*   يَرْفَعُ الْوَعْيَ بِـ (yarfa'u al-wa'ya bi-) - he raises awareness about (verb + Masdar + preposition)

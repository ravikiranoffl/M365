# Day 256 - Irregular verb mastery

## Lesson

"Irregular verb mastery" at this advanced stage means applying your understanding of weak letters (حُرُوفُ الْعِلَّةِ) and irregular verb categories to more complex verb forms. We'll now examine how Form IV (أَفْعَلَ) and Form V (تَفَعَّلَ) verbs behave when their roots contain weak letters, with detailed conjugation examples for `هُوَ` (he), `هِيَ` (she/it), and `أَنَا` (I).

Recall the **weak letters** (حُرُوفُ الْعِلَّةِ - 'ḥurūfu al-ʿillah'): `أَلِفٌ` (`ا`/`ى`), `وَاوٌ` (`و`), and `يَاءٌ` (`ي`). A **sound verb** (`فِعْلٌ صَحِيحٌ`) has no weak letters, while an **irregular verb** (`فِعْلٌ مُعْتَلٌّ`) has one or more.

### 1. Form IV Irregular Verbs (الفِعْلُ الْمُعْتَلُّ فِي الْبِنَاءِ الرَّابِعِ)
Form IV (`أَفْعَلَ` - afʿala) often indicates causation, meaning "to make [someone/something] do [Form I action]."

*   **a. Assimilated Form IV (مِثَالٌ فِي الْبِنَاءِ الرَّابِعِ)**
    *   **Definition:** First root letter is weak (`و` or `ي`). Like Forms II and III, the initial `و` **does not drop** in Form IV.
    *   **Example Verb:** `أَوْصَلَ` (awṣala) - 'aw-ṣa-la' (He delivered/caused to arrive).
        *   **Root:** و ص ل (w-ṣ-l) (Form I: `وَصَلَ` - he arrived; Form II: `وَصَّلَ` - he delivered)
        *   **Past Tense (الْمَاضِي):**
            *   `هُوَ أَوْصَلَ` (huwa awṣala) - He delivered. (Weak letter `و` is present).
            *   `هِيَ أَوْصَلَتْ` (hiya awṣalat) - She delivered. (Weak letter `و` is present).
            *   `أَنَا أَوْصَلْتُ` (anā awṣaltu) - I delivered. (Weak letter `و` is present).
        *   **Present Tense (الْمُضَارِعُ):**
            *   `هُوَ يُوصِلُ` (huwa yūṣilu) - He delivers. (The `و` remains).
            *   `هِيَ تُوصِلُ` (hiya tūṣilu) - She delivers. (The `و` remains).
            *   `أَنَا أُوصِلُ` (anā ūṣilu) - I deliver. (The `و` remains).
    *   **Key Behavior:** The initial `و` remains stable throughout conjugations in Form IV.

*   **b. Hollow Form IV (أَجْوَفُ فِي الْبِنَاءِ الرَّابِعِ)**
    *   **Definition:** Middle root letter is weak (`و` or `ي`). The original weak letter often appears as `ا` in the past tense for `هُوَ`/`هِيَ`, but converts to `ي` in the present and drops for some past tense conjugations with suffixes.
    *   **Example Verb:** `أَقَامَ` (aqāma) - 'a-qā-ma' (He established/resided).
        *   **Original Root:** ق و م (q-w-m) (Form I: `قَامَ` - he stood)
        *   **Past Tense (الْمَاضِي):**
            *   `هُوَ أَقَامَ` (huwa aqāma) - He established. (The `و` becomes `ا`).
            *   `هِيَ أَقَامَتْ` (hiya aqāmat) - She established. (The `و` becomes `ا`).
            *   `أَنَا أَقَمْتُ` (anā aqamtu) - I established. (The `ا` (from `و`) drops completely, and the preceding vowel adjusts).
        *   **Present Tense (الْمُضَارِعُ):**
            *   `هُوَ يُقِيمُ` (huwa yuqīmu) - He establishes. (The `و` reappears as `ي`).
            *   `هِيَ تُقِيمُ` (hiya tuqīmu) - She establishes. (The `و` reappears as `ي`).
            *   `أَنَا أُقِيمُ` (anā uqīmu) - I establish. (The `و` reappears as `ي`).
    *   **Key Behavior:** The middle weak letter (`و` or `ي`) typically appears as `ا` in `هُوَ`/`هِيَ` past, drops in `أَنَا` past (with vowel adjustment), and reappears as `ي` in the present tense.

*   **c. Deficient Form IV (نَاقِصٌ فِي الْبِنَاءِ الرَّابِعِ)**
    *   **Definition:** Third root letter is weak (`و` or `ي`). Behaves similarly to Form I and II Nāqiṣ.
    *   **Example Verb:** `أَلْقَى` (alqā) - 'al-qā' (He threw/delivered).
        *   **Original Root:** ل ق ي (l-q-y) (Form I: `لَقِيَ` - he met)
        *   **Past Tense (الْمَاضِي):**
            *   `هُوَ أَلْقَى` (huwa alqā) - He threw. (The `ي` becomes `ى` - alif maqṣūrah).
            *   `هِيَ أَلْقَتْ` (hiya alqat) - She threw. (The `ى` (from `ي`) drops entirely for feminine suffix).
            *   `أَنَا أَلْقَيْتُ` (anā alqaytu) - I threw. (The `ي` reappears as `ي`).
        *   **Present Tense (الْمُضَارِعُ):**
            *   `هُوَ يُلْقِي` (huwa yulqī) - He throws. (The `ي` appears as `ي`).
            *   `هِيَ تُلْقِي` (hiya tulqī) - She throws. (The `ي` appears as `ي`).
            *   `أَنَا أُلْقِي` (anā ulqī) - I throw. (The `ي` appears as `ي`).
    *   **Key Behavior:** The final weak letter undergoes similar transformations to Form I and II Nāqiṣ verbs (converting to `ى` in the past `هُوَ`, dropping for `هِيَ`, and typically becoming `ي` in the present tense).

*   **d. Doubly Weak Form IV (لَفِيفٌ فِي الْبِنَاءِ الرَّابِعِ)**
    *   **Separated (لَفِيفٌ مَفْرُوقٌ):** First and third root letters are weak.
        *   **Example Verb:** `أَوْفَى` (awfā) - 'aw-fā' (He fulfilled).
        *   **Original Root:** و ف ي (w-f-y) (Form I: `وَفَى`, Form II: `وَفَّى`)
        *   **Past Tense (الْمَاضِي):**
            *   `هُوَ أَوْفَى` (huwa awfā) - He fulfilled. (The `و` is present, `ي` becomes `ى`).
            *   `هِيَ أَوْفَتْ` (hiya awfat) - She fulfilled. (The `و` is present, `ى` (from `ي`) drops).
            *   `أَنَا أَوْفَيْتُ` (anā awfaytu) - I fulfilled. (The `و` is present, `ي` reappears as `ي`).
        *   **Present Tense (الْمُضَارِعُ):**
            *   `هُوَ يُوفِي` (huwa yūfī) - He fulfills. (The `و` is present, `ي` appears as `ي`).
            *   `هِيَ تُوفِي` (hiya tūfī) - She fulfills. (The `و` is present, `ي` appears as `ي`).
            *   `أَنَا أُوفِي` (anā ūfī) - I fulfill. (The `و` is present, `ي` appears as `ي`).
        *   **Key Behavior:** The initial `و` remains stable (like Form IV Mithāl), and the final `ي` behaves like a Form IV Nāqiṣ.

    *   **Conjoined (لَفِيفٌ مَقْرُونٌ):** Second and third root letters are weak.
        *   **Example Verb:** `أَحْيَا` (aḥyā) - 'aḥ-yā' (He revived).
        *   **Original Root:** ح ي ي (ḥ-y-y) or ح ي و
        *   **Past Tense (الْمَاضِي):**
            *   `هُوَ أَحْيَا` (huwa aḥyā) - He revived. (The `ي` is present, final `ي` becomes `ا`).
            *   `هِيَ أَحْيَتْ` (hiya aḥyat) - She revived. (The `ي` is present, `ا` (from `ي`) drops).
            *   `أَنَا أَحْيَيْتُ` (anā aḥyaytu) - I revived. (The `ي` reappears as `ي`).
        *   **Present Tense (الْمُضَارِعُ):**
            *   `هُوَ يُحْيِي` (huwa yuḥyī) - He revives. (The `ي` appears as `ي`).
            *   `هِيَ تُحْيِي` (hiya tuḥyī) - She revives. (The `ي` appears as `ي`).
            *   `أَنَا أُحْيِي` (anā uḥyī) - I revive. (The `ي` appears as `ي`).
        *   **Key Behavior:** The two weak letters interact, with the first `ي` (from root) generally remaining and the final weak letter behaving like a Nāqiṣ verb.

### 2. Form V Irregular Verbs (الفِعْلُ الْمُعْتَلُّ فِي الْبِنَاءِ الْخَامِسِ)
Form V (`تَفَعَّلَ` - tafaʿʿala) is often the reflexive or passive of Form II.

*   **a. Assimilated Form V (مِثَالٌ فِي الْبِنَاءِ الْخَامِسِ)**
    *   **Definition:** First root letter is weak (`و` or `ي`). The initial `و` usually **remains**.
    *   **Example Verb:** `تَوَصَّلَ` (tawaṣṣala) - 'ta-waṣ-ṣa-la' (He reached/arrived at).
        *   **Root:** و ص ل (w-ṣ-l)
        *   **Past Tense (الْمَاضِي):**
            *   `هُوَ تَوَصَّلَ` (huwa tawaṣṣala) - He reached. (The `و` is present).
            *   `هِيَ تَوَصَّلَتْ` (hiya tawaṣṣalat) - She reached. (The `و` is present).
            *   `أَنَا تَوَصَّلْتُ` (anā tawaṣṣaltu) - I reached. (The `و` is present).
        *   **Present Tense (الْمُضَارِعُ):**
            *   `هُوَ يَتَوَصَّلُ` (huwa yatawaṣṣalu) - He reaches. (The `و` remains).
            *   `هِيَ تَتَوَصَّلُ` (hiya tatawaṣṣalu) - She reaches. (The `و` remains).
            *   `أَنَا أَتَوَصَّلُ` (anā atawaṣṣalu) - I reach. (The `و` remains).
    *   **Key Behavior:** The initial `و` remains stable throughout conjugations in Form V.

*   **b. Hollow Form V (أَجْوَفُ فِي الْبِنَاءِ الْخَامِسِ)**
    *   **Definition:** Middle root letter is weak (`و` or `ي`). The weak letter remains stable and geminated due to the Form V pattern.
    *   **Example Verb:** `تَقَوَّمَ` (taqawwama) - 'ta-qaw-wa-ma' (He was evaluated/straightened himself).
        *   **Original Root:** ق و م (q-w-m)
        *   **Past Tense (الْمَاضِي):**
            *   `هُوَ تَقَوَّمَ` (huwa taqawwama) - He was evaluated. (The `و` is geminated).
            *   `هِيَ تَقَوَّمَتْ` (hiya taqawwamat) - She was evaluated. (The `و` is geminated).
            *   `أَنَا تَقَوَّمْتُ` (anā taqawwamtu) - I was evaluated. (The `و` is geminated).
        *   **Present Tense (الْمُضَارِعُ):**
            *   `هُوَ يَتَقَوَّمُ` (huwa yataqawwamu) - He is evaluated. (The `و` is geminated).
            *   `هِيَ تَتَقَوَّمُ` (hiya tataqawwamu) - She is evaluated. (The `و` is geminated).
            *   `أَنَا أَتَقَوَّمُ` (anā ataqawwamu) - I am evaluated. (The `و` is geminated).
    *   **Key Behavior:** The middle weak letter `و` or `ي` typically becomes stable and geminated in Form V.

*   **c. Deficient Form V (نَاقِصٌ فِي الْبِنَاءِ الْخَامِسِ)**
    *   **Definition:** Third root letter is weak (`و` or `ي`). The weak letter typically appears as `ى` in past and present singular.
    *   **Example Verb:** `تَرَبَّى` (tarabbā) - 'ta-rab-bā' (He was raised/educated himself).
        *   **Original Root:** ر ب و (r-b-w) (Form I: `رَبَا` - he grew; Form II: `رَبَّى` - he raised)
        *   **Past Tense (الْمَاضِي):**
            *   `هُوَ تَرَبَّى` (huwa tarabbā) - He was raised. (The `و` becomes `ى` - alif maqṣūrah).
            *   `هِيَ تَرَبَّتْ` (hiya tarabbat) - She was raised. (The `ى` (from `و`) drops for feminine suffix).
            *   `أَنَا تَرَبَّيْتُ` (anā tarabbaytu) - I was raised. (The `و` reappears as `ي`).
        *   **Present Tense (الْمُضَارِعُ):**
            *   `هُوَ يَتَرَبَّى` (huwa yatarabbā) - He is raised. (The `و` becomes `ى`).
            *   `هِيَ تَتَرَبَّى` (hiya tatarabbā) - She is raised. (The `و` becomes `ى`).
            *   `أَنَا أَتَرَبَّى` (anā atarabbā) - I am raised. (The `و` becomes `ى`).
    *   **Key Behavior:** The final weak letter (`و` or `ي`) typically converts to `ى` (alif maqṣūrah) in both `هُوَ`/`هِيَ` past and present singular. It drops for `هِيَ` past with `تْ` suffix, and reappears as `ي` for `أَنَا` past.

*   **d. Doubly Weak Form V (لَفِيفٌ فِي الْبِنَاءِ الْخَامِسِ)**
    *   **Separated (لَفِيفٌ مَفْرُوقٌ):** First and third root letters are weak.
        *   **Example Verb:** `تَوَفَّى` (tawaffā) - 'ta-waf-fā' (He passed away/died).
        *   **Original Root:** و ف ي (w-f-y)
        *   **Past Tense (الْمَاضِي):**
            *   `هُوَ تَوَفَّى` (huwa tawaffā) - He passed away. (`و` present, `ي` becomes `ى`).
            *   `هِيَ تَوَفَّتْ` (hiya tawaffat) - She passed away. (`و` present, `ى` (from `ي`) drops).
            *   `أَنَا تَوَفَّيْتُ` (anā tawaffaytu) - I passed away. (`و` present, `ي` reappears as `ي`).
        *   **Present Tense (الْمُضَارِعُ):**
            *   `هُوَ يَتَوَفَّى` (huwa yatawaffā) - He passes away. (`و` present, `ي` becomes `ى`).
            *   `هِيَ تَتَوَفَّى` (hiya tatawaffā) - She passes away. (`و` present, `ي` becomes `ى`).
            *   `أَنَا أَتَوَفَّى` (anā atawaffā) - I pass away. (`و` present, `ي` becomes `ى`).
        *   **Key Behavior:** The initial `و` remains stable, and the final `ي` behaves like a Form V Nāqiṣ.

    *   **Conjoined (لَفِيفٌ مَقْرُونٌ):** Second and third root letters are weak.
        *   **Example Verb:** `تَشَوَّى` (tashawwā) - 'ta-shaw-wā' (It was grilled/roasted).
        *   **Original Root:** ش و ي (sh-w-y)
        *   **Past Tense (الْمَاضِي):**
            *   `هُوَ تَشَوَّى` (huwa tashawwā) - It was grilled. (`و` present, `ي` becomes `ى`).
            *   `هِيَ تَشَوَّتْ` (hiya tashawwat) - It was grilled. (`و` present, `ى` (from `ي`) drops).
            *   `أَنَا تَشَوَّيْتُ` (anā tashawwaytu) - I was grilled. (`و` present, `ي` reappears as `ي`).
        *   **Present Tense (الْمُضَارِعُ):**
            *   `هُوَ يَتَشَوَّى` (huwa yatashawwā) - It is grilled. (`و` present, `ي` becomes `ى`).
            *   `هِيَ تَتَشَوَّى` (hiya tatashawwā) - It is grilled. (`و` present, `ي` becomes `ى`).
            *   `أَنَا أَتَشَوَّى` (anā atashawwā) - I am grilled. (`و` present, `ي` becomes `ى`).
        *   **Key Behavior:** The second weak letter (`و`) remains stable, and the final `ي` behaves like a Form V Nāqiṣ.

Mastering these specific patterns for Form IV and Form V verbs will further significantly expand your active vocabulary and comprehension of complex Arabic texts. Remember, the general rules for weak letters often carry across forms, but each form has its own specific pattern.

## Examples

Let's see these Form IV and V irregular verbs in action, integrated into the sophisticated sentence structures you've been mastering. Pay attention to how they function within the advanced rhetoric.

1.  **"Undoubtedly, effective policy-making, by continuously adapting (`مُتَكَيِّفَةً`), often delivered (`أَوْصَلَتْ`) deep insights to societal needs. This, nevertheless, demanded (`وَيَدْعُو إِلَى`) establishing (`إِقَامَةِ`) a broad consensus and continuously provoking (`وَتُثِيرُ`) reflection."**
    *   `مِمَّا لَا شَكَّ فِيهِ، أَنَّ صُنْعَ السِّيَاسَةِ الْفَعَّالِ، مُتَكَيِّفَةً بِاسْتِمْرَارٍ، أَوْصَلَتْ كَثِيرًا مَا رُؤًى مُتَعَمِّقَةً لِلِاحْتِيَاجَاتِ الْمُجْتَمَعِيَّةِ. مَعَ ذَلِكَ، وَيَدْعُو هَذَا إِلَى إِقَامَةِ تَوَافُقٍ وَاسِعٍ وَإِثَارَةِ التَّأَمُّلِ بِاسْتِمْرَارٍ.`
    *   **Irregular Verb Focus:**
        *   `مُتَكَيِّفَةً` (mutakayyifatun - adapting, active participle Form V): From `تَكَيَّفَ` (Sound verb in Form V). Just showing it in context of adaptive policy-making.
        *   `أَوْصَلَتْ` (awṣalat - she delivered, past tense Form IV): From `أَوْصَلَ` (Assimilated, و ص ل). The `و` is present.
        *   `إِقَامَةِ` (iqāmatun - establishing, Masdar Form IV): From `أَقَامَ` (Hollow, ق و م). This Masdar shows the `ي` (from `و`) as part of the stem.
    *   **Cohesion Breakdown:** This sentence builds on policy argumentation and cohesion. `مِمَّا لَا شَكَّ فِيهِ، أَنَّ` (Day 196) sets a strong opening. The subject is `صُنْعَ السِّيَاسَةِ الْفَعَّالِ` (effective policy-making, Day 250). It's described as `مُتَكَيِّفَةً بِاسْتِمْرَارٍ` (continuously adapting, Day 255/251). It `أَوْصَلَتْ كَثِيرًا مَا رُؤًى مُتَعَمِّقَةً لِلِاحْتِيَاجَاتِ الْمُجْتَمَعِيَّةِ` (often delivered deep insights to societal needs, **new for today** `أَوْصَلَتْ` + Day 139 `كَثِيرًا مَا` + Day 248 `رُؤًى مُتَعَمِّقَةً` + Day 220 `احْتِيَاجَاتٍ مُجْتَمَعِيَّةٍ`). `مَعَ ذَلِكَ` (Day 225) introduces a further call to action: `يَدْعُو هَذَا إِلَى إِقَامَةِ تَوَافُقٍ وَاسِعٍ وَإِثَارَةِ التَّأَمُّلِ بِاسْتِمْرَارٍ` (this calls for establishing a broad consensus and continuously provoking reflection, Day 156/250, Day 250 `تَوَافُقٍ وَاسِعٍ` + Day 253 `إِثَارَةُ التَّأَمُّلِ` + Day 251 `بِاسْتِمْرَارٍ`).

2.  **"While some believe that current policies are hindering public awareness and causing dangerous polarization, others argue, in contrast, that policies should reach (`أَنْ تَتَوَصَّلَ`) effective solutions and fulfill (`تُوفِيَ`) their promises. This requires continuous monitoring and evaluation to ensure objectivity, enabling us to throw (`أَنْ نُلْقِيَ`) light on complex issues."**
    *   `بَيْنَمَا يَرَى الْبَعْضُ أَنَّ السِّيَاسَاتِ الْحَالِيَّةَ تُعِيقُ الْوَعْيَ الْعَامَّ وَتَتَسَبَّبُ فِي اسْتِقْطَابٍ خَطِيرٍ، يُجَادِلُ آخَرُونَ بِالْمُقَابِلِ أَنَّ السِّيَاسَاتِ يَجِبُ أَنْ تَتَوَصَّلَ إِلَى حُلُولٍ فَعَّالَةٍ وَأَنْ تُوفِيَ بِوُعُودِهَا. وَهَذَا يَسْتَلْزِمُ رَصْدًا مُتَوَاصِلًا وَتَقْيِيمًا لِضَمَانِ الْمَوْضُوعِيَّةِ، مُمَكِّنًا إِيَّانَا أَنْ نُلْقِيَ الضَّوْءَ عَلَى الْقَضَايَا الْمُعَقَّدَةِ.`
    *   **Irregular Verb Focus:**
        *   `تَتَوَصَّلَ` (tatawaṣṣala - she reaches, subjunctive Form V): From `تَوَصَّلَ` (Assimilated, و ص ل). The `و` is present.
        *   `تُوفِيَ` (tūfiya - she fulfills, subjunctive Form IV): From `أَوْفَى` (Doubly Weak - Separated, و ف ي). The `و` and `ي` are present.
        *   `نُلْقِيَ` (nulqiya - we throw, subjunctive Form IV): From `أَلْقَى` (Deficient, ل ق ي). The `ي` is present.
    *   **Cohesion Breakdown:** This sentence presents contrasting arguments (Day 146/186). The first criticizes policies for `تُعِيقُ الْوَعْيَ الْعَامَّ وَتَتَسَبَّبُ فِي اسْتِقْطَابٍ خَطِيرٍ` (Day 230/226, Day 238/221/155). The counter-argument is that policies (`السِّيَاسَاتِ`) `يَجِبُ أَنْ تَتَوَصَّلَ إِلَى حُلُولٍ فَعَّالَةٍ` (must reach effective solutions, Day 121 + **new for today** `تَتَوَصَّلَ` + `حُلُولٌ فَعَّالَةٌ` - effective solutions, Day 147 `فَعَّالَةٌ`) and `أَنْ تُوفِيَ بِوُعُودِهَا` (and fulfill their promises, **new for today** `تُوفِيَ` + `وُعُودٌ` - promises, plural of `وَعْدٌ` from Form I `وَعَدَ`). The requirement (`وَهَذَا يَسْتَلْزِمُ`) is `رَصْدًا مُتَوَاصِلًا وَتَقْيِيمًا لِضَمَانِ الْمَوْضُوعِيَّةِ` (continuous monitoring and evaluation to ensure objectivity, Day 231/162, Day 235). The active participle `مُمَكِّنًا إِيَّانَا` (enabling us, Day 246 context) leads to the action `أَنْ نُلْقِيَ الضَّوْءَ عَلَى الْقَضَايَا الْمُعَقَّدَةِ` (to throw light on complex issues, **new for today** `نُلْقِيَ` + `ضَوْءٌ` - light, `قَضَايَا مُعَقَّدَةٌ` - complex issues, Day 215/170).

3.  **"It is important to point out that the journey of digital journalism, as a pivotal transformation, must track a unique path that is characterized by continuous efforts to enrich public discourse. This approach, which embodies human values and international cooperation, requires a firm and steadfast stance on new legislation, ensuring accountability and preventing dangerous polarization, offering a comprehensive outlook on societal needs, marking a decisive turning point in the destiny of civilization, and contributing to the crystallization of good governance and international law, while always maintaining objectivity.     *   `مِنَ الْمُهِمِّ الْإِشَارَةُ إِلَى أَنَّ رَحْلَةَ الصِّحَافَةِ الرَّقْمِيَّةِ، كَتَحَوُّلٍ مِحْوَرِيٍّ، يَجِبُ أَنْ تَتَتَبَّعَ مَسَارًا فَرِيدًا يَتَّسِمُ بِجُهُودٍ مُتَوَاصِلَةٍ لِإِثْرَاءِ الْخِطَابِ الْعَامِّ. وَهَذَا النَّهْجُ، الَّذِي يُجَسِّدُ الْقِيَمَ الْإِنْسَانِيَّةَ وَالتَّعَاوُنَ الدَّوْلِيَّ، يَسْتَلْزِمُ مَوْقِفًا حَازِمًا صَامِدًا بِشَأْنِ تَشْرِيعٍ جَدِيدٍ، ضَامِنًا الْمُحَاسَبَةَ وَمُتَجَنِّبًا الِاسْتِقْطَابَ الْخَطِيرَ، وَمُقَدِّمًا نَظْرَةً شَامِلَةً لِلِاحْتِيَاجَاتِ الْمُجْتَمَعِيَّةِ، مُشَكِّلًا مَحَطَّةً فَارِقَةً فِي مَصِيرِ الْحَضَارَةِ، وَمُسْهِمًا فِي تَبْلُوُرِ الْحُكْمِ الرَّشِيدِ وَالْقَانُونِ الدَّوْلِيِّ، مَعَ الْحِفَاظِ دَائِمًا عَلَى الْمَوْضُوعِيَّةِ. عَلَاوَةً عَلَى ذَلِكَ، يُمْكِنُ لِلسِّيَاسَاتِ الَّتِي يَصُوغُهَا صِحَافِيُّو الْمُوَاطِنِينَ الْحُصُولُ عَلَى تَأْيِيدٍ وَاسِعٍ عَبْرَ الْمِنَصَّاتِ مُتَعَدِّدَةِ الْوَسَائِطِ التَّفَاعُلِيَّةِ، بِشَرْطِ أَنْ تَتَنَاوَلَ قَضَايَا حَاسِمَةً بِتَبْرِيرٍ وَاضِحٍ وَتَقْيِيمٍ دَقِيقٍ، وَأَنْ تَضْمَنَ جَدْوَى نَتَائِجِهَا مِنْ خِلَالِ مُقَارَبَةٍ تَقُومُ عَلَى التَّوَافُقِ، وَأَنْ تَتَكَيَّفَ بِاسْتِمْرَارٍ لِلتَّغَلُّبِ عَلَى عَقَبَاتِ التَّنْفِيذِ، وَأَنْ تَخْضَعَ لِمُرَاجَعَةٍ نَقْدِيَّةٍ وَقِيَاسِ فَعَالِيَّةٍ، وَأَنْ تَتَرَبَّى بِاسْتِمْرَارٍ فِي بِيئَةٍ بَنَّاءَةٍ تُحْيِيَ الْأَمَلَ.`
    *   **Irregular Verb Focus:**
        *   `تَتَرَبَّى` (tatarabbā - she is raised, subjunctive Form V): From `تَرَبَّى` (Deficient, ر ب و). The final `ى` remains.
        *   `تُحْيِيَ` (tuḥyiya - she revives, subjunctive Form IV): From `أَحْيَا` (Doubly Weak - Conjoined, ح ي ي). The `ي` is present.

The first part is largely recycled from Day 255's example 3, demonstrating coherence and progressive difficulty. `عَلَاوَةً عَلَى ذَلِكَ` (Day 196) introduces a new point. The conditions for policies (`بِشَرْطِ أَنْ تَتَنَاوَلَ... وَأَنْ تَضْمَنَ... وَأَنْ تَتَكَيَّفَ... وَأَنْ تَخْضَعَ`) are detailed using Masdars and verbs from Day 245, 250, 251, 255. The new addition is `وَأَنْ تَتَرَبَّى بِاسْتِمْرَارٍ فِي بِيئَةٍ بَنَّاءَةٍ تُحْيِيَ الْأَمَلَ` (and are continually raised in a constructive environment that revives hope, **new for today** `تَتَرَبَّى` + `بِيئَةٌ بَنَّاءَةٌ` - constructive environment, `بِيئَةٌ` - environment, `بَنَّاءَةٌ` - constructive, Day 231 `بِنَائِيٌّ` context + **new for today** `تُحْيِيَ` + `أَمَلٌ` - hope).

        *   `نَتَوَصَّلُ` (natawaṣṣalu - we reach, present tense Form V): From `تَوَصَّلَ` (Assimilated, و ص ل). The `و` is present.
        *   `إِقَامَةِ` (iqāmatun - establishing, Masdar Form IV): From `أَقَامَ` (Hollow, ق و م).

It starts with the media's role in guiding public opinion (`تَوْجِيهِ الرَّأْيِ الْعَامِّ`) towards MSA while acknowledging dialects (Day 242), contributing to cohesion and mitigating misunderstanding (Day 242/236). *   `بَيْنَمَا يَرَى الْبَعْضُ أَنَّ السِّيَاسَاتِ الْحَالِيَّةَ تُعِيقُ الْوَعْيَ الْعَامَّ وَتَتَسَبَّبُ فِي اسْتِقْطَابٍ خَطِيرٍ، يُجَادِلُ آخَرُونَ بِالْمُقَابِلِ أَنَّ السِّيَاسَاتِ يَجِبُ أَنْ تَتَوَصَّلَ إِلَى حُلُولٍ فَعَّالَةٍ وَأَنْ تُوفِيَ بِوُعُودِهَا. وَهَذَا يَسْتَلْزِمُ رَصْدًا مُتَوَاصِلًا وَتَقْيِيمًا لِضَمَانِ الْمَوْضُوعِيَّةِ، مُمَكِّنًا إِيَّانَا أَنْ نُلْقِيَ الضَّوْءَ عَلَى الْقَضَايَا الْمُعَقَّدَةِ.`
*   `مِنَ الْمُهِمِّ الْإِشَارَةُ إِلَى أَنَّ رَحْلَةَ الصِّحَافَةِ الرَّقْمِيَّةِ، كَتَحَوُّلٍ مِحْوَرِيٍّ، يَجِبُ أَنْ تَتَتَبَّعَ مَسَارًا فَرِيدًا يَتَّسِمُ بِجُهُودٍ مُتَوَاصِلَةٍ لِإِثْرَاءِ الْخِطَابِ الْعَامِّ. وَهَذَا النَّهْجُ، الَّذِي يُجَسِّدُ الْقِيَمَ الْإِنْسَانِيَّةَ وَالتَّعَاوُنَ الدَّوْلِيَّ، يَسْتَلْزِمُ مَوْقِفًا حَازِمًا صَامِدًا بِشَأْنِ تَشْرِيعٍ جَدِيدٍ، ضَامِنًا الْمُحَاسَبَةَ وَمُتَجَنِّبًا الِاسْتِقْطَابَ الْخَطِيرَ، وَمُقَدِّمًا نَظْرَةً شَامِلَةً لِلِاحْتِيَاجَاتِ الْمُجْتَمَعِيَّةِ، مُشَكِّلًا مَحَطَّةً فَارِقَةً فِي مَصِيرِ الْحَضَارَةِ، وَمُسْهِمًا فِي تَبْلُوُرِ الْحُكْمِ الرَّشِيدِ وَالْقَانُونِ الدَّوْلِيِّ، مَعَ الْحِفَاظِ دَائِمًا عَلَى الْمَوْضُوعِيَّةِ. عَلَاوَةً عَلَى ذَلِكَ، يُمْكِنُ لِلسِّيَاسَاتِ الَّتِي يَصُوغُهَا صِحَافِيُّو الْمُوَاطِنِينَ الْحُصُولُ عَلَى تَأْيِيدٍ وَاسِعٍ عَبْرَ الْمِنَصَّاتِ مُتَعَدِّدَةِ الْوَسَائِطِ التَّفَاعُلِيَّةِ، بِشَرْطِ أَنْ تَتَنَاوَلَ قَضَايَا حَاسِمَةً بِتَبْرِيرٍ وَاضِحٍ وَتَقْيِيمٍ دَقِيقٍ، وَأَنْ تَضْمَنَ جَدْوَى نَتَائِجِهَا مِنْ خِلَالِ مُقَارَبَةٍ تَقُومُ عَلَى التَّوَافُقِ، وَأَنْ تَتَكَيَّفَ بِاسْتِمْرَارٍ لِلتَّغَلُّبِ عَلَى عَقَبَاتِ التَّنْفِيذِ، وَأَنْ تَخْضَعَ لِمُرَاجَعَةٍ نَقْدِيَّةٍ وَقِيَاسِ فَعَالِيَّةٍ، وَأَنْ تَتَرَبَّى بِاسْتِمْرَارٍ فِي بِيئَةٍ بَنَّاءَةٍ تُحْيِيَ الْأَمَلَ.`
*   `مِمَّا لَا شَكَّ فِيهِ، أَنَّ الْإِعْلَامَ يَلْعَبُ دَوْرًا مِحْوَرِيًّا فِي تَوْجِيهِ الرَّأْيِ الْعَامِّ نَحْوَ اعْتِنَاقِ الْعَرَبِيَّةِ الْفُصْحَى مَعَ الِاعْتِرَافِ بِاللَّهَجَاتِ الْعَرَبِيَّةِ الْمُتَنَوِّعَةِ فِي سِيَاقَاتِهَا الْمُحَدَّدَةِ، مِمَّا يُسْهِمُ فِي تَرْسِيخِ التَّمَاسُكِ اللُّغَوِيِّ وَتَخْفِيفِ تَدَاعِيَاتِ سُوءِ الْفَهْمِ. وَصِحَافِيُّو الْمُوَاطِنِ، بِتَبَنِّي زَاوِيَةٍ فَرِيدَةٍ وَتَقْدِيمِ تَصْوِيرٍ حَيٍّ مِنْ خِلَالِ التَّغْطِيَةِ اللَّحْظِيَّةِ، يُمْكِنُهُمْ أَنْ يُبْرِزُوا الْجَانِبَ الْإِنْسَانِيَّ، ضَامِنِينَ مُشَارَكَةَ الْجُمْهُورِ وَمُعَزِّزِينَ رُؤًى مُتَعَمِّقَةً حَوْلَ الْقَضَايَا الْمُلِحَّةِ مِنْ خِلَالِ تَصْوِيرِ الْبَيَانَاتِ وَالْمُحْتَوَى مُتَعَدِّدِ الْوَسَائِطِ التَّفَاعُلِيِّ عَلَى الْمِنَصَّاتِ الرَّقْمِيَّةِ، وَالَّذِي يُوَفِّيَ بِهَدَفِ تَحْقِيقِ الْمَصِيرِ الْحَقِيقِيِّ لِلتَّوَاصُلِ، مُحَقِّقًا فِي النِّهَايَةِ بِنَاءَ الصِّلَةِ وَجَذْبَ الْعَوَاطِفِ، وَمُتَطَلِّبًا دَائِمًا التَّحَقُّقَ مِنْ الْمَعْلُومَاتِ وَمُقَارَبَةً قَائِمَةً عَلَى التَّوَافُقِ فِي صُنْعِ السِّيَاسَةِ، كَمَا نُوَاصِلُ رَحْلَتَنَا نَحْوَ الْإِصْلَاحِ بِهَدَفِ كَشْفِ الْحَقَائِقِ الْخَفِيَّةِ وَتَغْيِيرِ الْمَفَاهِيمِ مِنْ خِلَالِ الْوَاقِعِ الْمُعَزَّزِ وَالْوَاقِعِ الِافْتِرَاضِيِّ، مُعَزَّزًا بِصِحَافَةٍ صَوْتِيَّةٍ، كُلُّ ذَلِكَ خَاضِعًا لِمُرَاجَعَةٍ نَقْدِيَّةٍ وَقِيَاسِ فَعَالِيَّةٍ لِتَجَنُّبِ الْآثَارِ غَيْرِ الْمَقْصُودَةِ وَالتَّغَلُّبِ عَلَى عَقَبَاتِ التَّنْفِيذِ، وَإِقَامَةِ الثِّقَةِ وَإِحْيَاءِ الْكَرَامَةِ بِاسْتِمْرَارٍ.`

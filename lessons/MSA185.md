# Day 185 - Policy analysis language

## Lesson

"Policy analysis language" is all about providing you with the specialized terms needed to engage in detailed discussions about how policies are conceived, developed, implemented, and evaluated. This means understanding the structures policies operate within, who makes them, how their effects are measured, and what alternatives exist.

Think of it as having a sophisticated set of tools to articulate the intricate world of policy-making. You'll learn specific terms for the foundational aspects of policies, the actors involved, methods of assessment, and the dynamics of change and choice.

Here are the key vocabulary and phrases you'll learn today:

### 1. Core Policy Concepts:

*   **`إِطَارٌ`** (iṭārun) - 'i-ṭaar' (masculine noun)
    *   *Meaning:* Framework, frame, context.
    *   *Explanation:* This term is fundamental for discussing the structure or conceptual boundaries within which a policy or discussion operates.
        *   *Example:* `إِطَارُ السِّيَاسَةِ` (iṭāru as-siyāsah) - Policy framework.
*   **`صَانِعُ السِّيَاسَةِ`** (ṣāniʿu as-siyāsah) - 'ṣaa-ni-'u as-see-yaa-sah' (masculine noun phrase, Idafa)
    *   *Meaning:* Policymaker (singular).
    *   *Explanation:* `صَانِعٌ` (ṣāniʿun) means 'maker' or 'creator'. This refers to an individual responsible for creating or implementing policies.
    *   *Plural:* **`صُنَّاعُ السِّيَاسَةِ`** (ṣunnāʿu as-siyāsah) - 'ṣun-naa-'u as-see-yaa-sah' (masculine plural noun phrase, Idafa) - Policymakers.

### 2. Policy Assessment & Development:

*   **`تَقْيِيمُ أَثَرِ السِّيَاسَةِ`** (taqyīmu athari as-siyāsah) - 'taq-yee-mu a-tha-ri as-see-yaa-sah' (masculine Masdar phrase, Idafa)
    *   *Meaning:* Policy impact assessment.
    *   *Explanation:* This phrase combines `تَقْيِيمٌ` (evaluation, Day 156), `أَثَرٌ` (impact, Day 122), and `سِيَاسَةٌ` (policy, Day 146). It refers to the process of evaluating the effects or consequences of a policy.
*   **`دِرَاسَةُ جَدْوَى`** (dirāsatu jadwā) - 'di-raa-sa-tu jad-waa' (feminine noun phrase, Idafa)
    *   *Meaning:* Feasibility study.
    *   *Explanation:* This phrase combines `دِرَاسَةٌ` (study, Day 156) and `جَدْوَى` (feasibility, Day 178 context). It refers to an analysis of the practicality of a proposed plan or project.
*   **`إِصْلَاحٌ`** (iṣlāḥun) - 'iṣ-laaḥ' (masculine Masdar, Form IV)
    *   *Meaning:* Reform.
    *   *Explanation:* This term refers to the improvement or amendment of what is wrong, corrupt, or unsatisfactory. It implies a significant change to a system or policy.
    *   *Verb form:* **`يُصْلِحُ`** (yuṣliḥu) - 'yuṣ-li-ḥu' (present tense verb, Form IV) - He reforms/amends. Its feminine form is `تُصْلِحُ` (tuṣliḥu).
*   **`مُشَاوَرَةٌ`** (mushāwaratun) - 'mu-shaa-wa-ra-tun' (feminine Masdar, Form III)
    *   *Meaning:* Consultation.
    *   *Explanation:* This refers to the act of seeking advice or opinions, especially in a formal context, before making a decision.
    *   *Verb form:* **`يُشَاوِرُ`** (yushāwiru) - 'yu-shaa-wi-ru' (present tense verb, Form III) - He consults. Its feminine form is `تُشَاوِرُ` (tushāwiru).
        *   *Example:* `مُشَاوَرَةُ أَصْحَابِ الْمَصْلَحَةِ` (stakeholder consultation, Day 167 `أَصْحَابُ الْمَصْلَحَةِ`).

### 3. Policy Choices & Acceptance:

*   **`خِيَارٌ`** (khiyārun) - 'khi-yaar' (masculine noun)
    *   *Meaning:* Option, choice.
    *   *Explanation:* A specific alternative that can be chosen.
    *   *Plural:* **`خِيَارَاتٌ`** (khiyārātun) - 'khi-yaa-raat' (feminine plural noun) - Options, choices.
        *   *Example:* `خِيَارَاتُ السِّيَاسَةِ` (policy options).
*   **`قَبُولٌ`** (qabūlun) - 'qa-bool' (masculine Masdar, Form I)
    *   *Meaning:* Acceptance, approval.
    *   *Explanation:* This refers to the act of receiving or assenting to something as suitable or satisfactory.
        *   *Example:* `قَبُولٌ عَامٌّ لِلسِّيَاسَةِ` (general public acceptance of the policy).

Remember to seamlessly integrate these new terms with your existing advanced grammar, statistical vocabulary (Days 151, 158, 165, 172), polling language (Days 152, 159, 166, 173), corporate terms (Days 153, 160, 167, 174), energy vocabulary (Days 154, 161, 168, 175), narrative techniques (Days 155, 162, 169, 176), academic register (Days 156, 163, 170, 177, 181), debate vocabulary (Day 183), and media ethics terminology (Day 184).

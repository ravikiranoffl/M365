# Day 167 - Advanced Journalism: Corporate reporting

## Lesson

"Advanced Corporate reporting" means we're going beyond just net profits and share values to discuss the broader financial landscape, strategic direction, and critical relationships that define a company's success or failure. It involves understanding more abstract financial concepts, long-term strategic initiatives, and the diverse groups influenced by (and influencing) corporate decisions.

Think of it as having the most powerful lenses in your advanced Arabic toolkit, allowing you to examine and present complex corporate narratives with the utmost critical depth and analytical precision. You'll learn specific terms for overarching financial indicators, strategic long-term vision, modern business transformations, and the full spectrum of a company's interested parties.

Here are the key vocabulary and phrases you'll learn today:

### 1. Advanced Market & Financial Metrics:

*   `مُؤَشِّرُ السُّوقِ` (mu'ashshiru as-sūqi) - 'mu-'ash-shi-ru as-soo-qi' (masculine noun phrase, Idafa)
    *   *Meaning:* Market index.
    *   *Explanation:* You know `مُؤَشِّرٌ` (indicator, Day 165) and `سُوقٌ` (market, Day 103/153). Together, this refers to a statistical measure of changes in a stock market.
    *   *Plural:* `مُؤَشِّرَاتُ السُّوقِ` (mu'ashshirātu as-sūqi) - Market indices.
*   `رَأْسُ الْمَالِ السُّوقِيُّ` (ra'su al-māli as-sūqiyyu) - 'ra'-su al-maa-li as-soo-qiy-yu' (masculine noun phrase)
    *   *Meaning:* Market capitalization.
    *   *Explanation:* `رَأْسُ الْمَالِ` (capital) refers to the total value of a company's outstanding shares. `سُوقِيٌّ` (market-related) you know from `حِصَّةٌ سُوقِيَّةٌ` (Day 160).
*   `أَرْبَاحُ السَّهْمِ` (arbāḥu as-sahmi) - 'ar-baa-ḥu as-sah-mi' (feminine plural noun phrase, Idafa)
    *   *Meaning:* Earnings per share (EPS).
    *   *Explanation:* This is a key financial metric, indicating the portion of a company's profit allocated to each outstanding share of common stock. You know `أَرْبَاحٌ` (profits, Day 153) and `سَهْمٌ` (share, Day 153).
*   `تَوْزِيعَاتُ الْأَرْبَاحِ` (tawzīʿātu al-arbāḥi) - 'taw-zee-'aa-tu al-ar-baaḥ' (feminine plural noun phrase, Idafa)
    *   *Meaning:* Dividends.
    *   *Explanation:* `تَوْزِيعَاتٌ` (distributions) is the plural of `تَوْزِيعٌ` (distribution/dividing, Masdar, new today). This refers to a sum of money paid regularly (typically quarterly) by a company to its shareholders out of its profits.

### 2. Strategic Vision & Modern Transformation:

*   `خُطَّةٌ اسْتِرَاتِيجِيَّةٌ` (khuṭṭatun istirātījiyyatun) - 'khuṭ-ṭa-tun is-ti-raa-tee-jee-ya-tun' (feminine noun + adjective)
    *   *Meaning:* Strategic plan.
    *   *Explanation:* `خُطَّةٌ` (plan, Day 103) and `اسْتِرَاتِيجِيَّةٌ` (strategic) is the feminine adjective from `اسْتِرَاتِيجِيَّاتٌ` (strategies, Day 152). This refers to a company's long-term vision and objectives.
*   `التَّحَوُّلُ الرَّقْمِيُّ` (at-taḥawwulu ar-raqmiyyu) - 'at-ta-ḥaw-wu-lu ar-raq-mi-y-yu' (masculine noun + adjective)
    *   *Meaning:* Digital transformation.
    *   *Explanation:* `تَحَوُّلٌ` (shift/change, Day 166) and `رَقْمِيٌّ` (digital, new today) from `رَقْمٌ` (number, Day 151). This refers to the integration of digital technology into all areas of a business.
*   `تَوَسُّعٌ فِي الْأَسْوَاقِ الْعَالَمِيَّةِ` (tawassuʿun fī al-aswāqi al-ʿālamiyyah) - 'ta-was-su-'un fī al-as-waa-qi al-'aa-la-mee-ya-ti' (masculine Masdar phrase)
    *   *Meaning:* Global market expansion.
    *   *Explanation:* `تَوَسُّعٌ` (expansion, Masdar of Form V `تَوَسَّعَ`) is a term you've seen in the Masdar `تَوْسِيعٌ` (expanding, Day 154). `أَسْوَاقٌ` (markets, Day 161) and `عَالَمِيَّةٌ` (global, Day 103).

### 3. Stakeholder Relations & Oversight:

*   `أَصْحَابُ الْمَصْلَحَةِ` (aṣḥābu al-maṣlaḥah) - 'aṣ-ḥaa-bu al-maṣ-la-ḥah' (masculine plural noun phrase, Idafa)
    *   *Meaning:* Stakeholders.
    *   *Explanation:* `أَصْحَابُ` (owners/people of, plural of `صَاحِبٌ` - owner/companion, new today) and `الْمَصْلَحَةِ` (the interest/benefit, new today). This refers to any individuals or groups who have an interest or are affected by the company's actions (e.g., employees, customers, suppliers, communities, shareholders). You know `مَصَالِحُ` (interests, Day 117).
*   `تَحْقِيقٌ دَاخِلِيٌّ` (taḥqīqun dākhiliyyun) - 'taḥ-qee-qun daa-khi-liy-yun' (masculine noun + adjective)
    *   *Meaning:* Internal investigation.
    *   *Explanation:* `تَحْقِيقٌ` (investigation, new today - Masdar of Form II `حَقَّقَ`) refers to a formal inquiry. `دَاخِلِيٌّ` (internal, new today) is the adjective. This refers to an investigation conducted within a company.
*   `مَوَاعِيدُ نِهَائِيَّةٌ` (mawāʿeedu nihā'iyyatun) - 'ma-waa-'ee-du ni-haa-'ee-ya-tun' (feminine plural noun phrase, diptote)
    *   *Meaning:* Deadlines.
    *   *Explanation:* `مَوَاعِيدُ` (appointments/dates, plural of `مَوْعِدٌ` - appointment/date, new today) and `نِهَائِيَّةٌ` (final/deadline-related, new today) is the feminine adjective from `نِهَائِيٌّ` (final, Day 163 in context of `تَكْلِفَةٌ نِهَائِيَّةٌ`). This refers to the latest time or date by which something should be completed.

Remember to seamlessly integrate these new terms with your existing advanced grammar, statistical vocabulary (Days 151, 158, 165), polling language (Days 152, 159, 166), core corporate terms (Days 153, 160), energy vocabulary (Days 154, 161), narrative techniques (Days 155, 162), academic register (Days 156, 163), and especially with your comprehensive argumentation structures. This will enable you to write and speak about business news with exceptional depth and analytical rigor.

## Examples

1.  **"It is certain that the company's strategic plan emphasizes digital transformation to achieve global market expansion. And the evidence for that is that the latest annual report shows a noticeable increase in market capitalization."**
    *   `مِنَ الْمُؤَكَّدِ أَنَّ الْخُطَّةَ الِاسْتِرَاتِيجِيَّةَ لِلشَّرِكَةِ تُشَدِّدُ عَلَى التَّحَوُّلِ الرَّقْمِيِّ لِتَحْقِيقِ تَوَسُّعٍ فِي الْأَسْوَاقِ الْعَالَمِيَّةِ. وَالدَّلِيلُ عَلَى ذَلِكَ أَنَّ التَّقْرِيرَ السَّنَوِيَّ الْأَخِيرَ يُظْهِرُ زِيَادَةً مَلْحُوظَةً فِي رَأْسِ الْمَالِ السُّوقِيِّ.`
    *   **Breakdown:**
        *   `مِنَ الْمُؤَكَّدِ أَنَّ` (It is certain that - Day 125).
        *   `الْخُطَّةَ الِاسْتِرَاتِيجِيَّةَ لِلشَّرِكَةِ تُشَدِّدُ عَلَى التَّحَوُّلِ الرَّقْمِيِّ` (the company's strategic plan emphasizes digital transformation - `الْخُطَّةَ الِاسْتِرَاتِيجِيَّةَ` (**new for today**) Accusative subject of `أَنَّ`; `لِلشَّرِكَةِ` (for the company, Day 120); `تُشَدِّدُ عَلَى` (she emphasizes on, Day 153); `التَّحَوُّلِ الرَّقْمِيِّ` (**new for today**) Genitive).
        *   `لِتَحْقِيقِ تَوَسُّعٍ فِي الْأَسْوَاقِ الْعَالَمِيَّةِ` (to achieve global market expansion - `لِتَحْقِيقِ` (to achieve, Masdar Form II); `تَوَسُّعٍ فِي الْأَسْوَاقِ الْعَالَمِيَّةِ` (**new for today**) Genitive Idafa).
        *   `وَالدَّلِيلُ عَلَى ذَلِكَ أَنَّ` (And the evidence for that is that - Day 132).
        *   `التَّقْرِيرَ السَّنَوِيَّ الْأَخِيرَ يُظْهِرُ زِيَادَةً مَلْحُوظَةً فِي رَأْسِ الْمَالِ السُّوقِيِّ` (the latest annual report shows a noticeable increase in market capitalization - `التَّقْرِيرَ السَّنَوِيَّ الْأَخِيرَ` (annual report, Day 153/152) Accusative subject of `أَنَّ`; `يُظْهِرُ` (he shows, Day 115); `زِيَادَةً مَلْحُوظَةً` (noticeable increase, Day 115/165) Accusative object; `فِي رَأْسِ الْمَالِ السُّوقِيِّ` (**new for today**) Genitive).

2.  **"While some believe that increasing earnings per share will enhance shareholder confidence, others point out that an internal investigation is currently being carried out due to concerns from some stakeholders about transparency. Therefore, deadlines are needed for compliance."**
    *   `بَيْنَمَا يَرَى الْبَعْضُ أَنَّ زِيَادَةَ أَرْبَاحِ السَّهْمِ سَتُعَزِّزُ ثِقَةَ الْمُسَاهِمِينَ، يُشِيرُ آخَرُونَ إِلَى أَنَّ تَحْقِيقًا دَاخِلِيًّا يَتِمُّ حَالِيًّا بِسَبَبِ قَلَقِ بَعْضِ أَصْحَابِ الْمَصْلَحَةِ حَوْلَ الشَّفَافِيَّةِ. لِذَلِكَ، مَوَاعِيدُ نِهَائِيَّةٌ مَطْلُوبَةٌ لِلِالْتِزَامِ.`
    *   **Breakdown:**
        *   `بَيْنَمَا يَرَى الْبَعْضُ أَنَّ` (While some believe that - Day 146).
        *   `زِيَادَةَ أَرْبَاحِ السَّهْمِ سَتُعَزِّزُ ثِقَةَ الْمُسَاهِمِينَ` (increasing earnings per share will enhance shareholder confidence - `زِيَادَةَ` (increase, Day 115) Accusative subject of `أَنَّ`; `أَرْبَاحِ السَّهْمِ` (**new for today**) Genitive Idafa; `سَتُعَزِّزُ` (will enhance, Day 127); `ثِقَةَ الْمُسَاهِمِينَ` (shareholder confidence, Day 160) Accusative object).
        *   `يُشِيرُ آخَرُونَ إِلَى أَنَّ` (others point out that - Day 146).
        *   `تَحْقِيقًا دَاخِلِيًّا يَتِمُّ حَالِيًّا بِسَبَبِ قَلَقِ بَعْضِ أَصْحَابِ الْمَصْلَحَةِ حَوْلَ الشَّفَافِيَّةِ` (an internal investigation is currently being carried out due to concerns from some stakeholders about transparency - `تَحْقِيقًا دَاخِلِيًّا` (**new for today**) Accusative subject of `أَنَّ`; `يَتِمُّ حَالِيًّا` (is currently being carried out, Day 156/141); `بِسَبَبِ قَلَقِ بَعْضِ أَصْحَابِ الْمَصْلَحَةِ` (due to concern of some stakeholders - `قَلَقٌ` (concern, Day 166), `أَصْحَابِ الْمَصْلَحَةِ` (**new for today**) Genitive Idafa); `حَوْلَ الشَّفَافِيَّةِ` (about transparency, Day 153) Accusative).
        *   `لِذَلِكَ، مَوَاعِيدُ نِهَائِيَّةٌ مَطْلُوبَةٌ لِلِالْتِزَامِ` (Therefore, deadlines are needed for compliance - `لِذَلِكَ` (Day 132); `مَوَاعِيدُ نِهَائِيَّةٌ` (**new for today**) Nominative subject; `مَطْلُوبَةٌ` (needed, Day 146); `لِلِالْتِزَامِ` (for compliance, Day 160) Genitive).

3.  **"Unfortunately, the market index shows some fluctuations this quarter. Moreover, the forecasts indicate that dividends will decrease next year, compared to stable earnings per share."**
    *   `لِسُوءِ الْحَظِّ، يُسَجِّلُ مُؤَشِّرُ السُّوقِ بَعْضَ التَّقَلُّبَاتِ هَذَا الرُّبْعَ. عَلَاوَةً عَلَى ذَلِكَ، تُشِيرُ التَّوَقُّعَاتُ إِلَى أَنَّ تَوْزِيعَاتِ الْأَرْبَاحِ سَتَتَنَاقَصُ الْعَامَ الْقَادِمَ، مُقَارَنَةً بِأَرْبَاحِ سَهْمٍ مُسْتَقِرَّةٍ.`
    *   **Breakdown:**
        *   `لِسُوءِ الْحَظِّ` (Unfortunately - Day 99).
        *   `يُسَجِّلُ مُؤَشِّرُ السُّوقِ بَعْضَ التَّقَلُّبَاتِ هَذَا الرُّبْعَ` (the market index records some fluctuations this quarter - `يُسَجِّلُ` (he records, Day 165); `مُؤَشِّرُ السُّوقِ` (**new for today**) Nominative subject; `بَعْضَ التَّقَلُّبَاتِ` (some fluctuations, Day 158) Accusative object; `هَذَا الرُّبْعَ` (this quarter, Day 153)).
        *   `عَلَاوَةً عَلَى ذَلِكَ` (Moreover - Day 107).
        *   `تُشِيرُ التَّوَقُّعَاتُ إِلَى أَنَّ تَوْزِيعَاتِ الْأَرْبَاحِ سَتَتَنَاقَصُ الْعَامَ الْقَادِمَ` (forecasts indicate that dividends will decrease next year - `تُشِيرُ التَّوَقُّعَاتُ إِلَى أَنَّ` (forecasts indicate that, Day 158); `تَوْزِيعَاتِ الْأَرْبَاحِ` (**new for today**) Accusative subject of `أَنَّ`; `سَتَتَنَاقَصُ` (she will decrease, Day 158); `الْعَامَ الْقَادِمَ` (next year, Day 110)).
        *   `مُقَارَنَةً بِأَرْبَاحِ سَهْمٍ مُسْتَقِرَّةٍ` (compared to stable earnings per share - `مُقَارَنَةً بِـ` (compared to, Day 158); `أَرْبَاحِ سَهْمٍ` (**new for today**) Genitive Idafa; `مُسْتَقِرَّةٍ` (stable, feminine, Day 158) Genitive adjective).

4.  **"It is important to point out that the company's commitment to digital transformation, according to a recent study, reflects its long-term strategic plan. This is a clear indicator of its future competitiveness."**
    *   `مِنَ الْمُهِمِّ الْإِشَارَةُ إِلَى أَنَّ الْتِزَامَ الشَّرِكَةِ بِالتَّحَوُّلِ الرَّقْمِيِّ، وَفْقًا لِدِرَاسَةٍ حَدِيثَةٍ، يَعْكِسُ خُطَّتَهَا الِاسْتِرَاتِيجِيَّةَ طَوِيلَةَ الْأَمَدِ. وَهَذَا مُؤَشِّرٌ وَاضِحٌ لِتَنَافُسِيَّتِهَا الْمُسْتَقْبَلِيَّةِ.`
    *   **Breakdown:**
        *   `مِنَ الْمُهِمِّ الْإِشَارَةُ إِلَى أَنَّ` (It is important to point out that - Day 156).
        *   `الْتِزَامَ الشَّرِكَةِ بِالتَّحَوُّلِ الرَّقْمِيِّ` (the company's commitment to digital transformation - `الْتِزَامَ` (commitment, Day 160) Accusative subject of `أَنَّ`; `الشَّرِكَةِ` (Day 120) Genitive Idafa; `بِالتَّحَوُّلِ الرَّقْمِيِّ` (**new for today**) Genitive).
        *   `وَفْقًا لِدِرَاسَةٍ حَدِيثَةٍ` (according to a recent study - Day 158/156/130).
        *   `يَعْكِسُ خُطَّتَهَا الِاسْتِرَاتِيجِيَّةَ طَوِيلَةَ الْأَمَدِ` (reflects its long-term strategic plan - `يَعْكِسُ` (he reflects, Day 159); `خُطَّتَهَا الِاسْتِرَاتِيجِيَّةَ` (**new for today** + pronoun) Accusative object; `طَوِيلَةَ الْأَمَدِ` (long-term, Day 160 context `أَمَدٌ` - term) Accusative adjective phrase).
        *   `وَهَذَا مُؤَشِّرٌ وَاضِحٌ لِتَنَافُسِيَّتِهَا الْمُسْتَقْبَلِيَّةِ` (This is a clear indicator of its future competitiveness - `مُؤَشِّرٌ وَاضِحٌ` (clear indicator, Day 165/105); `لِتَنَافُسِيَّتِهَا الْمُسْتَقْبَلِيَّةِ` (for its future competitiveness - `تَنَافُسِيَّةٌ` (competitiveness, Day 160), `مُسْتَقْبَلِيَّةٌ` (future, Day 153 context `مُسْتَقْبَلٌ` - future) Genitive).

## 🗣️ Key Sentences
*   مِنَ الْمُؤَكَّدِ أَنَّ الْخُطَّةَ الِاسْتِرَاتِيجِيَّةَ لِلشَّرِكَةِ تُشَدِّدُ عَلَى التَّحَوُّلِ الرَّقْمِيِّ لِتَحْقِيقِ تَوَسُّعٍ فِي الْأَسْوَاقِ الْعَالَمِيَّةِ. وَالدَّلِيلُ عَلَى ذَلِكَ أَنَّ التَّقْرِيرَ السَّنَوِيَّ الْأَخِيرَ يُظْهِرُ زِيَادَةً مَلْحُوظَةً فِي رَأْسِ الْمَالِ السُّوقِيِّ.
*   بَيْنَمَا يَرَى الْبَعْضُ أَنَّ زِيَادَةَ أَرْبَاحِ السَّهْمِ سَتُعَزِّزُ ثِقَةَ الْمُسَاهِمِينَ، يُشِيرُ آخَرُونَ إِلَى أَنَّ تَحْقِيقًا دَاخِلِيًّا يَتِمُّ حَالِيًّا بِسَبَبِ قَلَقِ بَعْضِ أَصْحَابِ الْمَصْلَحَةِ حَوْلَ الشَّفَافِيَّةِ. لِذَلِكَ، مَوَاعِيدُ نِهَائِيَّةٌ مَطْلُوبَةٌ لِلِالْتِزَامِ.
*   لِسُوءِ الْحَظِّ، يُسَجِّلُ مُؤَشِّرُ السُّوقِ بَعْضَ التَّقَلُّبَاتِ هَذَا الرُّبْعَ. عَلَاوَةً عَلَى ذَلِكَ، تُشِيرُ التَّوَقُّعَاتُ إِلَى أَنَّ تَوْزِيعَاتِ الْأَرْبَاحِ سَتَتَنَاقَصُ الْعَامَ الْقَادِمَ، مُقَارَنَةً بِأَرْبَاحِ سَهْمٍ مُسْتَقِرَّةٍ.

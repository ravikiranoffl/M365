# Day 153 - Advanced Journalism: Corporate reporting

## Lesson

"Corporate reporting" is a specialized area of journalism that focuses on the activities, performance, and strategic decisions of companies and corporations. This includes everything from announcing quarterly financial results to discussing leadership changes, market strategies, and their impact on society and the environment.

Think of it as adding a new, powerful lens to your advanced Arabic toolkit, enabling you to articulate complex business narratives with precision and authority. You'll learn specific terms for financial results, company structure, strategic moves, and modern corporate concepts.

Here are the key vocabulary and phrases you'll learn today:

1.  **Company Structure and Leadership:**
    *   `إِدَارَةٌ` (idāratun) - 'i-daa-ra-tun' (feminine noun)
        *   *Meaning:* Management.
        *   *Explanation:* This refers to the act of managing or the collective body of managers.
    *   `مُدِيرٌ تَنْفِيذِيٌّ` (mudeerun tanfeedhiyyun) - 'mu-dee-run tan-fee-dhiy-yun' (masculine noun + adjective)
        *   *Meaning:* CEO (Chief Executive Officer) / Executive Director.
        *   *Explanation:* `مُدِيرٌ` means 'director/manager' and `تَنْفِيذِيٌّ` means 'executive'. Together, they form the title for a CEO.
    *   `رَئِيسُ مَجْلِسِ الْإِدَارَةِ` (ra'eetsu majlisi al-idārati) - 'ra-'ee-su maj-li-si al-i-daa-ra-ti' (Idafa phrase)
        *   *Meaning:* Chairman of the Board (literally: President of the Board of Management).
        *   *Explanation:* `رَئِيسٌ` (president/chairman), `مَجْلِسٌ` (council/board), `إِدَارَةٌ` (management).

2.  **Financial Performance and Reporting:**
    *   `أَرْبَاحٌ` (arbāḥun) - 'ar-baah' (feminine plural noun, broken plural)
        *   *Meaning:* Profits.
        *   *Explanation:* The plural of `رِبْحٌ` (profit). Use this to refer to a company's financial gains.
    *   `خَسَائِرُ` (khasā'iru) - 'kha-saa-'ir' (feminine plural noun, broken plural, diptote)
        *   *Meaning:* Losses.
        *   *Explanation:* The plural of `خَسَارَةٌ` (loss). Remember it's a diptote, so it won't take Tanween, and its Genitive case will be Fatha.
    *   `إِيرَادَاتٌ` (īrādātun) - 'ee-raa-daa-tun' (feminine plural noun)
        *   *Meaning:* Revenues / Income.
        *   *Explanation:* Refers to the total income a company generates from its normal business activities.
    *   `نَفَقَاتٌ` (nafaqātun) - 'na-fa-qaa-tun' (feminine plural noun)
        *   *Meaning:* Expenses / Expenditures.
        *   *Explanation:* Refers to the costs incurred by a company to operate.
    *   `سَهْمٌ` (sahmun) - 'sahm' (masculine singular noun)
        *   *Meaning:* Share / Stock.
        *   *Explanation:* A unit of ownership in a company.
    *   `أَسْهُمٌ` (ashumun) - 'a-shum' (masculine plural noun, broken plural)
        *   *Meaning:* Shares / Stocks (plural).
        *   *Explanation:* The plural of `سَهْمٌ`.
    *   `سُوقُ الْأَسْهُمِ` (sūqu al-ashumi) - 'soo-qu al-ash-um' (Idafa phrase)
        *   *Meaning:* Stock market.
        *   *Explanation:* `سُوقٌ` means 'market' (Day 103).
    *   `أَدَاءٌ مَالِيٌّ` (adā'un māliyyun) - 'a-daa-'un maa-liy-yun' (masculine noun + adjective)
        *   *Meaning:* Financial performance.
        *   *Explanation:* `أَدَاءٌ` means 'performance' and `مَالِيٌّ` means 'financial' (related to `مَالٌ` - money).
    *   `تَقْرِيرٌ سَنَوِيٌّ` (taqreerun sanawiyyun) - 'taq-ree-run sa-na-wiy-yun' (masculine noun + adjective)
        *   *Meaning:* Annual report.
        *   *Explanation:* `تَقْرِيرٌ` means 'report' (Day 115/134) and `سَنَوِيٌّ` means 'annual'.

3.  **Corporate Actions and Concepts:**
    *   `ادْمِجَاجٌ` (idmijājun) - 'id-mi-jaa-jun' (masculine noun)
        *   *Meaning:* Merger.
        *   *Explanation:* When two or more companies combine to form a new single entity.
    *   `اسْتِحْوَاذٌ` (istiḥwādhun) - 'is-tiḥ-waa-dhun' (masculine noun)
        *   *Meaning:* Acquisition.
        *   *Explanation:* When one company takes over another.
    *   `شَفَّافِيَّةٌ` (shaffāfiyyatun) - 'shaf-faa-fee-ya-tun' (feminine noun)
        *   *Meaning:* Transparency.
        *   *Explanation:* Openness and accountability in business practices.
    *   `اسْتِدَامَةٌ` (istidāmatun) - 'is-ti-daa-ma-tun' (feminine noun)
        *   *Meaning:* Sustainability.
        *   *Explanation:* The ability to maintain something at a certain rate or level (seen in `تَنْمِيَةٌ مُسْتَدَامَةٌ` - sustainable development, Day 131).
    *   `مَسْؤُولِيَّةٌ اجْتِمَاعِيَّةٌ لِلشَّرِكَاتِ` (mas'ooliyyatun ijtima'iyyatun lil-sharikaat) - 'mas-'oo-liy-ya-tun ij-ti-maa-'iy-ya-tun lil-sha-ri-kaat' (feminine noun phrase)
        *   *Meaning:* Corporate Social Responsibility (CSR).
        *   *Explanation:* The concept that companies should operate in a way that benefits society and the environment. `مَسْؤُولِيَّةٌ` (responsibility), `اجْتِمَاعِيَّةٌ` (social), `لِلشَّرِكَاتِ` (for companies).
    *   `حَوْكَمَةٌ` (ḥawkamatu) - 'ḥaw-ka-ma-tun' (feminine noun)
        *   *Meaning:* Governance.
        *   *Explanation:* The system by which organizations are directed and controlled (often seen as `حَوْكَمَةُ الشَّرِكَاتِ` - corporate governance).

4.  **Verbs for Corporate Reporting:**
    *   `يُنَاقِشُ` (yunāqishu) - 'yu-naa-qi-shu' (present tense verb, Form III)
        *   *Meaning:* He discusses.
        *   *Explanation:* Useful for reporting on discussions within management or board meetings. Feminine form: `تُنَاقِشُ` (tunāqishu).

Remember to integrate these new terms with your existing advanced grammar, statistical vocabulary (`نِسْبَةٌ مِئَوِيَّةٌ`, `مُعَدَّلٌ`, `أَرْقَامٌ`, `يَبْلُغُ`, `أَظْهَرَ`), polling language (`اسْتِطْلَاعُ الرَّأْيِ`, `الرَّأْيُ الْعَامُّ`, `يُؤَيِّدُ`, `يُعَارِضُ`), and argumentation structures (`لَا مَفَرَّ مِنْ أَنَّ`, `بَيْنَمَا يَرَى الْبَعْضُ أَنَّ`, `وَالدَّلِيلُ عَلَى ذَلِكَ أَنَّ`, etc.).

## Examples

Let's look at some examples that seamlessly integrate corporate reporting language with other learned concepts. Carefully read each English sentence, then the Arabic translation, and pay close attention to the breakdown of how different elements are combined.

1.  **"It is certain that the company's financial performance shows big profits. And the evidence for that is that the annual report revealed revenues of more than $10 million in the past year."**
    *   `مِنَ الْمُؤَكَّدِ أَنَّ الْأَدَاءَ الْمَالِيَّ لِلشَّرِكَةِ يُظْهِرُ أَرْبَاحًا كَبِيرَةً. وَالدَّلِيلُ عَلَى ذَلِكَ أَنَّ التَّقْرِيرَ السَّنَوِيَّ كَشَفَ عَنْ إِيرَادَاتٍ بَلَغَتْ أَكْثَرَ مِنْ 10 مَلَايِينَ دُولَارٍ فِي الْعَامِ الْمَاضِي.`
    *   **Breakdown:**
        *   `مِنَ الْمُؤَكَّدِ أَنَّ` (It is certain that - Day 125).
        *   `الْأَدَاءَ الْمَالِيَّ لِلشَّرِكَةِ يُظْهِرُ أَرْبَاحًا كَبِيرَةً` (the company's financial performance shows big profits - `الْأَدَاءَ الْمَالِيَّ` (**new for today**) Accusative subject of `أَنَّ`; `لِلشَّرِكَةِ` (for the company, Day 120); `يُظْهِرُ` (he shows, Day 115); `أَرْبَاحًا كَبِيرَةً` (**new for today**/Day 54) Accusative object).
        *   `وَالدَّلِيلُ عَلَى ذَلِكَ أَنَّ` (And the evidence for that is that - Day 132).
        *   `التَّقْرِيرَ السَّنَوِيَّ كَشَفَ عَنْ إِيرَادَاتٍ بَلَغَتْ أَكْثَرَ مِنْ 10 مَلَايِينَ دُولَارٍ فِي الْعَامِ الْمَاضِي` (the annual report revealed revenues of more than $10 million in the past year - `التَّقْرِيرَ السَّنَوِيَّ` (**new for today**) Accusative subject of `أَنَّ`; `كَشَفَ عَنْ` (he revealed about, Day 151); `إِيرَادَاتٍ` (**new for today**) Genitive after `عَنْ` (object of `كَشَفَ`); `بَلَغَتْ` (she reached, Day 151); `أَكْثَرَ مِنْ` (more than, Day 110); `10 مَلَايِينَ دُولَارٍ` (10 million dollars - `مَلَايِينُ` is plural of `مِلْيُونٌ` (million, Day 151), here `مَلَايِينَ` is Genitive after `مِنْ` and used as a number for dollars, `دُولَارٍ` (dollars, Day 110)); `فِي الْعَامِ الْمَاضِي` (in the past year, Day 110)).

2.  **"While some believe that the merger of the two companies will lead to economic growth, others point out that it may cause job losses. Therefore, the management needs to ensure transparency."**
    *   `بَيْنَمَا يَرَى الْبَعْضُ أَنَّ ادْمِجَاجَ الشَّرِكَتَيْنِ سَيُؤَدِّي إِلَى نُمُوٍّ اقْتِصَادِيٍّ، يُشِيرُ آخَرُونَ إِلَى أَنَّهُ قَدْ يُسَبِّبُ خَسَائِرَ فِي فُرَصِ الْعَمَلِ. لِذَلِكَ، تَحْتَاجُ الْإِدَارَةُ إِلَى ضَمَانِ الشَّفَّافِيَّةِ.`
    *   **Breakdown:**
        *   `بَيْنَمَا يَرَى الْبَعْضُ أَنَّ` (While some believe that - Day 146).
        *   `ادْمِجَاجَ الشَّرِكَتَيْنِ سَيُؤَدِّي إِلَى نُمُوٍّ اقْتِصَادِيٍّ` (the merger of the two companies will lead to economic growth - `ادْمِجَاجَ` (**new for today**) Accusative subject of `أَنَّ`; `الشَّرِكَتَيْنِ` (the two companies, dual Genitive); `سَيُؤَدِّي إِلَى` (will lead to, Day 139); `نُمُوٍّ اقْتِصَادِيٍّ` (economic growth, Day 103/115) Genitive).
        *   `يُشِيرُ آخَرُونَ إِلَى أَنَّهُ قَدْ يُسَبِّبُ خَسَائِرَ فِي فُرَصِ الْعَمَلِ` (others point out that it may cause job losses - `يُشِيرُ آخَرُونَ إِلَى أَنَّ` (Day 146); `ـهُ` (it, Day 24); `قَدْ يُسَبِّبُ` (may cause - `يُسَبِّبُ` (he causes, Form II verb, new); `خَسَائِرَ` (**new for today**) Accusative diptote object; `فِي فُرَصِ الْعَمَلِ` (in job opportunities, Day 140) Genitive).
        *   `لِذَلِكَ، تَحْتَاجُ الْإِدَارَةُ إِلَى ضَمَانِ الشَّفَافِيَّةِ` (Therefore, the management needs to ensure transparency - `لِذَلِكَ` (Day 132); `تَحْتَاجُ إِلَى` (she needs, Day 132); `الْإِدَارَةُ` (**new for today**) Nominative subject; `ضَمَانِ` (ensuring - Masdar from `ضَمِنَ` - to ensure, new); `الشَّفَّافِيَّةِ` (**new for today**) Genitive Idafa).

3.  **"It is inevitable that Corporate Social Responsibility is an essential part of modern management. Moreover, recent public opinion polls show that a majority of the population supports sustainability efforts."**
    *   `لَا مَفَرَّ مِنْ أَنَّ الْمَسْؤُولِيَّةَ الِاجْتِمَاعِيَّةَ لِلشَّرِكَاتِ جُزْءٌ أَسَاسِيٌّ مِنَ الْإِدَارَةِ الْحَدِيثَةِ. عَلَاوَةً عَلَى ذَلِكَ، تُظْهِرُ اسْتِطْلَاعَاتُ الرَّأْيِ الْأَخِيرَةُ أَنَّ أَغْلَبِيَّةً مِنَ السُّكَّانِ تُؤَيِّدُ جُهُودَ الِاسْتِدَامَةِ.`
    *   **Breakdown:**
        *   `لَا مَفَرَّ مِنْ أَنَّ` (It is inevitable that - Day 146).
        *   `الْمَسْؤُولِيَّةَ الِاجْتِمَاعِيَّةَ لِلشَّرِكَاتِ جُزْءٌ أَسَاسِيٌّ مِنَ الْإِدَارَةِ الْحَدِيثَةِ` (Corporate Social Responsibility is an essential part of modern management - `الْمَسْؤُولِيَّةَ الِاجْتِمَاعِيَّةَ لِلشَّرِكَاتِ` (**new for today**) Accusative subject of `أَنَّ`; `جُزْءٌ أَسَاسِيٌّ` (essential part, Day 139); `مِنَ الْإِدَارَةِ الْحَدِيثَةِ` (of modern management - `إِدَارَةٌ` (**new for today**), `حَدِيثَةٌ` (modern, Day 130)) Genitive).
        *   `عَلَاوَةً عَلَى ذَلِكَ` (Moreover - Day 107).
        *   `تُظْهِرُ اسْتِطْلَاعَاتُ الرَّأْيِ الْأَخِيرَةُ أَنَّ أَغْلَبِيَّةً مِنَ السُّكَّانِ تُؤَيِّدُ جُهُودَ الِاسْتِدَامَةِ` (recent public opinion polls show that a majority of the population supports sustainability efforts - `تُظْهِرُ` (she shows, Day 115); `اسْتِطْلَاعَاتُ الرَّأْيِ الْأَخِيرَةُ` (recent public opinion polls, Day 152/152/152) Nominative subject; `أَنَّ أَغْلَبِيَّةً مِنَ السُّكَّانِ` (that a majority of the population - `أَغْلَبِيَّةٌ` (majority, Day 152), `سُكَّانٌ` (population, Day 152)) Accusative subject of `أَنَّ`; `تُؤَيِّدُ` (she supports, Day 152); `جُهُودَ الِاسْتِدَامَةِ` (sustainability efforts - `جُهُودٌ` (efforts, Day 145), `اسْتِدَامَةٌ` (**new for today**)) Accusative Idafa).
